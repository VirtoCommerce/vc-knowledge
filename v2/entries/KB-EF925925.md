Yes, and nothing warns them. The cart page is rendered once from a read and never revises itself: there is no poll, no push, and expanding the discount breakdown is pure client-side, so a promotion edited in Admin leaves the rendered summary saying whatever was true when it was drawn. It is corrected by a server round trip and only by one -- a reload, an SPA navigation that refetches the cart, or ANY cart mutation, including one with no bearing on price such as choosing a shipment or payment method. That is the trap: the LAST control a shopper touches before Place order is usually a mutation, so on a normal checkout the summary is refreshed in the same click that enables Place order and the numbers look right. When the cart was already complete, Place order is the next click with no mutation between it and the last render, and the order is built from a fresh server-side evaluation with no comparison against what the page showed. Observed: cart page showing subtotal 319.00, discount -63.80, total 255.20, Place order enabled; the promotion deactivated in Admin 22 seconds later with the page untouched; Place order clicked; the order was created at total 319.00 with discounts [] -- 63.80 more than the figure the shopper clicked under, with no interstitial, no revised summary and no message. The confirmation page carries no money at all, so the first place the new total appears is the order page, which asks for it under Pay now. The order is a snapshot of the moment of PLACEMENT, not of what the shopper was shown, and keeps no trace that a discount was ever displayed. The page's small print -- Availability, shipping, tax and promotions are not final until you complete your order -- is the only notice given and is present whether or not anything moved.

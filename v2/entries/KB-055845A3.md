The storefront cart re-evaluates PROMOTIONS on every read (that is @kb(KB-35A09C64), which held here) but the line item's UNIT PRICE follows some other, slower path, and it can settle on a value that matches neither the current configuration nor the price the line was written at. Observed on one line, all times within seven minutes: line mutated to qty 5 while a high-priority assignment made the price 111.11, and the cart then showed 111.11 x5 = 555.55; the assignment was expired, and the product page showed 399.99; the assignment was made valid again, and the product page showed 111.11 once more; the cart was then loaded fresh for the first time since the mutation and served 399.99 x5 = 1999.95, and went on serving it across repeated fresh loads while the product page for the same shopper, store and SKU served 111.11 in the same minutes; one quantity change re-priced the line to 111.11. So the stale figure was neither the mutation-time price (111.11) nor the live price (111.11) but the price from a window the cart was never read during -- which rules out 'frozen at mutation' and rules out 'recomputed on read', and points at an evaluated-price cache the catalog read path and the cart read path do not invalidate together. WHAT IS ESTABLISHED, and is enough to work with: the cart's unit price can disagree with the product page for the same shopper at the same instant; reading the cart again does not fix it; a cart mutation does. WHAT IS NOT: which event refreshes it, and how long it stays wrong. Do not reason about an open cart's unit price from the current pricing configuration, and when a shopper and an operator disagree about a price, ask whether the number came off a product page or out of a cart before comparing anything.

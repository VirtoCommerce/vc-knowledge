STEP 1 - reach the module. Marketing is not on the default left rail and #!/marketing falls through to the workspace. Open More, then Marketing, then Promotions, then Add. The blade is called New promotion.

STEP 2 - the three mandatory things. Create stays disabled until the promotion has a name, at least one ELIGIBILITY CRITERION and at least one REWARD; the two missing ones are flagged in red inside Promotion conditions. Add user group offers Everyone, which is the criterion to use when the promotion is meant to apply to all shoppers. Add reward opens a long menu of reward shapes; the one that takes a percentage off the whole cart is "...% off cart subtotal, no more than $..." and it writes RewardCartGetOfRelSubtotal. Leave the no-more-than box at 0 for no cap.

STEP 3 - scope it to a store. Stores starts empty, which is NOT store-wide-safe to leave: click Add under Stores and pick the store. Empty start date means active from save; empty expiration means active until deactivated.

STEP 4 - the description is free text and it is what the SHOPPER SEES. The storefront prints the promotion description, not its name, as the label under the cart Discount line and on the order page - so a rate written there is the only human-readable rate anywhere in the money record. Write the rate into it deliberately.

STEP 5 - Create POSTs /api/marketing/promotions and the blade reloads from GET /api/marketing/promotions/{id}. Editing afterwards is Save, which PUTs to /api/marketing/promotions and returns 204.

STEP 6 - see it apply. It takes effect on the very next storefront cart read with no cart mutation; the cart Discount line carries a chevron that expands to one row per cart-level reward. A reward on a single product does NOT appear there - it shows as a struck-through line price and a synthesised "Line items" row.

STEP 7 - who used it. The promotion blade has a USAGE HISTORY widget; it opens a list titled "Orders with this promotion" and it is an order SEARCH, not a stored redemption log.

Pitfall: the promotions grid and the product picker are both ui-grid, and a row-selection click aimed at the picker can land on the promotions list behind it, arming the Delete button over somebody else promotion. Select in the picker by its own grid, and check the toolbar afterwards.

One discount is stored twice at different precision and both are served: the discount row keeps the raw unrounded product of the reward (a 10% reward on a 348.99 subtotal is stored as 34.8990 in CustomerOrder.discounts[].discountAmount and in the order-level discountAmount), while discountTotal, the order header Discount field and the GraphQL OrderDiscountType.amount are rounded to currency precision (34.90). The Admin order screen shows both at once - the Discounts grid renders the unrounded 34.899 while the header and Total discount on the same blade render 34.90 - so a reconciliation that mixes the two sources will be off by the rounding remainder.

**Anchor corrected.** `CustomerOrder.discountTotal` → `GET /api/order/customerOrders/{id}` — Neither of this entry's anchors was a coordinate the derived plane projects, so validate reported it unreachable and the coordinate hook never offered it to anyone standing on an order. I re-observed the same split on a second order and could not find this entry through kb deliver at all; I wrote a duplicate of it before consolidate showed me it was here. Moving the order-header half onto the route both values are read from puts it where the next reader of an order will meet it. Discount.discountAmount is left alone: it names the REST model field the claim is about.

Cancelling an order cascades to the PAYMENT and not to the SHIPMENT. After Cancel document on the Admin order blade, reading the order back over GET /api/order/customerOrders/{id} showed the order at status Cancelled with isCancelled true, cancelledDate set and cancelReason carrying the text typed into the dialog; its inPayment had likewise moved to status Cancelled with isCancelled true; and its shipment was still status New with isCancelled false. So a cancelled order leaves a live shipment document behind, and anything that counts open shipments -- a fulfilment queue, a warehouse worklist -- will keep seeing it. This is not a one-off: it is the mechanism, and it is why a store used for order testing accumulates New shipments belonging to orders that are all Cancelled. Cancelling is also the only option, since an order cannot be deleted, so the shipment cannot be cleared by removing its parent.

**Retired.** The cascade claim is right and was reproduced independently by all three arms of round two. Its last sentence is false: an order CAN be deleted, from REST and from the Admin UI. Correcting rather than disputing, because the entry is mostly true and the false clause is the part that would be acted on. Superseded by KB-0C102D97.

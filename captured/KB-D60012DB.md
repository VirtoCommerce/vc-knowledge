The discount row's discountAmountWithTax is always 0, whatever the store's tax configuration. Nothing on the placement path ever assigns it: RewardExtensions.ApplyRewards builds the Discount without that field, the cart and order totals calculators set WithTax only on LineItem, Shipment and PaymentIn, and CustomerOrderBuilder copies the row verbatim, so the field sits at its CLR default. Proven across stores on one deployment: on a store with no tax provider the row reads 0 while discountAmount holds the real reduction; on a store with an ACTIVE 20 percent provider the line item's own discountAmountWithTax is correctly populated (29.50 -> 35.40) and discounts[0].discountAmountWithTax is STILL 0. The order-level discountTotalWithTax is populated on both, because it is derived as DiscountAmount x (1 + TaxPercentRate) and never reads the row. Reading the row's WithTax field as the discount silently reports no discount at all. Confirmed on platform 3.1007.27, Orders 3.1000.4, XCart 3.1000.6.

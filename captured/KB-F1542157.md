Different scopes, not a disagreement. Round one's arm B read the Admin Line items blade header as Discount 0.00 and Total 1,612.97 while the order header on the same order read Discount 241.95 and Total 1,371.02. The promotion was an order-level reward and the platform does not spread it across lines: every items[].discountAmount in the payload was 0, and the whole 241.95 sits in the order's own discounts collection. So the Line items header is the untouched sum of the lines and the order header is the sum after the order-level reward, and both are right. Reading the blade header as the order total is the mistake, and it is easy to make because the two screens sit one click apart and neither says which scope it is showing.

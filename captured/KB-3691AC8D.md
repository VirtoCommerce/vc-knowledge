A cart-level percentage reward is PERSISTED on the order at full precision and rounded only by the projection that reads it. The order's discount row carries discountAmount as the raw product of rate and base - a 15% reward on a 236.27 subtotal is stored as 35.4405 - and the order's own discountAmount repeats it, while discountTotal is rounded to currency precision (35.44). The storefront GraphQL projection of the SAME order returns OrderDiscountType.amount already rounded, so an Admin or REST reader sees 35.4405 and a customer sees 35.44. That difference decides whether the rate can be reconstructed arithmetically: 35.4405/236.27 divides out to exactly 0.15, 35.44/236.27 gives 0.1500063, and no rounding of the second recovers the first as a round rate. The trailing digits are not currency, they are the only surviving arithmetic evidence of the rate - and they only survive where one reward was taken against one base. The same order rounds line-item money hard: every line carries isDiscountAmountRounded true with discountAmount 0.0000.

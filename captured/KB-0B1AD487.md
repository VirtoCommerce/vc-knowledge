Not once anything else discounts the same cart. A percentage-off-cart-subtotal reward is computed against the subtotal AFTER every item-level reward has been taken off, at full precision, and that base is published in no field. subTotal is the LIST subtotal and does not move when a line item is discounted; the item reward shows up only as the line item own discountTotal and placedPrice, never as an entry in the cart or order discounts collection, which holds cart-level rewards alone. Worked case: list subtotal 236.27, a 10 percent item reward on a 106.28 line takes 10.628, leaving 225.642, and a 25 percent cart reward on THAT is reported as 56.4105 - so 56.4105 divided by the published subTotal 236.27 yields 23.88 percent and not 25. The recovery only works when the discounts collection holds exactly one entry and no line item carries a discount; check both before dividing. With a single cart reward and clean lines the division is exact, because the stored amount keeps the unrounded product.

**Retired.** It said the division is exact because the stored amount keeps the unrounded product, without saying that only some readers are given those digits. Whether an auditor can divide at all depends on which surface they read, and that was the load-bearing half. Superseded by KB-A3CE7FBA.

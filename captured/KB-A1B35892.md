It becomes ONE ordinary line item on the base product, never one line per chosen option, and the chosen options live on that line's configurationItems. Three things follow that catch people out. (1) The line's sku is synthesised as 'Configuration-' + the base product's own SKU, and it is the SAME string for every line of that product, so sku cannot tell two differently configured lines apart -- two lines with the same productId and the same sku, differing only in their configurationItems and their price, is the normal state of a cart. (2) The money is rolled up, not itemised: listPrice = placedPrice = extendedPrice on the parent line = base product price + the sum over selected Product options of (option listPrice * the option's configured quantity). Observed: a 10.85 base plus a 13.10 option at quantity 3 plus a 149.00 option at quantity 1 gave a single line at 199.15, and the options contribute no line and no subtotal of their own. (3) The option quantity is fixed by the configuration and NOT a buyer's choice -- the storefront renders it in a disabled spinbutton -- so a section's quantity is part of the product definition, while the buyer chooses only WHICH option, one per section, radio-style, with an explicit 'None' for a section that is not required. A stored CartConfigurationItem carries productId, sectionId, name, sku, quantity, imageUrl, catalogId, categoryId, type, customText and files; the storefront's CartConfigurationItemType exposes only id, sectionId, name, type, productId, quantity, customText and files of those.

A RewardCartGetOfRelSubtotal reward lands only on the cart-level CartType.discounts collection and on CartType.discountTotal. Every LineItemType keeps discountTotal 0 and an empty discounts array, so a storefront or a test that reads only line-item discounts sees no discount at all even while the cart total is correctly reduced.

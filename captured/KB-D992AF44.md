Nothing on the order moves. The order-level discount row is a COPY taken at placement: it stores the promotion name and the promotion description as text of its own, alongside promotionId and the money. Renaming the promotion, rewriting its description, changing the reward rate and finally deactivating it left every one of those unchanged when the order was re-read from REST, from the Admin order Discounts widget and from the storefront order page. The GraphQL field named promotionName is part of that snapshot and not a live lookup, although the schema describes it as "Name of the promotion" - so a reader who assumes it resolves through promotionId will assume wrongly in the safe direction. Cancelling the order changes none of it either. The consequence for an audit is the reverse of what the wording suggests: the ORDER is the trustworthy record and the promotionId is the untrustworthy part, because the promotion it points at stays editable and will happily describe a different rate under the same id. Read the discount row, never the promotion, to say what an order was given.

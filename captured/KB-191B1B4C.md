The storefront's Product variations panel lists the MASTER PRODUCT ITSELF as one of its rows, alongside the variations, and it applies the same Show in stock filter as a category page with that filter ON by default. Two consequences that bite together. When every real variation is out of stock, the panel shows exactly one row, it looks like the variation, and it is the master -- so the line item you get carries the master's productId and sku, and the properties the row displayed were the master's own, not a variation's. And because the filter is inside the panel's own Filters sidebar rather than the page's, you can stare at a page that says it has variations and never see them. Turn Show in stock off in the panel's Filters and the out-of-stock variations appear, with their quantity controls correctly disabled. A variation remains reachable on its own product page by its own SEO slug whatever the panel shows, and that page is where you can see its real price and stock. The panel is fed by a products search, so it can only ever show what the store's search index holds.

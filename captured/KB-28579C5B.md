The storefront cart re-evaluates PROMOTIONS on every read but does NOT re-evaluate the LINE ITEM's unit price: that number is whatever pricing resolved to at the last cart MUTATION, and it survives reload, re-navigation and fresh page loads indefinitely. Observed end to end on one line: with a high-priority assignment live the line was added at 111.11; the assignment was then expired, and a fresh /cart load served 399.99 x5 while the promotion layer was still being recomputed correctly on that same read (the cart-subtotal promotion re-fired against the NEW subtotal, so read-time promotion evaluation was demonstrably running); the assignment was made valid again, the product page immediately showed 111.11, and the cart still served 399.99 across repeated fresh loads; one quantity change then re-priced the line to 111.11. So the two layers of a cart's money refresh on different triggers, and the shopper-visible unit price is the stale one. This is the price-list leg of @kb(KB-AD1FA66B), which describes the read path as recomputing 'every figure the storefront shows' -- that holds for the promotion layer and not for the unit price underneath it. Consequences: a price correction does not reach open carts, a shopper can check out at a price no longer configured anywhere, and a shopper comparing the product page with their own cart sees two different numbers for the same SKU with nothing on either page explaining why.

**Retired.** the original claim said the cart line carries the price resolved at the last cart MUTATION; my own timeline refutes that -- the last mutation resolved 111.11 and a later read with no mutation in between served 399.99. The divergence is real and reproducible but the refresh trigger is not what I wrote, so the mechanism sentence had to go rather than stand as an unverified guess Superseded by KB-055845A3.

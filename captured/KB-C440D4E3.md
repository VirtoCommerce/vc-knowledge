There is none, and looking for one wastes a round. /api/catalog/products carries 26 operations -- get by id, get by ids, get by outer id, save, patch, clone, new-variation templates, associations, configurations, price lists -- and not one of them searches. Catalog search on this platform is POST /api/catalog/listentries, which takes catalogId, keyword, objectTypes (CatalogProduct / Category), searchInChildren and searchInVariations. Two things about it decide what you get back. searchInVariations is the switch that includes or excludes variation products, and the difference between the two counts is exactly the number of variations in that subtree, which is the cheapest way to find out whether a catalog has any. And productType is not a filter worth reaching for: its values here are Physical, Digital and null, so it does not separate the kinds anyone actually wants to separate -- variations are identified by mainProductId, and configurability is not on the product at all.

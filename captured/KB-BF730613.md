Three values, not two, and the middle one is the trap. null means the account has never been locked. 0001-01-01 means it was locked and then EXPLICITLY UNLOCKED - SecurityController.UnlockUser calls SetLockoutEndDateAsync with DateTimeOffset.MinValue, so the sentinel is a date in the distant past and reads at a glance like a lockout that expired long ago, when what it actually records is a deliberate unlock and therefore that somebody locked this account at some point. 9999-12-31 means permanently locked: LockUser writes DateTime.MaxValue. Only a value in the FUTURE blocks a sign-in, so 0001-01-01 blocks nothing while telling you the account has a history. A near-future date is the ordinary failed-password lockout rather than a deliberate block, and accessFailedCount is what separates those two.

Three things appear and one disappears. Appearing: a 'Create your own configuration' button, a 'Configure the parameters' panel with one collapsible block per section, and a running price in the price block that is the base price plus the extended price of every selected option and updates as you choose. Disappearing: the ordinary Add-to-cart button -- on a configurable product the quantity stepper in the price block IS the add control, and incrementing it adds the line immediately. Per section the buyer gets a RADIO group, so exactly one choice per section, and an explicit 'None' row that is preselected on any section that is not required; a Product option row shows image, name as a link to that product, a few properties, unit price, the configured quantity in a DISABLED spinbutton and the extended price; a Text section shows one free-text box plus None; a File section shows a drag-and-drop uploader (any format, 1MB per file, 5 files max) plus None. Two traps. The whole selection is client-side state only: it is lost on reload, and nothing exists server-side until the line is added. And because the stepper is the add control, a configurable product whose sections are all optional can be put in the cart, and then on an order, with NO configuration at all -- an empty configurationItems array and the bare base price, with nothing on any surface marking it as unconfigured. A section that IS required blocks this differently: the panel shows a persistent 'Complete all required options to finalize your selection' and the price block offers no way to add at all.

**Retired.** One clause was wrong: it said the ordinary Add-to-cart button DISAPPEARS on a configurable product. There is no Add-to-cart button on this storefront for ANY product -- the quantity stepper is the add control everywhere, as flow KB-AFB2D3C5 step 2 already records. Nothing about the button is configurable-specific, and leaving that clause would have taught the next agent a difference that does not exist. Superseded by KB-0C163966.

Because the filter reads the same contact status the badge does, and that field says Approved for everyone. Round three's arm B filtered Company members by Status = Blocked and got 'There are no results found' while a permanently locked account sat in the unfiltered list wearing a green Active tick; Status = Invited returned zero the same way, with a never-registered account present. So the filter is not broken and it is not stale - it is consistent with the badge, and both are reading a CRM field that carries no authentication state. The practical consequence is worse than the badge alone: a maintainer who filters by Blocked to audit who is shut out gets an empty list and concludes nobody is, which is the opposite of the truth. Two corroborating details from the same page: the row action for the locked account reads Block user rather than Unblock user, and the never-registered one is offered Edit role / Block user / Delete rather than anything about the invitation.

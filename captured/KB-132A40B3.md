Less than it looks. Round three's arm B stopped on exactly this: for an account where every readable field says the person is fine, lastLoginDate null is consistent with never having attempted a sign-in AND with having attempted it and never got in. The field records a SUCCESS, so a failure leaves it untouched and indistinguishable from an absence of attempts. Nothing else on the account distinguishes the two either - an account issued and never given a usable password reads identically to one nobody has tried. So when somebody reports that they cannot sign in and every field says they should be able to, the answer is not visible on this deployment through the API at all, and saying so is the honest end of that investigation rather than a gap in the looking.

Nowhere. Admin has exactly two pricing views of a product and neither is the shopper's. (1) The product blade's Price WIDGET shows ONE headline number from GET /api/products/{productId}/{catalogId}/pricesWidget -- an evaluation with a catalogId and NO shopper, so every group-conditioned assignment is filtered out and store-scoped assignments never enter at all. It therefore always reproduces the ANONYMOUS price, and an operator reads it as 'the' price. (2) Clicking it opens the Prices blade, which lists every pricelist the product has a row in with its Catalog and Store columns -- the editorial set, four different numbers on the product used here, with nothing marking which one wins for whom. Observed simultaneously: the widget read 399.99 USD while the signed-in B2B shopper's product page read 111.11 for the same product in the same store, because the winning assignment was gated on a group carried by the shopper's organization. The gap is structural, not cosmetic: the widget's evaluation context cannot express a shopper, so no amount of looking at Admin will reproduce a conditioned price. To answer 'why does this customer see X', read the assignments (their scope, dates, priority and condition) against that customer's organization's groups; the Admin price widget is not evidence about any signed-in shopper. A related blind spot in the same module: the Price list assignments GRID has Name, Catalog, Price list, Currency, Priority, Activation, Expiration and Modified columns but NO Store column, so a store-scoped assignment shows a blank Catalog cell and nothing at all to say what it is scoped to -- the Store picker exists only inside the detail blade.

A live cart carries money in two places that can disagree, and the promotion change does not touch either of them. Every figure the storefront shows is recomputed at READ time from the promotions that are live at that instant -- create, edit or disable a promotion and the very next Query.cart reflects it with no cart mutation in between. The STORED cart is different: GET /api/carts/{id} returns whatever the last cart MUTATION wrote, and nothing else refreshes it. A read never writes back -- observed by reading the storefront cart repeatedly while GET /api/carts/{id} kept returning subTotal 0 / discounts [] on a cart that had never been mutated since its line was added. A mutation writes back everything: after one unrelated mutation (choosing a shipment method, which changes no price) the stored cart carried subTotal, discountTotal, total and a full discount row with promotionId, name and description -- the same snapshot shape an order gets. So after marketing edits the promotion the two surfaces disagree indefinitely: the shopper's reads show the new answer while GET /api/carts/{id} keeps serving the reward computed under the OLD rule, promotionId and all, until the shopper next touches the cart. Anything server-side that reads the stored cart -- an abandoned-cart mailer, a CSR looking at it, a report -- is reading the older of two true answers. The direction of the error is whichever way the promotion moved.

**Retired.** Right, but it left a neighbour teaching something false. KB-35A09C64, served beside it on query.cart and carttype.discounts, says promotions are 'not frozen into the stored cart' -- true of the storefront read path and false of GET /api/carts/{id}, which is exactly the surface somebody builds an abandoned-cart mailer on. The replacement names that clause and says which half of it survives, and adds the second half of the measurement (what the stale stored row does next). Superseded by KB-AD1FA66B.

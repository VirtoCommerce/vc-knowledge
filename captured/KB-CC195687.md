An order line item for a product variation records the variation and nothing about its parent. The stored record carries productId, sku, name, catalogId and categoryId, all of them the VARIATION's own, and there is no mainProductId, titularItemId or any other parent field on OrderLineItemType -- the parent link simply is not part of what an order stores. The only way back is OrderLineItemType.product, which is not stored data: it is resolved live against the catalog at query time, and product.masterVariation is what names the parent. Two consequences. A REST reader of GET /api/order/customerOrders/{id} cannot tell a variation from a standalone product at all -- productType says Physical for both. And because the link is a live resolve, an order that was placed against a variation loses the parent entirely if that product is later deleted or restructured, while the order's own money and sku stay correct. Verified on a placed order: line 53MF88 resolved product.masterVariation.id to the parent, and the same order's REST record for that line had no parent field of any kind.

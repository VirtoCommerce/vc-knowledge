With page A published (store B2B-store, en-US, everyone, no dates) on permalink P, POST /api/page-builder-pages/grouped with body {status:"Published", pages:[{status:"Published",...}], permalink:P, same targeting} returned 200; the new group is Published, permalink-usages reports A as Certain, admin search shows both Published, and xAPI pageDocuments returns both documents for P. slugInfo then resolves deterministically to the newer page. PUT /api/page-builder-pages/grouped with status:"Published" on a Draft group did NOT publish (status stays Draft). The conflict check lives only in POST grouped/publishing/{groupId}.

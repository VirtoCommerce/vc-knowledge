On /account/points-history, Redeemed rows show the points amount unsigned, in the same plain text and colour as Earned rows: no minus sign and no colour distinction. Direction is conveyed only by the Type column (Redeemed vs Earned). There is no per-row running balance either, only a single overall Balance figure at the top of the page. This is BY DESIGN (confirmed by the product team), so an expectation of signed or coloured redeemed amounts is a wrong oracle, not a product defect.

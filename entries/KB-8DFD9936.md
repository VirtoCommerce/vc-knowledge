The order with a subscribed line was placed (Credit card (test)) and the payment page was left. My subscriptions → Active (1) listed the line with the badge "Starting". The detail page said "Your subscription starts as soon as the first payment is confirmed.", with Next delivery "No upcoming deliveries.", an empty Pay with field, Change subscription editable and Cancel available. An expired-card decline (0069) left it Starting, and the declined card was not saved. After paying with 3220 and approving the simulated 3-D Secure check (an earlier Decline of the check showed "The payment wasn't confirmed. You can try again."), the subscription became active: next delivery one month out, Pay with Visa •••• 3220, card saved as default. On the decline, authorizePayment carried storeForFutureUse=true and the consentId from acceptSubscriptionTerms. A later one-time order with a new card and "Save this card" unticked sent storeForFutureUse=false and consentId="".

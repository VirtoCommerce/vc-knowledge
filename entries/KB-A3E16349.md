On vcptcore_qa1, where B2B-store and New-super list each other in trustedGroups (Admin Links: "customers from these stores can use their credentials to sign in"), POST /connect/token grant_type=password with storeId=B2B-store and the correct password: a contact whose account belongs to New-super returned 200 with a token; a contact whose account belongs to a store that exists on no env (AGENT-TEST-OTP-FOREIGN-STORE) and a contact whose security account has no storeId both returned 400 user_cannot_login_in_store. This closes the password-grant half of the trusted-group question at the API; the OTP-grant half is exercised by suite 105.

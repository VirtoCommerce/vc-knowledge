2026-11-28, vcptcore (Catalog 3.1055.0-alpha.2598), with requests pinned to one backend instance by the ingress affinity cookie. Steps: categories C>C2 (and C3), products linked into C, C2 and C3 and one owned by C2. GET each product with respGroup=Full (warms the cache), then POST /api/catalog/listentries/move re-parents C under another category of the same catalog (204). Result: GET /api/catalog/categories/{C2} shows the new path at once, but GET /api/catalog/products/{id}?respGroup=Full keeps the old outline for every linked product, still 5 s later (and 20 s in earlier runs), 7/7 runs. The same product with respGroup=ItemInfo,Links,Outlines (a different cache key) shows the new path in the same second. POST /api/platform-cache/reset (204, permission cache:reset) makes the Full read correct. The owned product was stale right after the move and correct 5 s later. No cross-catalog history is needed. Without session affinity, results flip between instances. Reproduced on vcptcore_regression (Catalog 3.1048.4), where directly linked products are re-saved by the move (the VCST-6376 bug) and so look fresh. Source (vc-module-catalog dev and 3.1048.4): outlines are built in ItemService.ProcessModels and cached per id+responseGroup. ItemService.ConfigureCache adds no category token, and CategoryService.ClearCacheAsync expires only CatalogTreeCacheRegion. Do not use the Full product read as the oracle for outlines after a move. Use a cold respGroup, or reset the cache first.

Signed-in B2B buyer, B2B-store, cart with one one-time line. The /cart payment dropdown listed 6 methods incl. 'Credit card (test)' (MockStoredCardPaymentMethod). Choosing it sent Mutation.addOrUpdateCartPayment; after a reload the dropdown still showed it and Query.cart payments[].paymentGatewayCode = MockStoredCardPaymentMethod. No card-number/expiry/security-code fields render on /cart for this method (the Stripe method does render them there). One Place order click sent exactly one Mutation.createOrderFromCart (no errors, order status New) and navigated to /checkout/payment, which offers saved cards as radios (default preselected) plus 'Use a new card' and a 'Pay $<total>' button. Pay sent Mutation.authorizePayment (isSuccess true) and landed on /checkout/payment/success: 'Payment successful' / 'Order CO<number> has been successfully submitted'. The cart is empty afterwards; the platform order has payment Authorized.

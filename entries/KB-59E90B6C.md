Theme 2.59 storefront, signed-in buyer and anonymous xAPI alike. Product with USD prices in two price lists: index doc price_usd=[240] (list A) and price_usd_<listB>=100; the shopper is shown sale 100. Range 150-500 returns it (card shows $100), [90 TO 110] does not. Second product: index price_usd=[9.99], shown 'From $8.30' (other list); [8 TO 9] excludes it, [9 TO 10] includes it. Single-price-list products filter correctly. vc-module-catalog FiltersHelper.CreatePriceRangeFilter uses per-price-list fields only when pricelists are passed in the criteria, otherwise the generic price_<cur> field — the generic field is what is matched here.

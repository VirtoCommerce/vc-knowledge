Signed in as a sales rep (store B2B-store), I opened /company/customer-sales (Current year by default) under each locale prefix the store offers and read the footnote below the charts. Each one had exactly one period after the date: en "Jan 2026 – Nov 2026, current month to Nov 12. Sales are net, …"; de "… laufender Monat bis 12. Nov. Umsatz netto …" (same text on 3 fresh loads); fr "… jusqu'au 12 nov. Les ventes …"; ru "… текущий месяц по 12 нояб. Продажи …"; pt "… mês atual até 12/11. As vendas …"; es, it and pl were the same. With a closed quarter (?period=q3/q2) the footnote reads "<from> – <to>. Sales are net …" and also has one period: de "Juli 2026 – Sept. 2026. Umsatz …", ru "июль 2026 г. – сент. 2026 г. Продажи …", en Q2 "Apr 2026 – Jun 2026. Sales …". Earlier builds (SalesRep 3.1017.0-pr-28-9f00) rendered ".." where the localized date already ended in a period; VCST-6793 fixed it. The store has no no, sv or fi language, so those could not be observed.

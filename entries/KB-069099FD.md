QA Storybook, Edge (Chromium), Storybook manager in full-screen mode so the preview width equals the window width. I typed values into the value Control and measured the text ink (a Range over .vc-stat-widget__value) against the card rect. The Colors grid is grid-cols-1, xs(480):grid-cols-2, lg(1024):grid-cols-4, gap 16, and Tailwind tracks are minmax(0,1fr), so a card does not widen to its figure. Results:
- 1024px, 236px cards: "$123,456,789.00" (229.1px, 30px bold) ends 13.1px past the right border on all 8 cards, inside the 16px gap. Document scrollWidth equals 1024 (rightmost ink at 1021).
- DeltaColors at 1024 renders the same.
- 1024px with "$12,345,678.90" (211.7px): 4.3px inside the border.
- 1280 and 768: fits.
- 480px (2 columns, 216px cards) with "$12,345,678.90": 15.7px past the border, ending 0.3px before the next card.
- Basic (max-w-64, 256px card) at 375 with "$123,456,789.00": 6.9px inside the border.
The rule is that the figure crosses the border when its width exceeds card width minus 20px. A 9-digit figure therefore overflows at 1024–1075, and an 8-digit one at 480–511.

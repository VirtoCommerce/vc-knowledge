On theme 2.63.0-alpha.2681 a sales rep shared a 2-item list with scope Customer to one organization (changeWishlist response sharingSetting.access = Write). A member of that organization (Purchasing agent) opened /shared-list/<key>: heading, 'Recommended by your sales representative' banner (renders a few seconds after the heading), per-row quantity + 'Add to cart' / 'Update cart', in-stock counts and an Order summary Subtotal - but no remove-item control on any row and no list actions. The same user opening /account/lists/<listId> is redirected to /403 'Access denied'. So in the storefront the recipient cannot remove items despite access=Write in the owner's sharing payload; the list is not on the recipient's /account/lists either.

Signed in, CyberSource selected on /cart. Card number empty or Luhn-invalid (1234567890123456): the microform iframe gets a red border and aria-invalid, but no error text is rendered. CVV empty: red border only, no text. CVV of 2 digits: no feedback at all (no border, no aria-invalid). In every case Place order is disabled, so nothing is submitted. The parent-page fields do show text: empty cardholder name gives 'This field is required'; expiry 01/2020 gives 'Expiration date must be in the future'; month 13/00/99 gives 'Provide a valid expiration month. Expiration date must be in the future'. vc-frontend payment-processing-cyber-source.vue gives the two iframe fields only a VcLabel and no message slot, while the cardholder and expiry VcInputs bind :message. CVV over 3 digits is truncated to 3 for a Visa, and letters are filtered out.

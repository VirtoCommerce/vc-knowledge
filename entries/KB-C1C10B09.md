2026-11-18 on B2B-store, product P tagged with exactly one group G (personalization tagged item tags=[G], EventBasedIndexation=true, TagsInheritancePolicy=DownTree), P with inStockQuantity 0 and status Disabled in all 5 of its inventory records (none in the store's main fulfillment center). As a customer whose contact Groups=[G] and no organization: Query.products(query=<P sku>, filter='') -> totalCount 1 (availabilityData isInStock=false, isAvailable=false); with 'inStock:true' or the storefront's filter '... inStock_variations:true' -> 0; dropping only the inStock_variations term from the storefront filter -> 1. Anonymous: 0 with or without the stock term, and Query.product(id) -> null. So personalization grants visibility correctly; the storefront /search 0 for the group member is the default Show in stock filter, and the PDP shows price plus a Stock alert (back-in-stock) button instead of quantity/add-to-cart because P is out of stock. A positive personalization check must use an in-stock tagged product, or turn Show in stock off.

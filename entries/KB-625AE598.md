On vcst_qa B2B-store (virtual catalog), Color facet with PriorityAscending on /catalog rendered Black, Blue, Emerald green, Green/dark-forest first (ties A-Z), i.e. Black treated as priority 1. Every catalog-level dictionary property named exactly "Color" on the physical catalogs gives Black priority 4 (and the virtual store catalog's own Color dictionary, which the sort ignores, gives 2). The only dictionary items giving Black priority 1 are on a category-level Variation property named lowercase "color" in another physical catalog (items White=0, Black=1, Red=2, Blue=3, ...) and on a property named "Color_pallete". So the priority lookup reached beyond exact-name "Color" catalog-level properties; the lowercase "color" property (case-insensitive name match, category-level) is the likely source. To predict a priority order, collect the smallest non-zero SortOrder over every physical-catalog dictionary property whose name matches the facet case-insensitively, including category-level ones. Not isolated by removing one of the two candidates.

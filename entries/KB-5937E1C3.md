Read in vc-frontend dev @3f3457e68, client-app/shared/company/utils/index.ts: getContactRoles(contact) builds [...contact.rolesInOrganization, ...contact.securityAccounts.flatMap(sa => sa.roles)], drops roles without an id, and de-duplicates by id (lodash uniqBy). convertToExtendedContact stores that list as extended.roles, and client-app/pages/company/members.vue renders it joined with ", " in the desktop Roles cell (max-w-xs truncate, title shows the full list) and in the mobile card. So a member can show several roles at once: any global (security-account) role is added to the organization role, and nothing in the frontend limits the list to the organization being viewed. A long list is cut at 320 px on desktop.

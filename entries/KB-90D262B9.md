Measured with getBoundingClientRect at a 375px viewport. Classic design (the storefront's design on this stand; also the Storybook classic story, identical to the dev base build): every page number button is 32×32 px, adjacent page buttons touch (0 px gap; .vc-pagination__pages column-gap computes to normal), and the page row sits 6 px from the Previous/Next buttons (38 px tall, 120×38 / 104×38, compact 38×38) when the pager wraps. Soft design (measured in Storybook on the same build; the stand's storefront bundles classic): the same 32×32 page buttons become round pills with a 1 px ring and a 2 px gap; page row to Previous/Next stays 6 px. Every target clears WCAG 2.5.8's 24 px minimum; the gaps are below BL-UI-006's ≥ 8 px spacing clause in both designs, and the 0 px classic gap predates the soft design.

The PDP fetches Query.product(id).associations(first: 30) with NO group argument, and only when product.associations.totalCount > 0 (vc-frontend pages/product.vue fetchRelatedProducts / searchRelatedProducts.graphql). Seeded live: an AGENT-TEST product given 10 associations via POST /api/catalog/products/associations (type "Accessories", one of Catalog.AssociationGroups' allowed values; associatedObjectType product) reported associations.totalCount=10 on xAPI after a Product reindex, and associations(first:30) returned all 10 products. The separate "Recommended / Customers bought together" widget is XRecommend's related-products / bought-together model, a semantic index search that associations do not feed. Before seeding, no product on the stand had any association.

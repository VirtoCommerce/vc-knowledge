Buyer with 12 Approved organization memberships (above the 10-org search threshold), one membership locked permanently via REST (isCurrentlyLocked=true). Desktop 1280x900, /account/dashboard, header Organizations control opened: a search combobox renders and the locked organization's row is a native <button disabled role=option> titled 'This organization is locked. Contact your administrator for access.'. Typing the locked org's name filters the list to the current organization (pinned first) plus the locked row, still disabled. A real click on it is refused (element not enabled); ArrowDown skips it. Clearing the search shows the same disabled row; clicking it is likewise refused. In both cases no /connect/token request is issued, the active organization is unchanged, no /403 and no console error. The guard therefore does not depend on whether the list is filtered, at least at the UI level.

Ten full-page loads as a signed-in B2B buyer across two fresh Chromium contexts, timings read from the HAR. The quotes module's en locale is a separate lazy chunk that the theme's module init starts loading without awaiting it (the quotes module init calls loadModuleLocale with void), and the list page builds its column titles and page head with t() once in setup into non-reactive values. Every load where the page chunk (list or detail) came from browser cache in about 1 ms while the locale chunk went to the network for a 304 revalidation (20-50 ms) rendered raw keys: quotes.quote_number_label / date_label / status_label / total_label and title '... & quotes.meta.title' on the list, '... & quote_details.title' on the detail (7 of 7). Every load where the page chunk itself came from the network (first visit in a context, post-login redirect) rendered translated text (3 of 3). Template text such as the H1 stays translated in both cases because it re-renders when messages merge. In-app navigation never shows it. The locale chunk revalidates on every load because the CDN served it with an Age larger than its max-age=14400 and must-revalidate, so how often it shows varies with CDN asset age. Even when translated, the quote detail and edit tab title reads 'Quote request #' with no number, from the same setup-time evaluation.

On vcptcore_qa1 (theme 2.59.0-pr-2477-dcf7, 2026-10-01) a buyer with two organizations signed in by password (active org A), switched to org B in the account menu, signed out, then signed in by emailed code. The POST /connect/token grant_type=otp_email body carried organization_id=<B> and the header showed org B active, matching the password path (BL-AUTH-015 step 1). The org choice is persisted, so the contact stays on B for later sign-ins until switched back.

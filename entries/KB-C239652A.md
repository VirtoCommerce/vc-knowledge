With store setting Punchout.Enabled=true and an active Punchout user mapping (POST /api/punchout-user-mappings) whose externalId equals Sender/Credential/Identity, a cXML 1.2.041 PunchOutSetupRequest operation=create with the configured SharedSecret returns HTTP 200 with cXML Status 200 OK and PunchOutSetupResponse/StartPage/URL = <store url>/punchout/<43-char token>. Opening that URL in the storefront redirects to the home page signed in as the mapped contact, under the contact's organization, with a 'Punchout mode' label next to the account menu in the top header. Observed on VirtoCommerce.Punchout 3.1000.0-pr-1 (pre-release).

2026-10-15, admin token, B2B-store, Datatrans sandbox (Sandbox setting unset, so its default true applies). POST /api/order/payments/payment/refund with orderId, paymentId, outerId and transactionId equal to the payment's outerId, amount equal to the full payment sum, reasonCode Other and a reasonMessage returned 200 {refundStatus: Pending, succeeded: true, errorMessage: null}. GET /api/order/customerOrders/{id} then showed a new refund document (RE-number) with status Pending, amount equal to the sum, and the reason message, while the PaymentIn stayed status Paid, paymentStatus Paid, isCancelled false. A re-read minutes later showed the same. In source (vc-module-datatrans DatatransPaymentMethod.RefundProcessPaymentAsync) the result sets IsSuccess and NewPaymentStatus from the Datatrans transaction status but never sets NewRefundStatus, which would explain the Pending refund document. So after a successful Datatrans refund the platform shows neither a Processed refund nor a Refunded payment. Confirm a refund in the Datatrans merchant back office, not from platform state.

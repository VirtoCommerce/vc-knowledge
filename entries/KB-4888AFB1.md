Stored a USD list price of 12.346 (EUR 10.995) for a new product via PUT /api/products/prices in a store-assigned price list; store B2B-store, USD currency decimalDigits=2, roundingType=Rounding001, midpointRounding=AwayFromZero, store setting Stores.EnablePriceRoundingForTotalsCalculation=true. xAPI Query.product returns price.list 12.35 USD and 11 EUR. addItem (qty 1, USD) as a B2B buyer: listPrice 12.35, salePrice 12.35, placedPrice 12.35, extendedPrice 12.35. With an active 10% RewardItemGetOfRel promotion scoped to that product only: listPrice 12.35, placedPrice 11.12 (11.115 rounded away from zero) but extendedPrice 11.11 (list minus a discount rounded to 1.24), so placed x qty != extended for qty 1. Consequence: with a 2-decimal currency neither a 3-decimal list price nor a percent promotion yields a 3-decimal placedPrice; a case needing one is untestable unless the currency itself carries more decimal digits.

On vcst_qa (2026-10-07, B2B-store, signed-in B2B buyer) typing a quantity on /cart sends ChangeFullCartItemsQuantity (Mutation.changeCartItemsQuantity). The server COMMITS the out-of-range quantity (line quantity becomes the typed value) and answers HTTP 200 with an empty errors array. For a product with minQuantity 7 / maxQuantity 21 / packSize 1, quantity 1 gives the line validationError PRODUCT_MIN_MAX_QTY ("You can order from 7 to 21 items", objectType LineItem) plus a cart-level PRODUCT_MIN_QTY with objectType CartProduct ("Product quantity 1 is less than minimum 7"). For a product with minQuantity 6 / packSize 6, quantity 7 gives PRODUCT_PACK_SIZE_LIMIT on the line with errorMessage " Order in packs of 6" (leading space in the server text) and errorParameters qty=7, packSize=6; quantity 1 on that product also yields only PRODUCT_PACK_SIZE_LIMIT (the pack-size check wins over the minimum). A multiple of the pack size (12) clears the error. While such an error is on the cart, Place order on /cart is disabled.

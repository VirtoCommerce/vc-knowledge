Source read (vc-module-order CustomerOrderStatisticService.CalculateRevenue): revenue per currency = sum of InPayments.Sum where the payment CreatedDate is in the window and the payment is not cancelled; no order-status filter. OrderCount = count of non-cancelled orders by order CreatedDate. On vcptcore_qa1 the Month widget showed revenue 14402.76 while the sum of all order totals in the window was 17596.94 - the difference is orders without (or with zero) payments, which looked like 'status New excluded' but is the payment basis. A REST-created order without payments adds 0 to revenue, so revenue cannot prove an order of the window's last days was counted; orderCount can.

A Push Message saved with Audience = Match by conditions (Match ALL, one row "Customer type is Employee") plus one picked person reads the caption "Customers where Customer type is Employee, plus John Doe." with headline "2 recipients" (members matched 3, people in scope 2). After Save, close, and reopen from the message list, the details blade comes back with the Match by conditions radio checked, Match=ALL, the condition row intact, the person chip intact ("Person | John Doe | 1 recipient"), the SAME caption and the same headline - it does not collapse to a picked-recipients-only caption. Separately, a message saved in Everyone mode reopens with the Everyone radio checked and the caption "Everyone - all registered customers", not as a raw member query. Observed on VirtoCommerce.PushMessages 3.1006.0-pr-28-21fe with vc-shell 2.6.1.

On both wizard steps (select items, and the details step at /account/returns/:id/edit) the quantity field corrects a value typed past the returnable maximum to that maximum on the spot, and that is the value saved: on theme 2.60.0-pr-2511-81f1 (vcst, 2026-10-13, buyer, a line with 25 returnable) typing End then '0' after 25, or filling 250, shows '25' while focused and after blur, the footer counts 25 items, CreateReturn/UpdateReturn carry quantity 25, and a reload shows '25 of 25 available'. Submit needs a reason on every line ('1 line(s) still need a reason'). Source: return-quantity-input.vue clamp + 'corrects the field on the spot', added by vc-frontend 62d91663 (VCST-5883, #2500, merged 2026-09-30) with a test 'shows the maximum rather than the number typed past it'. Before that fix, on the pre-merge theme 2.59 pr-2500 build (2026-09-29), the field kept DISPLAYING the typed value ('50 of 5 available') after blur while autosave and Continue stored the clamped 5.

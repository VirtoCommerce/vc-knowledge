On vcst_qa with the hub delivered by the SalesRep plugin (theme 2.60.0-alpha.2579): a Purchasing agent and an Organization maintainer of one organization both see Corporate > "Sales reps" (exactly one link, desktop and the 375px menu) leading to /company/sales-reps with heading "Sales reps", a "Search by name, email or phone" box and a table Name / Email / Phone listing that organization's reps (Phone empty when unset). A multi-organization user who switched the active organization from the account menu (Organizations listbox) saw the table change to the newly active organization's reps without leaving the page; a rep whose per-organization membership is locked in the first organization was absent there and present for the second organization it serves unlocked. After switching to German the same single link is labelled "Vertriebsmitarbeiter". document.title stays "Virto Commerce" on this page.

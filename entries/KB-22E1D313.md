For a return submitted by the buyer and then declined by an agent (POST /api/return/{id}/authorize with approvedQuantity 0), /account/notifications shows exactly one 'Return {number} received' and exactly one 'Return {number} declined' message within ~45 s, newest first, and the header notification bell dropdown lists the declined message at the top. A partial approval produces 'Return {number} partly approved' and a buyer cancellation 'Return {number} cancelled'. Observed on vcptcore_qa1 2026-10-01.

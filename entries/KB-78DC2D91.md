Theme 2.60.0-pr-2523-4263, 2026-10-12, one browser tab, no reload. A holder of xapi:my_organization:return:view first opened My returns and the organization tab, then went through history to their order, created a return and submitted it, and followed the breadcrumb Returns link: My returns showed it as Requested at the top, and the organization tab listed it under the holder's name. Opening it from the organization tab showed a Cancel return button (own return stays actionable); after cancelling and pressing browser Back, the organization tab showed it as Cancelled and so did My returns. No console warnings were logged.

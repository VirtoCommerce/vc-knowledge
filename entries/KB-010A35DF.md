Guest, Chromium 390x844. Two variation parents (hasVariations=true, e.g. smartphones under Phones & Tablets > Smartphones) added from search results: each compare column header shows the product link and an 'N variations' link at the button position, with no 'Add to cart' button. Two plain in-stock products in one category show an 'Add to cart' button at the same position. Tabs: products under Snacks > Sauces and Snacks > Nachos landed in separate tabs 'Sauces 1' / 'Nachos 1'; smartphones under Phones & Tablets > Smartphones shared the tab 'Phones & Tablets'; products placed directly in the root category 'Options: hats' shared the tab 'Options: hats'. Consequence for test data: a compare case that needs 'Add to cart' in each column must use plain (no variations) products in one second-level category.

On vcst (theme 2.60.0-alpha.2579), clicking 'Add cart items to quote' on /cart created a Draft quote prefilled with every cart line (including an auto-added gift line at $0.00) and opened /account/quotes/{id}/edit; after Submit the quote was Processing and /cart showed 'Your cart is empty'. Observed on two separate quotes. vc-module-quote CreateQuoteFromCartCommandHandler converts the cart to a quote and then calls cartAggregate.ClearAsync() ('// Clear cart'), so the emptied cart is by design, not a defect. Also: after uploading a file on the draft, Submit is disabled until 'Save changes' is clicked.

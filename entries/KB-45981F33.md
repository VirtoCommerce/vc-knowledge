Publishing a page-builder page with changes replaces its published page id (b39154c1-... -> 04cc19c7..., later ids without dashes). (1) SPA path: a signed-in buyer had /agent-test-... open (rendered), the page was edited+republished via REST, the buyer clicked a footer link (/catalog) and pressed Back. The storefront sent NO new slugInfo request (served from client cache) and sent getPageDocument(id: <old id>) -> {"data":{"pageDocument":null}}, then rendered "404 Page not found" (document title "... | 404 Page not found"), reproduced 2 of 2; it stays 404 until a full reload, which renders the new content. No console errors. (2) Full-reload path: a reload issued during the publish call rendered 404 as well (slugInfo returned the old id, pageDocument(old id) null). A REST probe with 6 concurrent pollers (slugInfo then pageDocument as an org buyer) saw 2 of 74 samples in that state, all within ~0.5 s around the publish call. A new tab opened after publish showed the new content.

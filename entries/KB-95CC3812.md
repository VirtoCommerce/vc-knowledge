In the News admin app two articles were created in one store, each with an en-US SEO record isActive=true and the same semanticUrl; the second save (PUT /api/news) returned 200, GET /api/news/{id} returned the duplicate semanticUrl, and the blade showed no inline or toast warning. With both articles published, xAPI newsArticles listed both with that semanticUrl, while slugInfo(permalink, storeId, cultureName) returned the first-created article on 5 of 5 requests, so the second is unreachable by its own slug. GET /api/seoinfos/duplicates?objectId={second}&objectType=NewsArticle returned []. The News admin slug field validates only 'required'; the platform SEO detail blade's duplicate check compares only records of the same object. The docs do not say a duplicate-slug save is refused (resolution picks one best match at request time).

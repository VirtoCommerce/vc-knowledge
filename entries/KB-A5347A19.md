Theme 2.63.0-alpha.2681, store B2B-store. Anonymous /checkout redirects to /cart (checkout runs on /cart with Shipping details and Payment details sections). A signed-in buyer added one variation line ($9.00) and then lost the session (isolated browser context closed, so cookies and local storage were gone): reopening /cart showed the anonymous 'Your cart is empty' state with a Sign in link, with no redirect to /sign-in. After a password sign-in, /cart again showed the buyer's line with quantity 1 and the checkout sections.

Guest, Chromium, every POST /graphql carrying themeSnapshot aborted (route abort, net::ERR_FAILED) on a cold load of /demo-landing: two ThemeSnapshot attempts (inline {domain, permalink}, then refetch {storeId:"B2B-store", cultureName:"en-US"}), both failed; the page rendered at ~1.6 s with H1, header, logo and footer (no blank page, no page error; only the failed-resource console line). Navigating in-site to /contacts afterwards rendered normally and sent no further ThemeSnapshot, so a session whose boot fetch failed keeps the built-in templates until a full reload. Whether the theme would take over could not be shown visually on B2B-store: publication 16 renders like the built-in templates.

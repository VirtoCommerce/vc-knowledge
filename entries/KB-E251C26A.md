A Sales Representative serving two organizations was signed in on the storefront; the header showed organization Y as the current organization (the session token's organization claim was Y). The admin sent PUT /api/sales-rep without Y in organizations[]. Reloading /company/tasks in that session redirected to /403 with the heading "403", "Access denied" and "You do not have permissions to access the requested page"; the console logged "useOrganizationAddresses.fetchAddresses ApolloError: Access denied."; the pageContext response still returned the rep with permission sales-rep:access, contact.organizationId = Y and contact.organizations totalCount 1; the header still showed Y and the account menu offered only the profile link and Logout (no organization switch, one organization left). After Logout and a new sign-in, the header showed the remaining organization and /company/tasks rendered normally.

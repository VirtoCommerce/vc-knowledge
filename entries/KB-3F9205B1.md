Storefront 2.59.0-pr-2536 (local build against the vcst_qa backend), sales rep, Filters drawer, Created date = Custom date. At 375 and 639 px the control is one field (295x44 at 375) labelled 'Date range' inside group 'Date range'. It holds two textbox inputs named 'Start date' and 'End date' (MM/DD/YYYY, no aria-haspopup) and one button named 'Open calendar' (38x38, aria-haspopup=dialog). getBoundingClientRect gives each input 114x19 px, or 94x19 px once it holds a value. That is under 24 px high, so a touch-target audit that includes inputs flags both as below WCAG 2.5.8 AA. At 640 px it switches to two split fields: comboboxes 138x36 with aria-haspopup=dialog, and buttons 'Open calendar: Start date' and 'Open calendar: End date'. Typed and committed values carry over when the width crosses 640 px in either direction.

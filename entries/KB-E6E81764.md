On /bulk-order ('Bulk order pad') the page offers two modes: 'Copy&Paste' (a textarea taking 'SKU,Quantity' lines) and 'Manually' (SKU + quantity rows, 5 at a time, 'Add 5 more rows' extends the pad). Entering a SKU and pressing Enter shows no product name, image or price in the row, and there is no running total. Typing a partial SKU ('XKC') shows no suggestion list. There is no file upload or drag-and-drop control. 'Add to cart' (that label, not 'Add All to Cart') redirects to /cart, which held every entered line with its quantity (3 SKUs at qty 2/5/1 observed).

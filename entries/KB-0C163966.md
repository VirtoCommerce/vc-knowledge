Two things appear, and one existing control changes meaning. Appearing: a 'Create your own configuration' button, and a 'Configure the parameters' panel with one collapsible block per section. Changing: the price in the price block becomes a running total -- base price plus the extended price of every selected option, updating as you choose (10.85 base + a 13.10 option at quantity 3 + a 149.00 option at quantity 1 displayed as 199.15). The add control itself does NOT change: this storefront has no Add-to-cart button for any product, configurable or not, and the quantity stepper in the price block is what adds the line. Per section the buyer gets a RADIO group, so exactly one choice per section, and an explicit 'None' row preselected on any section that is not required; a Product option row shows image, name as a link to that product, a few properties, unit price, the configured quantity in a DISABLED spinbutton and the extended price; a Text section shows one free-text box plus None; a File section shows a drag-and-drop uploader (any format, 1MB per file, 5 files max) plus None. Two traps. The whole selection is client-side state only: it is lost on reload, and nothing exists server-side until the line is added. And because the stepper is the add control and it does not consult the configurator, a configurable product whose sections are ALL OPTIONAL can be put in the cart, and then on an order, with no configuration at all -- an empty configurationItems array and the bare base price, with nothing on any surface marking it as unconfigured. A section that IS required blocks this differently: the panel shows a persistent 'Complete all required options to finalize your selection' and the price block offers no way to add at all.

Seo 3.1004.2, Store 3.1005.1, Platform 3.1039.13, 2026-10-13. Temp store with seoInfos []: Stores > store > SEO widget > Add (en-US, slug) > OK > tick the row checkbox: Delete becomes enabled; Delete > Yes removes the row. 3/3 runs (two of them after a full page reload). Also enabled after deleting the last row and re-adding in the same open blade; after store Save + closing and reopening the SEO blade, delete + Save persisted (GET /api/stores/{id} seoInfos []). Same result on a category whose SEO list starts empty and on a product with an existing record. Unticking the row disables Delete again. Before the 3.1004.2 hotfix (VCST-6306) Delete stayed disabled in the empty-list case.

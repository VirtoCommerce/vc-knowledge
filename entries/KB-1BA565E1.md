Signed in as the TechFlow organization maintainer on vcst, Chromium, theme 2.62.0-pr-2638-4506-45068bb8. Below 1024 px /company/members renders member cards (flex items-center border-b px-5) whose text column is `grow py-4.5 [word-break:break-word]` with the default min-width:auto and holds the roles line `truncate text-sm`. The column cannot shrink below the nowrap roles text, so the ellipsis never appears. At 375 px: a 2-role member ("Purchasing agent, Organization employee") gave document scrollWidth 404 vs clientWidth 360, badge 280–372 and gear 372–400 past the card edge 353; a 4-role member gave scrollWidth 692 at 320/375/390 px, roles line 541 px wide. At 320 px even 2-role "Invite sent" cards put the gear 3 px past the card. Long names wrap correctly. Also seen by the operator on another org with a 3-role line including a long custom role name.

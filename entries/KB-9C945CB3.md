Store default en-US. Permalink with an any-language page and an en-US page: slugInfo for cultureName en-US, de-DE and fr-FR all returned the en-US page (languageCode en-US). Permalink with an any-language page and a de-DE page: en-US visitor got the any-language page, de-DE visitor the de-DE page. For a page targeted at organization A: anonymous, anonymous with userId=<org A user>, a buyer without that organization, the same buyer with userId=<org A user>, and a token requested with organization_id=<org A> by a non-member (issued 200, me.contact.organizationId empty) all got slugInfo null, pageDocument(Pages id) null and pageDocuments totalCount 0; the org A buyer got all three. Admin token without organization also got pageDocument null. After republishing, slugInfo returned the new Pages id and pageDocument resolved it at the first sample (~1.9 s).

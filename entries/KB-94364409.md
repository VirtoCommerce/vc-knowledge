Created 11 AGENT-TEST organizations via POST /api/members (names include "Harbor Freight Logistics", "harbor view supplies", "HARBOR Point Industrial", "Birch Hardware Co") and a contact holding Approved memberships in all of them. As that user, me.contact.organizations(statuses:["Approved"], searchPhrase:"Harbor") returned exactly the three Harbor orgs in any case (not Hardware); searchPhrase "Har" returned those three plus "Birch Hardware Co" — a case-insensitive word-prefix match, not a whole-word one. Both matched on the first query right after creation, with no explicit Member reindex. sort:"name:asc" ordered case variants case-insensitively (Harbor Freight, HARBOR Point, harbor view).

Signed in as a sales rep serving 7 test organizations, I recorded each row's name, YTD total/count, last-year total and Call customer state on /company/my-customers (unsorted), then clicked the YTD purchases header twice. Each click re-issued SalesRepCustomers with sort "ytd-purchases" then "ytd-purchases:asc"; the rows reordered descending then ascending, and every organization kept its own numbers and the same call control: single tel: link for orgs with one number, a "Choose a number to call" dropdown with the primary contact's and the address phone for the org with two, disabled "No phone number" for orgs with none. The sorted responses still carried phone, address.phone and primaryContact.phones per item, with no errors[] and no console errors.

Locked both memberships of a 2-org contact (REST lock, LockoutEnd null, isCurrentlyLocked=true on both; the contact's currentOrganizationId still pointed to one of them). Signed in fresh with password: sign-in succeeded and the header showed only the user name. /account/dashboard and a deep link to /account/orders both rendered their content with no /403 redirect and no console error. The account-menu Organizations listbox listed both orgs, both disabled, with none marked selected. xAPI me.contact.organizationId was null, organizations totalCount 2 with isLockedForCurrentUser true on both. A session signed in BEFORE the lock also rendered the dashboard, and its listbox showed the previously active org as disabled+selected. An earlier observation of a /403 dead end in this state (2026-09) did not reproduce.

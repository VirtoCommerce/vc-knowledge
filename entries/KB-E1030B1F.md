As a user whose role has builder permissions for one store only, create_page (dryRun first, then real) with set_page_settings and three add_section operations (title, products-carousel, text) created a Draft landing page; Admin Shell #/content/site/<store> listed it as Draft and the designer (#/content/site-builder-designer/<id>) rendered its sections in the storefront preview. edit_page_draft with the page's version_token and set_section_settings changed the title section's subtitle; after reloading the designer the preview showed the new subtitle. The storefront schema catalog resource (site-builder://stores/<store>/schemas) answered available:false until the store's designer was opened once, then listed the section types. The tools/list of that user also held create_section_type and change_section_type (custom section types of a later build).

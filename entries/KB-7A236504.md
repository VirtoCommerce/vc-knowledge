Storefront "Sign in with Passkey" (virtual authenticator, store B2B-store) sends POST /connect/token grant_type=passkey with scope, storeId and credential; the access token the storefront then sends as its bearer on /graphql carries the claim http://schemas.microsoft.com/ws/2008/06/identity/claims/authenticationmethod = "Passkey" (no amr claim). Observed on 4 passkey sign-ins of one account, with the password both expired and not expired. A password sign-in of the same account carries "Password" and an email-code sign-in "OTP". xAPI treats any value other than "Password" as a non-password sign-in, so the passkey session is not gated by an expired password.

Signed in as a plain sales rep at a 375x812 viewport, /company/my-customers/{orgId} renders breadcrumbs Home / Account / Sales Rep hub / My customers with class vc-breadcrumbs__link, each 16.8 px tall. The trail wraps: the first three links sit at y=114, 'My customers' at y=132. axe-core 4.10.2 rule target-size reports 3 violations (Home 37x16.8, Account 51x16.8, My customers 87x16.8: 'Target has insufficient size'), because the 24 px circles of the wrapped rows overlap; 'Sales Rep hub' passes on spacing. On /company/dashboard at 375 px the same rule reports 0 violations. Component is the storefront UI kit breadcrumbs, so the pattern likely applies wherever the trail wraps; only this page was measured.

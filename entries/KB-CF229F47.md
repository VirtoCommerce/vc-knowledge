As a user with admin-shell:access + order:access + order:read only, the Insights section listed Analytics > Reports (the menu item carries no permission gate). Clicking it routed to #/app/reports and opened a "Reports" blade with Refresh and "Open in new tab" (/apps/reports/#). The embedded /apps/reports/ loaded (200) and then navigated its frame to GET /api/power-bi-reports/redirect, which returned 403 with content-length 0. The frame stayed blank white: no report data, and no refusal message.

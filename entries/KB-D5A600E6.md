A customer whose own store is in "Use global setting" (global MFA off) called Mutation.requestMfaChangeCode and Mutation.updateMfaUserMode with the storeId of another store in CustomerChoice. Both succeeded and the user mode was stored as Enabled. Afterwards a password grant naming the CustomerChoice store answered two_factor_required, a grant naming the user's own store issued tokens in one step, and a grant with no storeId answered user_cannot_login_in_store. The storeId is client-supplied and the change is not tied to ApplicationUser.StoreId; whether this is allowed is an open question to the module author.

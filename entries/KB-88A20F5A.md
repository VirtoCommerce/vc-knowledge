On /forgot-password, submitting an email that belongs to no account sent Mutation sendPasswordResetEmail, which returned HTTP 200 data true with no errors, and the page showed 'We sent you a reset password link to your inbox' with a Home page link — identical to the response and UI for a registered buyer's email submitted minutes earlier. Nothing on the page or in the response distinguishes the two.

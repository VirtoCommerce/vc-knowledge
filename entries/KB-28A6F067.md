On vcst_qa 2026-11-26 (platform 3.1101.0) a freshly created organization (GET /api/white-labeling/organization/{id} returned nothing) opened Contacts > organization > Manage > White labeling with Enabled OFF. Uploading logo and favicon and setting a preset and link lists, then Save, created the record with isEnabled=false; the toggle had to be switched ON and saved again. A test that asserts Enabled=ON on an organization it configures from scratch must switch it on itself.

Anonymous, Chromium 1920x1080, store B2B-store, theme vc-frontend-next 3.3.0-pr-23-7be3 (carries upstream vc-frontend 2.62.0). A product with 4 square 275x275 _md images: every .image-gallery__img renders at 478x478 with computed object-fit: contain, and the four .image-gallery__thumb-img render at 80x80 with object-fit: contain, so nothing is cropped. A product with no images (product.images empty in xAPI) renders one img with class vc-image--fallback image-gallery__img, an inline SVG placeholder, computed object-fit: scale-down inside the 480x480 gallery box, so the placeholder icon stays small and centred instead of being stretched. Product review photos on the same page use object-fit: cover at 70x70 and request an _sm variant of /api/files/<id> that answers 404 before the original loads.

Sales rep, Edge, 1440 px. With 'Due today' picked, saving a new task due Nov 14 replaced the chip with 'Due Nov 14, 2026' and listed the task. With no filter applied (after Reset filters), saving a task due Nov 13 left the list unfiltered (28 tasks) and added no day chip. Reset filters sent two GraphQL requests that both query the salesRepTasks field: operation SalesRepTasks (first 15, no filter, the list) and operation SalesRepTaskMarkers (first 200, a 7-week period, the calendar dots); the markers request also carries the keyword and filters, which is why the dots follow them. A check that counts 'salesRepTasks requests' must count by operation name.

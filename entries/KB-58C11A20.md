Deleting a Draft page group created via create-group-page (never published) returned 200 application/json {"pageDeleted":false,"indexDeleted":true}. A follow-up GET /api/page-builder-pages/grouped/{groupId} returned 404 and POST /api/page-builder-pages/search for the store returned totalCount 0. So pageDeleted:false does not mean the group survived; a teardown must verify by GET 404, not by that flag.

On Return 3.1005.0-pr-28 a buyer whose order addresses carry no email and whose contact has no emails, but whose security account has one, submitted a return (submitReturn). The notification journal (POST /api/notifications/journal, objectType Return) held a ReturnRegisteredEmailNotification To the ACCOUNT email. With Return.NotifyOrganizationEmail on, a second row went To the organization's first email, cc/bcc null. The buyer address therefore resolves from the order address email, then the contact email, then the account email. The organization copy does not depend on where the buyer address came from. The platform refuses to create an account without an email, so a buyer with no address anywhere could not be tested.

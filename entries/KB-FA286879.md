Cart quantity is the sum of line-item units (Query.cart itemsQuantity), not the number of lines. At 0 units there is no badge. From 1 to 99 units the badge shows the number. At 100 units and above it shows '99+' (checked at 100, 150, 1543 and 11493). While the badge is capped, hovering or keyboard-focusing the desktop header Cart link shows a tooltip with the exact count, pluralised and number-formatted for the storefront language: en '1,543 items', ru '1 543 товара', pl '1543 produkty', pt-BR '11.493 itens', es '11.493 artículos'. These match Intl.NumberFormat and Intl.PluralRules. At 99 units or fewer, focus shows no tooltip. The Cart link's accessible name is 'Cart: N' with the exact count (e.g. 'Cart: 150'), localized ('Корзина: 1 543', 'Koszyk: 1543'), never '99+'. At 375 px the mobile header cart link and the burger-menu Cart row carry the same name. The label box and its spacing to the neighbouring header links do not change between 0, 99 and 150 units; only the badge grows. Click and Enter on the Cart link still open /cart. Observed on a PR build of the theme (stamped build).

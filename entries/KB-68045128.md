Signed in as a multi-org B2B buyer on B2B-store (store has MockStoredCardPaymentMethod isActive=true per the run's setup ledger). /account/payment-methods: Query.cardPaymentMethods returned a single entry, AuthorizeNetPaymentMethod (Accept.js publicParameters). Save card calls Accept.js tokenize then Mutation.addSavedPaymentMethod(paymentMethodCode=AuthorizeNetPaymentMethod); on this stand it returned SAVED_PAYMENT_METHOD_ERROR "The card could not be saved" for both 4111111111111111 and a 3220 card, and the page shows the generic "Something went wrong. Please try again.". A double click on Save card sent exactly one tokenize and one mutation. Separately, a cart holding only a one-time line (no subscription line) in either of the buyer's organizations listed AuthorizeNet, CyberSource, DefaultManual, Loyalty, Skyflow in availablePaymentMethods — MockStoredCardPaymentMethod (Credit card (test)) was absent. Whether the mock is filtered to subscription carts was not established (no subscribable product was in stock).

Signed in to the classic admin as a non-admin whose role holds admin-shell:access, order:access and order:read. Orders list (#!/workspace/orders) sent only customerOrders/indexed/searchEnabled and customerOrders/search (200) — no stores/search. Opening an order sent POST /api/stores/search twice (by objectIds and a 25-row list) and POST /api/members/search, all 403; the blade showed a red '403: Forbidden' banner and the Store field stayed 'Loading...'. So the order-blade 403 pattern predates the Vue Admin Shell; the Vue Orders list's extra stores/search call is new.

QA Storybook (storefront UI kit), Firefox. Row story (components-organisms-vcstatwidget--row, flex-wrap with grow basis-44): at 1920 all six cards are 301x156 with the figure top 51px from the card top and the delta top 115px, including the two cards with no description. At 1168 only five cards fit on the first line (214px each) and the sixth (Customers, error) wraps alone and stretches to 1136x92. At 900 the line is 204/204/204/207px: the "$402,900.00" card is held at its min-content width (figure 170px plus 37px padding and border), so figures never overflow. At 375 and in the Colors grid at 480 and 1024 nothing overflows (document scrollWidth equals viewport width). Basic story with icon, description, delta and deltaIcon set to undefined: head top 17px, height 28px (label min-height of two 14px lines), figure top 51px, the same as a full card, with no empty gaps. The sr-only output is absolute and takes no flex gap. In a mixed line, the loading card's dash sits at the figure line (51px), but the error row is pinned to the bottom (mt-auto): 65px at 900 in a 106px line, 115px at 1920. With Basic value "$1,512,400.00" and valueSuffix "items" typed into Controls (256px card), the figure stays on one line (194px) and "items" wraps onto a second line at 105px, growing the card to 193px.

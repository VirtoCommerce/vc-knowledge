Signed in on the storefront as a Purchasing agent and, separately, as an Organization maintainer of the same organization, neither holding sales-rep:access. Opening /company/my-customers directly settled on /account/dashboard within about 3 seconds. The account left rail showed Purchasing, Marketing, Corporate and User with no Sales Rep hub section and no My customers link; the 375px mobile account menu showed the same (Corporate holds Company info, Company members and Sales reps only). Every /graphql request on the page returned 200 and none of their operation names was salesRep*; no request went to a sales-rep GraphQL endpoint; there were no console errors. So the route guard turns away both a plain buyer and a privileged org maintainer before any sales-rep data is fetched.

Signed in as a loyalty-progress owner on B2B-store, opened the AGENT-TEST-MSN-PROGRESS-PARTIAL PerSkuAll modal. GetLoyaltyMissionProgress returned item rows: Target-B currentQuantity 1 / targetQuantity 3 and Target-A currentQuantity 2 / targetQuantity 2. The modal chip for Target-B read 'Buy at least 2' (= 3 - 1, the remaining), and the chip for Target-A read 'Buy at least 2' with a met-check icon (= its target). Raising the Target-B stepper to 2 moved 'Targets met' from 1 / 2 to 2 / 2. For a mission with no progress (current 0) remaining equals target, so the chip equals targetQuantity there (PERSKU-ALL: both chips 2, API targets 2).

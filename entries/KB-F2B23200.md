On /company/dashboard (scope dashboard) hiding My activity and saving sent SaveSalesRepLayout with my_activity hidden:true in region mainRight; after reload the widget was absent and no SalesRepActivities operation was sent (the other widgets' operations were). Clicking "Show My activity" in the Hidden widgets tray fired SalesRepActivities immediately. A keyboard move (space, arrow, space) of my_activity above tasks persisted after reload. On /company/my-customers/<org> (scope customerProfile) hiding Customer activity likewise suppressed SalesRepCustomerActivitySummary. Reset then Save restored the default (tasks before my_activity, nothing hidden). Moving a widget and then hiding it in the same edit session saved the original order, not the moved one. Non-rep members hitting /company/activities, /company/dashboard or /company/my-customers/<id> are redirected to /account/dashboard with no hub DOM rendered and no activity query sent.

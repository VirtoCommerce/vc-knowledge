At 375 and 768 px viewports on /company/my-customers/{id}/coupons the breadcrumb links (Home › Account › Sales Rep hub › My customers › customer › Promotion coupons) measure 17 px high with about 1 px between neighbours; axe reports target-size (serious) 4 times, and the spacing exception does not save them. The same breadcrumbs appear on the other hub pages. The "All promotion coupons" link in the profile widget header measures 156×18 px; it is the only control in that header, so it most likely passes through the 2.5.8 spacing exception. Coupon code buttons are ≥ 44 px; pagination buttons are 32 px (above AA, below 44).

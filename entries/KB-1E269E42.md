On Platform 3.1077.0 (PR build), BackgroundJobs 3.1052.0, Orders 3.1018.0, Subscription 3.1006.0-pr-128-7611: a storefront buyer placed an order (xAPI createOrderFromCart, DefaultManualPaymentMethod, FixedRate shipping) for a product with a monthly PaymentPlan. The CreateSubscriptionsFromOrdersJob payload is now {"OrderIds":[...]} and deserializes fine, but every attempt throws CreateSubscriptionException -> NullReferenceException at SendNotificationsSubscriptionChangedEventHandler.GetSubscriptionOrderEmailAsync (subscription.CustomerOrderPrototype is null on the SubscriptionChangedEvent NewEntry, because platform CrudService rebuilds NewEntry from the entity after commit). The exception is raised AFTER the subscription (and its prototype order) are committed and BEFORE the order is linked, so the initial run plus 3 Hangfire retries produced 4 Unpaid subscriptions per order; order.subscriptionId stays null and subscription.customerOrdersIds is null; the job ends Failed. Reproduced on 4 of 4 plan orders (3 plan-only + 1 mixed). An order with no plan product: job Succeeded, no subscription. Requeuing an old (3.1004-3.1006 whole-order) payload from the dashboard: it is read as ids (job reached the subscription save for the original order) — Succeeded when the product had no plan, same NRE when it did. Order status/in-payment status and the order change log were unchanged by the job.

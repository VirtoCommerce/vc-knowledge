2026-10-05, theme 2.58.0 against a backend with VirtoCommerce.Loyalty 3.1007.0-pr-17-df7a (read from GET /api/platform/modules). Shortly after a hard load a red alert appeared in the Notifications region at the top right: 'The frontend application expects newer versions of the Platform and one or more Modules. Frontend Application is running on an incompatible backend. Some features may be unavailable, degraded, or unstable until the Platform and Modules are upgraded to meet the storefront's requirements. Please share the report below with your administrator.' followed by 'VirtoCommerce.Loyalty 3.1008.0 ≥ 3.1007.0-pr-17-df7a' (required version, then installed version) and a 'Copy to clipboard' button. It overlaid the right side of the header while shown, was visible in screenshots taken about 3 s after load and absent from ones taken later, logged no console error, and did not block rendering or SEO resolution. It is an environment-version signal, not a defect of the page under test; the module list it compares comes from the storefront's InitializeApplication query (Query.store, settings.modules with moduleId and version).

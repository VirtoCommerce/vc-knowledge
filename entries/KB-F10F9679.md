On the B2B store holding 776 PageBuilder pages, POST /api/page-builder-pages/search with { storeId, take: 500 } returned 200 results while totalCount was 776. A canonical published page past the first 200 was therefore invisible to the caller. Paging with skip in steps of 200 returned all of them.

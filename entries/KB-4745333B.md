Signed-in buyer on B2B-store, culture de-DE, currency EUR, viewport 390x844. Product with a PERCENT 10 subscription plan whose price lists exist only in USD. GetProduct (currencyCode EUR) returned price.actual/list 0.0 EUR and availabilityData isBuyable=false, isAvailable=false, isInStock=true, availableQuantity 3529. The PDP still rendered group 'Purchase options': 'One-time purchase 0,00 €' (checked) and 'Subscribe & Save' with badge 'Save 10%' and price 'N/A'; selecting Subscribe & Save revealed 'Deliver' combobox 'Every month' and the two reassurance bullets. Quantity stepper showed 0 and was disabled, no add-to-cart control rendered. So the subscription block is not gated on buyability/price; the purchase is blocked only by the disabled stepper. The block's strings stayed English under de-DE. Layout fit 390 px without horizontal scroll.

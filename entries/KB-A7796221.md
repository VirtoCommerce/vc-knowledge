vcst_qa, theme 2.63.0, Chromium 375x812, signed in as a member of an organization with its own main-menu list (Home, Brands with Laptops/Phones/Tablets, Company, Support). Tapping button 'Main menu' opens a drawer whose top level is Dashboard, Catalog, Bulk order, Cart, Contact us, Compare plus the account block. Tapping Catalog replaces the list with a panel headed 'Catalog' listing Home, Brands, Company, Support and 'View all products in the category'. Tapping Brands replaces it with a panel headed 'Brands' listing Laptops, Phones, Tablets (no in-place expand/indent). Tapping Laptops navigates to /search?q=laptop and closes the drawer. vc-frontend 2.63.0 useNavigations.ts mobileMainMenuItems puts catalogMenuItems (the white-labeling mainMenuLinks when present) as children of the catalog entry.

On a blob-flow store (Page Builder publish-status flow "blob", FileSystem content storage), POST /api/content/pages/{storeId}?folderUrl=&draft=false multipart with a part named "X.page-draft" did not create X.page-draft: it overwrote the published X.page with that content (the -draft suffix was dropped). Uploading a part named "X.page" with draft=true wrote X.page-draft next to X.page; GET /api/pagebuilder/git/publish-status then reported published:true, hasChanges:true. A new page uploaded only with draft=true exists as X.page-draft (published:false), and POST /api/pagebuilder/git/publish?storeId&type=pages&path=/X.page answered 200 {"state":"Merged"} and turned it into X.page.

Two customer tokens (password grant with storeId): an organization member whose org role lacks xapi:my_organization:mfa:manage, and a customer with no organization. mfaSettings(storeId, organizationId = member's own org) returned organizationId + organizationMode Off with canChangeOrganizationMode false; with a foreign organization id, or as the customer without an organization, it returned organizationId null, organizationMode null, canChangeOrganizationMode false (no foreign mode disclosed). updateMfaOrganizationMode(enabled true, a bogus twoFactorToken and code) for the own org and for a foreign org answered succeeded false, errorCode mfa_organization_not_allowed, "You can't manage multi-factor authentication of this organization.", settings null, no errors[]; the admin GET /api/mfa/modes/organizations/{id} read Off before and after for both orgs. On this build InputRequestMfaChangeCodeType has only storeId (no organizationId).

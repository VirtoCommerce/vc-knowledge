As administrator with existing history, opening Inventory, Promotions and Contacts sent POST /api/admin-shell/menu/me/recent/{id} (204) each. After a full reload, GET /api/admin-shell/menu returned recent (23 ids) where customers moved from 18th to 9th and inventory/marketing stayed 7th/8th; the Home panel showed only the first 5 (orders, platform.menu, platform.settings, platform.security, catalog), so none of the three appeared. Module source (MenuComposer.RecordUse/RankRecent) adds 1 per use, halves a score every 7 days without use, keeps the top 30 and orders by score then last use; pinned and Home items are excluded. Frequency ranking is the design, not a recency list.

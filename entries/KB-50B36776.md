Firefox, browser zone UTC+1, en-US, rep serving 5 organizations. In the New task modal (fields Title*, Type, Due date* prefilled with today, Priority, Notes) the Due date calendar offered Nov 2 (yesterday) as an enabled day; picking it set 11/02/2026 and enabled Save. Save sent Mutation.createSalesRepTask {name, dueDate 2026-11-01T23:00:00.000Z (local midnight), priority Normal, type Other} with no customerId; the response returned the task (isActive true, completed null) with no errors[], the modal closed and 'Task saved' toasted. Badges went All 22->23, Overdue 10->11, Upcoming 4 and Completed 4 unchanged, without a reload. Under the Overdue tab the row read 'Expired Nov 2, 2026 · Other' with status 'Overdue' and action 'Mark as complete'. Deleting it through Edit task > Delete > OK restored All 22 and Overdue 10.

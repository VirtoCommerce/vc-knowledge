DELETE /api/loyalty-missions returns 500 once a mission has mission transactions: the database refuses with a RESTRICT foreign key from the mission-transaction table to the mission. The bulk delete is atomic, so a failed call leaves every mission in the batch intact. Combined with a Published mission being immutable, a mission that has progress can be neither edited nor deleted and recreated; a fresh mission is the only way forward.

Approved is pre-filled with the requested quantity; one click on Approve / decline commits immediately with no confirmation dialog, sets status Approved/PartiallyApproved/Rejected, renders Quantity/Approved/Decline reason read-only, and a ReturnApproved/ReturnPartiallyApproved/ReturnRejectedEmailNotification journal entry appears within ~1s (7 of 7 decisions on 2026-09-29, SMTP failing so status Error). Decline reason enables only when Approved < Quantity; Approve / decline is disabled while the blade is dirty after a Quantity edit.

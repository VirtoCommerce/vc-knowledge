Catalog 3.1046 (PR 909 build), store Search configuration > Barcode scanner blade: a field stored in Catalog.Search.BarcodeSearchFields that GET .../fields no longer lists (property deleted, or written through the generic store settings API) is listed first, checked, with a 'MISSING FROM INDEX' badge. Clicking it does nothing (stays checked, Save stays disabled), although it has no greyed styling and hover-highlights like other rows. On the first change of any other control (match mode toggle, or another field's checkbox) the row disappears from the list, is not offered again, and Save persists fields without it. The blade shows no warning naming the deleted property.

2026-10-15, admin token, B2B-store. Cancelled 6 orders by PUT /api/order/customerOrders with the full GET body plus status Cancelled, isCancelled true, cancelReason, cancelledDate. Each order read back Cancelled / isCancelled true. Three orders had a DefaultManualPaymentMethod inPayment at status New: those payments followed to status Cancelled, isCancelled true, cancelledState Completed within seconds. Three orders had an inPayment already at status Paid (one DatatransPaymentMethod, two SkyflowPaymentMethod): re-read immediately and again several minutes later, each stayed status Paid, isCancelled false, while its cancelledState read Completed. All 6 shipments stayed New. So the order-cancel cascade does not void or cancel a captured payment; a cancelled order can carry a live Paid payment, and money taken is not returned by cancelling. Anything that treats order cancellation as settling the payment (refund expectations, payment reports filtered on isCancelled) must handle Paid payments separately, e.g. via a refund.

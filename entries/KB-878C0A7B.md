Signed in as a sales rep with a 22-task relative-offset fixture seeded on 2026-10-15, opened /company/tasks on 2026-10-20 (Firefox, 1920 viewport). Chips read Today 1, All 22, Upcoming 4, Overdue 10, Completed 4. The Tasks widget calendar on /company/dashboard placed the seed's +5 completed task on today, the +7 pair on Oct 22 and the +14 task on Oct 29, which dates the seed day. All chips stay enabled and nothing errors; only the date-relative groups drift (Overdue grows, Today/Upcoming shrink), so All stays equal to the seeded total. A case asserting the seed-day group sizes therefore passes a count of All but fails every date-scoped chip.

Tested as a guest at 1920px in Firefox and Edge, on the PDP of a product without variations. The header 'Theme: auto' toggle was clicked once to reach dark (html.dark). The SKU line under the H1 (span.text-secondary-900 inside div.vc-copy-text) computes #0d1013 on the #121317 container background, 1.03:1, so it is effectively invisible. In light mode the same line is 18.28:1. The copy button stays visible at 16.96:1 because it reads --color-vc-text-outline-secondary. In the html.dark token block, the secondary scale is inverted only at 50, 100, 200, 600 and 700. Steps 300, 400 and 500 keep their light values, and so do 800 #242b34, 900 #0d1013 and 950 #010202. Source: mercury.dark.json and default.dark.json in vc-frontend dev. The other bundled dark presets (coffee, red, black-gold, watermelon, purple-pink) invert the whole scale. Filed as VCST-6307.

Seeded an AGENT-TEST contact with contact.organizations=[A,B] and two OrganizationMembership rows (POST /api/customer/organization-memberships): A Approved, B set in turn via full-row PUT to Invited, Rejected, Deleted, null, and Approved+isLocked. Signed in as the user (password grant, storeId=B2B-store) and queried me.contact.organizations. With NO statuses argument (and also with statuses:[]) totalCount was 1 and only A was listed for Invited, Rejected and Deleted — identical to statuses:["Approved"]. statuses:["Invited"] returned only B (myStatusInOrganization Invited); statuses:["Approved","Invited"] returned 2. A null-status row was counted and reported as myStatusInOrganization Approved (totalCount 2); an Approved row with isLocked=true was counted (totalCount 2, isLockedForCurrentUser true). So a storefront gate that reads the unfiltered totalCount cannot be made to diverge from the Approved-only list through a non-Approved membership status on this build — only by passing the non-Approved statuses explicitly.

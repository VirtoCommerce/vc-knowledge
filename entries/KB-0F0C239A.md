Picking a calendar day deselects the active scope chip and drops the ?filter query from the URL; a date chip with a Clear (x) control appears. Closing it with the day on screen, with the day paged off screen, after picking from All, and after picking from Upcoming all land on Today (Today chip pressed, current month, no filter query) and move focus to the Today chip. The page keeps no memory of the scope that was active before the pick. Separately, clicking the Today chip while a date chip exists leaves the date chip visible beside the pressed Today chip.

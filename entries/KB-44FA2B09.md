Signed in as a B2B buyer on vcst, searched q=kitchen and ticked Categories > TV & Multimedia (8 results), then loaded /search with a no-match keyword plus the same facet: page shows 0 results, 'There are no results found', and a 'Reset search' button. Clicking it navigated to /catalog?facets=<same TV & Multimedia outline facet>: keyword empty, the TV & Multimedia chip still active, total 81 (filtered) rather than the full catalog (3,611). So the button resets only the keyword, not the facets.

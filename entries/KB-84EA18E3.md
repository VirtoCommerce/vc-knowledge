GET /api/catalog/barcode-search/store/{storeId}/fields lists code, gtin and manufacturerPartNumber first, then catalog properties of type Product or Variation with value type ShortText under their lowercased name (e.g. a property MY_UPC is offered as my_upc). A ShortText property of type Catalog is not offered. A newly created Product-type ShortText property was listed on the next call without any product reindex.

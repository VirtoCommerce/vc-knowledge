Signed in as a sales rep on /company/dashboard, the first KPI card reads 'Orders in “New” status' (value, '$X total', 'of N created in the last 7 days'). In Edit layout, focusing that card and pressing Space announced 'Orders in “New” status grabbed. Position 1 of 6. Use the left and right arrow keys to move it, up and down to hide or show it, space to drop it, escape to cancel.'; ArrowLeft at the edge announced '... is already at position 1 of 6.', ArrowRight '... moved to position 2 of 6.', Space '... dropped at position 2 of 6.'. For a widget in the vertical mainLeft column (Top sellers) the grab text is '<name> grabbed. Position 2 of 2. Use the arrow keys to move it, space to drop it, escape to cancel.'; Escape restores the position and keeps focus on the handle. Cases expecting the name 'New orders' are stale.

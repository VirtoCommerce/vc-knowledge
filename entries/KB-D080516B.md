A membership status is one of Invited, Approved, Rejected or Deleted. Invited, Rejected and Deleted are blocking (so an Invited member cannot sign in to that organization); Rejected and Deleted can be re-invited. The effective status is the membership's own status, else the contact's global status, else Approved - so memberships with no status of their own inherit the contact's. Revoking an organization invite sets the status to Deleted. The storefront labels Approved as Active, Deleted as Inactive, Invited as Invited and a lock as Blocked; Rejected is rendered nowhere.

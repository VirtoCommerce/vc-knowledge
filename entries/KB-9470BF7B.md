Signed-in B2B buyer with one line (qty 1) in Saved for later and an empty cart. Clicking "Add all to cart" on /account/saved-for-later opened a dialog headed "Adding products to cart result" with a "Successfully added (1)" section listing the product, SKU and quantity, and a footer with a "View cart" link and "Print" and "OK" buttons. After OK and a reload, Saved for later showed its empty state and the line was in the cart, so the action moves rather than copies. With the viewport resized to 460x900 while the dialog was open, the dialog filled the viewport; the footer was 460px wide with 24px side padding; "View cart" was 412px wide (the full content width) on its own row, and Print and OK were 128px each on the row below. A sibling note: /bulk-order does not open this dialog (see KB-E5089A10).

With a store's barcode fields set to [gtin] (exact mode), Query.products with filter barcode:"<v>" returned: full 13-digit GTIN → 1; "*" → 313 (every product holding a gtin); first 12 digits + "*" → 1; first 12 digits + "?" → 1; first 12 digits without a wildcard → 0. HTTP 200, no errors[]. The response reports the generated filter gtin with the raw value "2945100000*". The storefront /search?barcode=<10-digit prefix>* then redirects to that product's page, because it opens the PDP on a single hit. Cause: EvalBarcodeFilterMiddleware builds a TermFilter from the raw value, and the ElasticSearch8 filters builder turns any single-value term containing * or ? into a WildcardQuery. The filter-syntax grammar has no escape for * or ?. Observed on XCatalog 3.1022.0-pr-113-f4a8, Catalog 3.1046.0-pr-909-2839, ElasticSearch8 3.1007.0. Tracked as VCST-6094.

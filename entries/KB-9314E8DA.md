On vcptcore_regression (theme 2.59.0, signed-in B2B buyer, B2B-store) the line quantity spinbutton on /cart swallows the '-', '.' and 'e' keys: typing 1 . 5 gives 15, typing 1 e 2 gives 12, and '-' then 1 gives 1. Replacing the whole field value the way a paste does (one input event) bypasses the filter. '-1' is sent as ChangeFullCartItemsQuantity quantity -1 and stored (HTTP 200, PRODUCT_MIN_QTY in validationErrors only, still -1 after a reload, the inline message 'You can order minimum 1 item(s)', a generic 'Something went wrong' notice). '1.5' is sent verbatim and rejected with HTTP 400 'Unable to convert 1.5 to Int'; the field keeps showing 1.5, the line total keeps the last server value, and a generic 'server is experiencing technical issues' toast appears. '1e2' is parsed as 100 and committed. The field also commits on input with a debounce, without waiting for blur, so an intermediate keystroke value (15) can be stored before the buyer finishes.

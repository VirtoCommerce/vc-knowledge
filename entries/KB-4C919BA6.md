On SalesRep 3.1012.0-pr-21, as the rep on /graphql/sales-rep, Mutation.sendCustomerCommunication(organizationIds:[orgA, orgB], sendPush, sendEmail) created exactly ONE PushMessage record (admin POST /api/push-message/search) whose memberIds equal the distinct union of the Contact members of both orgs minus the calling rep's own contact; a contact created as a member of both orgs appeared once, as did two pre-existing contacts in both orgs. The notifications journal (POST /api/notifications/journal, type SalesRepMessageEmailNotification) held one entry per distinct member email (17 entries, 17 distinct addresses, rep address absent). The contact's own Query.pushMessages on /graphql showed the message once. The same holds for a list-share message (message + /shared-list/<key> link) sent to both orgs. Legacy organizationId X combined with organizationIds [X, X uppercased] also yields one record with no duplicate memberIds.

Anonymous cart, Firefox. Typed 456 in the quantity stepper and blurred (cart = 456). Refocused, deleted with Backspace one char at a time (456 → 45 → 4 → empty), blurred: the field showed 4 and UpdateShortCartItemQuantity was sent with quantity 4 (catalog card on /search, PDP gave 6 when deleting after the first digit; 789 gave 7). On /cart the line went to 4 via ChangeFullCartItemsQuantity while the field was still focused and empty. Intermediate values (56, 6) were also sent before any blur. Select-all + one Backspace + blur restored 456 and sent nothing. An empty field never became 0 or removed the line. Source: VcQuantityStepper normalize() restores lastNonEmptyValue, which a watch on model updates on every keystroke.

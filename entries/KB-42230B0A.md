From x-cart 3.1038.0-pr-141-b404 and on the released XCart 3.1039.0 (vcptcore) and 3.1041.0 (vcst) with Cart 3.1012.0, observed 2026-10-12, SharingSettingType.targets comes back in one order, ascending by organization id (case-insensitive), in the createWishlist and changeWishlist responses and on every later wishlist(listId) read, whatever order the ids were added in (vc-module-x-cart WishlistType.cs orders Targets by SharedWithId, VCST-6152). The owner's deprecated sharingSetting.sharedWithId is targets[0] of that order, on writes and reads alike. A legacy changeWishlist with scope Customer and sharedWithId equal to any present target (first or not, any letter case) is a no-op (HTTP 200, no errors). On a list with more than one target, a sharedWithId that is not a present target is refused INVALID_OPERATION and nothing is written; on a single-target list it replaces the target. Without scope, a legacy sharedWithId (and addSharedWithIds) is ignored silently. On the earlier PR build x-cart 3.1037.0-pr-141 the write response listed targets in insertion order while reads returned another order, and legacy writes matched the first target only.

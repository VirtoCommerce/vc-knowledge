Subscription paid with mock card ending 9995 (renewal soft decline, insufficient funds), pickup shipping, store RetryScheduleHours=24,72,120. scheduler/run -> Notified. One POST renewals/{id}/process at 16:37:59: attempt 1 SoftDecline -> RetryScheduled 'next attempt +24h', then in the same call 'Retrying the payment' RetryScheduled -> Ordered, attempt 2 SoftDecline -> RetryScheduled +72h. Subscription stays Active. One POST renewals/{id}/retry-payment 15 s later: attempts 3 and 4 in the same call, then 'We couldn't charge your card after several attempts' -> Failed, subscription PastDue, card status stays Active. The renewal history states next-attempt dates that the same call then ignores. The scheduler path (ignoreSchedule=false) is not affected; the manual process/retry path loops through RetryScheduled because it ignores NextAttemptDate.

A full load of a product page renders the header Account menu button empty (chevron only, accessible name only) only where the ProductGuide widget mounts on that page; other PDPs and cold loads of / and /catalog show 'Org / Name'. vcptcore (theme 2.60.0-pr-2522-6f90, 2026-10-13, multi-org sales rep, 1920px): the Epson ET-2550 PDP was labelled at ~0.65 s and empty at ~1.9-2.2 s (2/2), the Xerox 3225 PDP and another PDP stayed labelled; vcst (2.60.0-pr-2511-81f1, no widget): three PDPs, / and /catalog labelled from ~0.66 s, never blank. The account button's org/name label is a span with classes 'hidden min-w-0 gap-1 font-bold xl:flex' (top-header.vue). The ProductGuide widget (cdn.useproductguide.com/pg.js, injected by the stand, not theme code) adds <style id="pg-widget-styles"> with '.hidden { display: none !important; }' when it mounts on a page; the !important beats xl:flex, so the label stays in the DOM with display none and the button shows only its chevron. The style survives SPA navigation, so the button stays empty on later routes until a full load of a page without the widget. Opening the menu still shows the right organization; it is a display defect caused by the stand's widget, not by the PDP itself.

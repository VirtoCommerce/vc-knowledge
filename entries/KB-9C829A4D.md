Theme 2.59.0, a Completed order paid with 'Test payment method': the sidebar 'Payment method' widget shows the method image and the name 'Test payment method' only. No payment status (Paid/Authorized) and no payment amount appear on the page; the amount is only visible as the order Total in 'Order summary', and the order status in 'Order data'. Source 2.59.0 pages/account/order-details.vue renders the widget as VcImage(paymentMethod.logoUrl) + paymentMethodName and nothing else. The StorefrontUserGuide Orders page does not promise payment status on the detail page; the only payment action it documents is Pay now for orders in 'Payment required' status.

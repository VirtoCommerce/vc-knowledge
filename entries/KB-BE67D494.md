Desktop header cart badge: 99 shows "99", 100 shows "99+", 150 shows "99+". With 150 items, hovering or keyboard-focusing the desktop cart link shows a tooltip "150 items" (role tooltip); the link's aria-label is "Cart: 150" while its visible text is "99+ Cart". At 375 px the mobile header cart link shows "99+" with aria-label "Cart: 150". An empty cart shows no badge.

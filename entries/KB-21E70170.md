At 1920x1080 on theme 2.59 pr-2500, a fresh load of /account/returns/:id first renders the shell with an H1 'Returns' and a spinner (no sidebar), then swaps in the return. When the return content exceeds the viewport (a 3-line PartiallyApproved return, 1220px) buffered layout-shift sums to 0.1456/0.1499/0.1457 over 3 loads, one ~0.145 entry whose sources are the content wrapper, header and footer (the vertical scrollbar appears and the footer is pushed down). A 1-line Requested return that fits the viewport measured 0.0012 on 3 loads. An order detail page of similar height measured 0.028.

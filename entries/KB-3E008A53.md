On /search the quantity stepper's Increase click queues Mutation.updateCartQuantity (operation UpdateShortCartItemQuantity) in the storefront's queued-mutations Apollo link (1000 ms debounce, single-flight, merges rapid clicks); no request goes out during the window. Navigating (page.goto /cart) within ~20 ms of the click: the app's beforeunload handler calls window.confirm, which Chrome blocks (console: "Blocked confirm('Some operations are still in progress...') during beforeunload") and then shows its native leave-page dialog; a Playwright MCP navigate waits on it until its 60 s timeout. Only after the dialog is accepted does the overdue debounce fire: the HAR shows the POST /graphql starting 2-6 ms after GET /cart, ~61-68 s after the click, status -1 (aborted by the unload). Whether the item lands is a race: 1 of 2 repros left the cart empty, 1 added it. Clicking and waiting >1 s before navigating never showed the prompt (6/6). This is client-side, not a backend hang, and unrelated to the order placed before.

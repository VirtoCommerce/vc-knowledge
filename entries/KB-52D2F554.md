On vcst 2026-11-23, the WhiteLabeling module swagger lists only GET /api/white-labeling/{id}, GET /organization/{organizationId}, GET /store/{storeId} and POST/PUT /api/white-labeling. DELETE /api/white-labeling?ids=<id> and DELETE /api/white-labeling/{id} both returned 405 for two organization-level records. After DELETE /api/members?ids= removed their organizations (204), GET /api/white-labeling/{id} still returned 200 with the record (organizationId pointing at the deleted org). So a teardown cannot remove these records over REST; they remain as orphans. The module source has no member-deleted event handler either.

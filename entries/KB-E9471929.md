With de active, navigating to /en/ ended at /de/en/ and the storefront 404 page ('404 Seite nicht gefunden'). /en-GB/ switched the locale to en-GB (URL kept /en-GB/). /en-US/ redirected to / with English (United States) active; from de, /en-US/, /en-US/accessories and /en-US/search?q=laptop all switched locale and normalised to /, /accessories, /search?q=laptop with no stacking. /de/accessories/, /de//accessories normalised to /de/accessories; /de%2Faccessories ended on a graceful 404 at /de/de%2Faccessories; a category whose slug starts with a locale code (destinations) kept its full slug after switching to Deutsch (/de/destinations). Switching language via the header button: the first 'English' entry is en-GB, the last is en-US.

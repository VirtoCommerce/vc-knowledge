On theme 2.60.0-pr-2523, /account/returns?scope=organization (5 columns RMA, Date, Buyer name, Status, Qty) at viewport 768 and 799 px renders the desktop table beside the account sidebar; cell text breaks inside words: RMA 'RET26100/8-00113', Date '10/8/20/26', Status 'Reques/ted', and a 32-char unbroken buyer name wraps across three lines inside its cell. At 600 and 375 px the list switches to stacked cards and the date/status read whole; at 1920 px the table reads whole. The sidebar labels at 768 also break mid-word ('Missions & challeng/es'). This holds for the early pr-2523 builds (9e4e67ef and before). vc-frontend 67896dcf (2026-10-08, 'stop the returns table breaking words mid-word', returns.vue mobile-breakpoint="lg" and fixed Date/Buyer/Status widths) moved the switch to 1024 px: on 2.60.0-pr-2523-b9a7 (local storefront over the vcst backend, 2026-10-12, Chromium and Firefox) the list is cards at 768, 799, 960 and 1023 px with RMA, Date and Status whole, and the table from 1024 px. The sidebar labels still break mid-word ('challeng/es', 'promoti/ons') from 768 to 1279 px. PR VirtoCommerce/vc-frontend#2523 is not merged.

USD Rounding001 with a 5% item discount on a 26.25 product: AwayFromZero gives placedPrice 24.94 and line 74.81 for qty 3; ToZero gives placedPrice 24.93 (24.9375 truncated, although it is not a midpoint) and line 74.82 (line = list total minus rounded discount 3.93). For a true midpoint 86.735 (55% of 157.70) ToZero and ToNegativeInfinity give 86.73, AwayFromZero and ToEven 86.74; a 2.625 discount gives 2.62 under ToEven/ToZero/ToNegativeInfinity and 2.63 under AwayFromZero. Currency changes in Admin (Store > Default currency edit > currency > Midpoint rounding > Save) take effect in xAPI cart totals immediately.

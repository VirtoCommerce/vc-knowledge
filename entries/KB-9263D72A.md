On vcst_qa (theme 2.62.0-pr-2629) the quantity spinbutton (input type number, PDP, /search card and /cart line) ignored Control+a as a select-all (clicking, pressing Control+a and typing 7 on a field showing 5 gave 57) and ignored End as a caret move (Backspace after End deleted a middle digit, 234 became 24). A triple-click selected the whole value (typing or Backspace then replaced it), and ArrowRight pressed four times after a click put the caret after the last digit, so Backspace deleted 234 to 23 to 2. A one-event fill replaces the value regardless of selection.

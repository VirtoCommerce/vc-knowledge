On /catalog (guest is enough) the 'Available at branches' checkbox in the category toolbar opens the 'Select branch' modal at >= sm; at 375px it is reached via Open filters -> 'Available at branches' in the filters sidebar. On theme 2.63.0-pr-2630 (vcst_qa) the search input's wrapper div carries bg-additional-50 and sm:pr-0.5: computed background white in light mode (near-black in dark mode), padding-inline-end 2px, wrapper 2px wider than the VcInput, so square corners of that background show behind the 8px-radius input on the neutral-50 header strip (most visible with the Paprika preset, strip #F5F1EA). Theme build 2.63.0-pr-2680 (VCST-6679, not yet released) replaces it with .branch-search__field: background transparent, padding-inline-end 0 at >= sm, 0px overhang; below sm the wrapper stays 44px tall with 10px end padding. Store preset on vcst_qa is Mercury (whiteLabelingSettings.themePresetName).

On vcst B2B-store, POST /api/taxes/search lists two providers that are both isActive true: AvaTaxRateProvider and FixedRateTaxProvider (Rate 20). With that configuration, POST /api/taxes/B2B-store/evaluate for a 45 USD line returns an empty array, and storefront carts measure taxTotal 0. On 2026-09-10 the same cart composition measured a 20% tax. Which provider was activated between those dates was not established. Observed 2026-10-01.

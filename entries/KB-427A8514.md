In /apps/page-builder-shell New page blade, filling a ~1050-character permalink leaves exactly 1024 characters in the field; End + an extra keypress adds nothing; Save stores a 1024-character permalink (GET grouped). The validation message says 'at most 2048 characters' and the REST endpoints accept 2048, so 1025-2048 permalinks cannot be entered in the shell and the length error cannot be reached by typing.

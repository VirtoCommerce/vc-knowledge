---
A /sign-in page was loaded while the store's Passkey scheme was active (button shown). The scheme was then switched off via PUT /api/store-authentication-schemes/{storeId} (Passkey isActive=false, read back false). After 60 s, without reloading, clicking 'Sign in with Passkey' sent POST /connect/token grant_type=passkey&storeId=<store>, which answered 200 with an access token; the customer was signed in and the sign-in log recorded a succeeded Passkey row. A fresh load of /sign-in hides the button, so the switch is enforced only in the UI, not at the token endpoint.

POST /api/marketing/promotions/coupons/add with [{ promotionId, code, memberId: <organization id> }] stored the coupon with that memberId, read back by POST /api/marketing/promotions/coupons/search. POST /api/marketing/promotions without startDate returned a promotion whose startDate was the creation time (to the second); a body with a future startDate (00:00:00Z ten days out) kept it, and isActive stayed true on the entity while the promotion was not yet running.

With Catalog.Search.EventBasedIndexation.Enable=true, a product was renamed while a full Product Build was at 50/413. xAPI products(storeId, query:<new name>) found it after 0.7 s, and the Build still completed successfully. Outside a build, the new name was searchable after 2.1 s. In source, IndexingJobs.IndexDocumentsAsync (the event path) calls the indexing manager directly, while manual builds, documentIds builds and the recurring IndexChangesJob go through RunIndexJobAsync and its lock.

On vcst_qa (theme 2.60.0-alpha.2579, SalesRep 3.1014.0-pr-13-4fbb), a rep created a list on /account/lists, opened Actions > Share, chose "Specific customers", picked one served organization and clicked Share; the card then read "Shared with 1 customer". A Purchasing agent and an Organization maintainer of that organization each got a push message "Hi! I've just shared the list "<name>" with your organization. Take a look:" with the /shared-list/<key> link (header bell and /account/notifications). Opening it as that buyer shows the list heading and the note "Recommended by your sales representative"; that note comes from the plugin chunk wishlist-rep-provenance-*.js under /modules/$(VirtoCommerce.SalesRep)/plugins/vc-frontend/assets/. List names are capped at 25 characters ("This field must not contain more than 25 characters").

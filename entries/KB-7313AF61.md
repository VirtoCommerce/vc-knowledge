Variation names are NOT in the storefront search index (searching "Light Brown Clemson Hat" returns no results), and variations do NOT appear as their own cards in their category listing (Configurable caps & shirts shows 6 results, all masters/configurables/simples, none of the hat's variations). What DOES work is the variation's own SEO slug, e.g. /products-with-options/configurable-caps-shirts/brown-vt-print-hat, which shows a real price ($8.99), real stock (3249) and a working quantity stepper. An ORDER line for a variation links back to the MASTER's slug (/products-with-options/configurable-caps-shirts/hat), which is a reliable way to tell a variation line from a simple-product line on an order.

Signed in on the storefront as a plain Sales Representative (store B2B-store), the account sidebar's "Sales Rep hub" section lists Reporting as the 2nd item (after Dashboard, before My customers); its link href is /company/customer-sales, which renders the Reporting page (title "Reporting", a "Sales statistics" card, period tabs with "Current year" pressed, customer select "All my customers (N)"). Opening /company/reporting as the same rep renders "404 Page not found". Source agrees: the plugin's constants.ts sets REPORTING_ROUTE_SEGMENT = "customer-sales" with the comment that the host owns /company/reporting (route split by VCST-6668). The ticket text (VCST-6255) still names /company/reporting, so a test written from the ticket targets the wrong route.

Not necessarily. On /products-with-options/configurable-caps-shirts/hat ("Men's Adjustable Scholarship Hat Team Color", advertised as 6 variations, From $4.00) the PDP renders four variant groups — Color, size_shirts, Size_chart, Fabric (data-test-id="variant-picker--<group>--<value>"). Picking a Color alone re-renders the H1 to a concrete variation name ("Light Brown Clemson Hat"), proving the picker does resolve a product, yet the Price stays "N/A" and the add control stays a DISABLED button whose accessible name is "Select options to proceed" — and it still does after one value is selected in ALL FOUR groups (Brown / 55 / No size / Cotton). No option is ever disabled or greyed as unavailable, so the UI gives no way to tell a valid combination from an invalid one; the offered matrix is 6x4x5x4 = 480 combinations against only 6 real variations. Two further traps for anyone trying to reach a variation another way: variation names are NOT in the storefront search index (searching "Light Brown Clemson Hat" returns no results), and variations do NOT appear as their own cards in their category listing (Configurable caps & shirts shows 6 results, all masters/configurables/simples, none of the hat's variations). What DOES work is the variation's own SEO slug, e.g. /products-with-options/configurable-caps-shirts/brown-vt-print-hat, which shows a real price ($8.99), real stock (3249) and a working quantity stepper. Note the resulting ORDER line links back to the MASTER's slug (/products-with-options/configurable-caps-shirts/hat), which is a reliable way to tell a variation line from a simple-product line on an order.

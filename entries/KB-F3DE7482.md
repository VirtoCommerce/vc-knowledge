With Deutsch chosen in the language switcher on the B2B-store storefront, navigating directly to /en/ becomes /de/en/ and shows '404 Seite nicht gefunden'; /en/accessories becomes /de/en/accessories and /en/search?q=laptop becomes /de/en/search?q=laptop, all 404, language stays DE (prefix stacking instead of a locale switch). With no non-default locale active, /en/destinations also returned '404 Page not found'. /de/<slug> works (/de/accessories renders the category) and a non-prefixed /destinations redirects to /de/destinations when DE is active; /de/accessories/ and /de//accessories normalise to /de/accessories, /de%2Faccessories gives a graceful 404.

On a vc-frontend 2.59 build (theme PR 2458), signed in as B2B org members, every full page load of /search?q=<unique term> (typed URL, or the return after sign-in) sent view_search_results twice with the same search_term: first as the _s=1 hit of the load (carrying up.* user properties), then again ~5 s later batched with page_view and the results-grid view_item_list (_s=2 or _s=3). Seen 6 of 6 times across 5 accounts. An in-app header search (SPA route change) sends it once, together with search. So GA counts of view_search_results (and anything built on them, such as a last-search or search-count readout) count every directly opened search page twice.

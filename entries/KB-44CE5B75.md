The PickupLocation model in vc-module-shipping has StoreId and IsActive properties (PickupLocation.cs lines 12 and 14). Each location therefore belongs to one store, and the count of active locations that the storefront pickup list pages through is per store. A test that needs an exact count of active locations (50/51/100/101) can get it on a dedicated store that owns exactly those locations. It does not need to deactivate locations of the shared store. Read in source; on vcst_qa B2B-store held 115 active locations (KB-50A4CB1D).

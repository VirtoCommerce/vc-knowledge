On a stand with Catalog.Search.EventBasedIndexation.Enable=false, deleting products via DELETE /api/catalog/products left all their Product index documents in place (ElasticSearch8): admin POST /api/catalog/search/products counted them in totalCount while items held only live products, and xAPI Query.products term facets counted them too (a GTIN held by 3 live products showed bucket 6). POST /api/search/indexes/index with [{documentType:'Product', documentIds:[deleted ids]}] cannot fix this: the Search module treats explicit ids as Modified changes, a missing product builds no document, and nothing is removed. POST /api/search/indexes/index with [{documentType:'Product', startDate, endDate}] and NO documentIds replays the catalog change feed for that window, and the Deleted CatalogProduct operation-log entries in it become index removals: 14 ghost documents (12 products + 2 variations) disappeared within ~10 s for a 6-second window around their deletion time. The deletion times come from POST /api/platform/changelog/v2/search {objectType:'CatalogProduct', operationTypes:['Deleted'], objectIds:[...]}; the change log records deletions regardless of the event-indexation setting. Admin search with objectIds:[...] and searchInVariations:true returns totalCount = how many of those ids still have an index document, which is a cheap check that the purge worked. Keep the window narrow: everything else modified inside it is re-indexed as well.

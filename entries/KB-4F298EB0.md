Observed on vcst with the vc-frontend barcode build: after switching the header currency to one with no product price list (EUR on this deployment), /search?barcode=<a GTIN three priced-in-USD products share> sent filter 'category.subtree:<catalog> price.EUR:(0 TO) barcode:"<v>"' and rendered 'No products found for barcode <v>'; in USD the same URL listed 3 products. /search?q=<same code> in EUR also returned no results, so the price filter applies to both routes; the barcode heading does not say the product exists but is unpriced.

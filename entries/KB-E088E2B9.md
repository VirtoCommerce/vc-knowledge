Renewals made due by nextOrderDate and processed via POST renewals/{id}/process (+ retry-payment). Hard decline (card 0021, attempt 1 HardDecline, CARD_DECLINED): renewal Failed, order status New, its PaymentIn status/paymentStatus Declined. Soft decline (card 9995) until RETRIES_EXHAUSTED after 4 attempts: renewal Failed, order status New, PaymentIn status/paymentStatus New, no transactions; the saved card stays Active. Seen on two renewals (16:38 and a fresh one at 18:28 UTC). Source (feat branch): the hard-decline branch of RenewalProcessor charge outcome sets payment.PaymentStatus = Declined and saves the order; OnSoftDeclineAsync -> FailAsync changes only the renewal and the subscription (PastDue), never the order payment. The order is not cancelled on either path.

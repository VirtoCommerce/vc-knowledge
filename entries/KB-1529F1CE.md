Opened the Designer image-control picker from an Image carousel section (images, multiple:true) and picked 1, 3 and 5 tiles across two folders: a span with aria-live=polite at the left of the pager row read "1 selected"/"3 selected"/"5 selected" and was empty (0x0) at 0; the Select button text stayed "Select" with the same 104x46 box at every count and was disabled at 0. Count and paginator were vertically centred (delta 0) at 1920 and 768 widths. At 390 width, going from 0 to 1 pick grew the pager row from 89.8 to 141.8px (moving it up 52px and shrinking the scrollable grid area), the paginator overflowed the row by ~15px, and its range label "1 – 3 of 3" wrapped one character per line (12.6px wide). From an Image section (single-select) the span stayed empty at every point and tiles had no selection marker. Selected tiles show a 24px round marker (primary background, white ring) with a centred white check glyph.

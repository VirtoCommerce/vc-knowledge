For a store, a product's stock is read from every fulfillment center assigned to the store - store.mainFulfillmentCenterId and each of additionalFulfillmentCenterIds. Stock on a fulfillment center assigned to neither leaves the product at zero for that store. Observed live 2026-10-08 on vcst_qa and vcptcore_qa1 with three fresh products, each with one enabled inventory record: 3 on the main center -> isInStock true, availableQuantity 3; 2 on an additional center -> isInStock true, availableQuantity 2; 4 on an unassigned center -> isInStock false, availableQuantity 0 and an empty inventories list. Anonymous xAPI product(id, storeId), xAPI products search and UCP search_products agree, from the first read about 10 s after creation.

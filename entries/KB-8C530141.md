Stock counts for a store only on the store's own main fulfillment center; stock written to another fulfillment center leaves the product at zero for that store. Disputed by a later observation: a product whose only inventory record (inStockQuantity 2, reserved 0, Enabled) sits on a fulfillment center that is in the store's additionalFulfillmentCenterIds but is not store.mainFulfillmentCenterId was returned by UCP search_products with availability.available_quantity 2 and is_in_stock true. So 'only the main fulfillment center counts' does not hold as stated; the zero-stock case is more likely stock on a fulfillment center that is not assigned to the store at all, neither main nor additional.

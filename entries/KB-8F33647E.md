On UCP 3.1007.0-pr-9-91ca an uppercase product_id on its own is not resolved: create_cart returns ucp.status success, empty line_items and a CART_PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE message. Duplicate grouping ignores case and keeps the FIRST entry's spelling: [lowercase 4, UPPERCASE 5] is refused as insufficient_stock requested 9, but [UPPERCASE 4, lowercase 5] and even the in-stock [UPPERCASE 2, lowercase 3] return success with an EMPTY cart and CART_PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE, so the valid lowercase entry is lost. The outcome depends on entry order.

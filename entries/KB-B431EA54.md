Signed in to /apps/admin-shell/ as a non-administrator holding only order:access and order:read (no admin-shell:access). GET /api/platform/security/currentuser returned canAccessAdminUI=true; POST /api/admin-shell/menu returned 200 with sections dashboard, orders, insights (Insights via the Reports item, which has no permission); the shell rendered rail Home/Orders/Insights and Orders was usable. The platform policy (VirtoCommerce:PlatformUI:Access, ConfigurationAdminUIAccessPolicy) defaults to AllowAdministrators=true and RequireAnyPermission=true with empty allow/deny lists, so such a user is admitted by design unless the stand configures the policy. A refused user was not tried on this stand.

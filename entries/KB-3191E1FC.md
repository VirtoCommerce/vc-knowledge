On 2026-11-11, signed in on the storefront of store B2B-store as a buyer with no organization who had 4 orders in November 2026: three in store B2B-store and one created by admin in store Electronics. /account/orders listed all 4 November orders (total $843.09). On /account/dashboard, the stat card Orders placed · MTD and the Sales Statistics month-to-date tile both showed 3 orders / $509.76. Query.orderStatistics(storeId: B2B-store) also returned 3 / 509.76, and the same query without storeId returned 4 / 843.09. Conclusion: the dashboard statistics are store-scoped by the storeId argument the storefront sends, while the order history is not. A case that compares the dashboard total with the order history must use orders from a single store.

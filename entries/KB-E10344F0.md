Admin signed in, Tasks app (/apps/tasks) > All tasks > Create, opened the Assignee picker and typed a keyword. The picker sent POST /api/task-management/members/search with {keyword, take:20, skip:0, memberTypes:["Contact","Employee"], deepSearch:true}. The 200 response carried the matching contact with securityAccounts[], and each account had passwordHash (an ASP.NET Identity v3 value) and securityStamp populated, plus concurrencyStamp, roles and lockout fields. After an assignee was selected, the blade sent GET /api/task-management/members/{id}, which returned the same securityAccounts with passwordHash and securityStamp populated. The stand runs with Authorization:ReturnPasswordHash = false. The developer guide (appsettings.json > Authorization) says that with false the hash is not returned, and the platform's own security endpoints blank it (SecurityController.ReduceUserDetails). Any role that can open the Tasks app's member search can therefore read password hashes of customer contacts. This is the same leak as KB-BCA7468D (GET /api/members/{id}), reached through the Task Management module's own endpoints. Do not paste these payloads into reports or snapshots.

All with pickup shipping, renewal made due by nextOrderDate, scheduler/run -> Notified (reminder, notifiedTotal 31.50), then POST renewals/{id}/process. Card 0021: order placed, attempt 1 HardDecline (raw 05, advice 21) -> Failed, lastErrorCode CARD_DECLINED, no retry date; subscription PastDue; saved card status NeedsUpdate (and it is no longer offered at checkout). Card 0069 cannot become a saved card: checkout payment is declined 'The payment was declined. Try another card.' and nothing is saved. Card 3184: attempt 1 RequiresAction -> PaymentActionRequired with nextProcessingDate +7 days; storefront subscription page shows 'Needs your attention / Confirm payment', which opens /account/orders/{orderId}/payment?card={cardId}; Pay -> simulated 3DS dialog -> Approve -> renewal 'Payment completed by the customer' -> Authorized. Price: list price raised 35 -> 40 after Notified; process -> ReviewRequired, reviewReasonCode PRICE_INCREASED 'The total went up from 31.50 to 36.00 USD', no order or payment created; POST renewals/{id}/accept -> order at 36.00, attempt 1 Succeeded -> Authorized. Each event writes a journal row (RenewalReminder, RenewalPaymentFailed, RenewalPaymentActionRequired, RenewalReviewRequired), but on this build every one is in Error with empty subject/body.

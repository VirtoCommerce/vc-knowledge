At 375px and 768px the 'Configure the parameters' option rows (option-product.vue, VcProductCard view-mode=item) render 'Price per item $X' and a bare 'Total' label; the amount element .vc-product-total .vc-product-price is present in the DOM with the value but computed display:none. At 1920px the amount shows. Source cause, identical at vc-frontend tags 2.51.2 and 2.59.0 and on dev: vc-product-price.vue hides '.vc-product-card--view-mode--item > .vc-product-card__wrapper .vc-product-price' below the 4xl container, and the intended override '.vc-product-total #{$wrapperSelector}' compiles to '.vc-product-card--view-mode--item .vc-product-total > .vc-product-card__wrapper .vc-product-price', which never matches because the total sits inside the wrapper, not around it. Not a regression of 2.59.

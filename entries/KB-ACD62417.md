Cold-loading /company/dashboard as an org buyer without sales-rep:access ended on /account/dashboard (silent redirect, no error), with no Sales Rep hub section in the rail. As an anonymous visitor the same URL ended on /sign-in?returnUrl=/company/dashboard, and after a password sign-in as an Advanced rep in the same tab it returned to /company/dashboard with the hub (Dashboard, My customers, Tasks, Document library) rendered. An org buyer saw the Corporate "Sales reps" link and /company/sales-reps listing the reps of the active organization (5 rows for one test org). A rep who logged out and signed in as a buyer in the same tab lost the hub section with no reload. First paint of a cold deep link is blank for about 1-3 seconds while the plugin boots.

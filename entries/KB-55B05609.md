Signed in as a contact with 11 approved organizations whose names are hyphenated (AGENT-TEST-Org-<Name>-<date>), opened the account-menu org switcher. Typing "Fill-Fil" sent GetOrganizations with searchPhrase "\"Fill-Fil\"" (the theme wraps the phrase in quotes) and the response held totalCount 11 — no filtering at all; the list showed all 11. Typing on to "Fill-Fillips" returned only the matching org plus the pinned active org. Typing "&'<script>alert(1)</script>" returned no orgs with a 'No results found' status, no script execution and no console error. Prefix matching on a single word works (see KB-94364409); the hyphen+partial-word combination degrades to an unfiltered list.

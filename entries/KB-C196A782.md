On vcst_qa (platform 3.1088.0-pr-3133, module VirtoCommerce.MFA 3.1000.0-pr-1-41b3) I sent POST /connect/token grant_type=mfa with two_factor_token=bogus, code=000000 and storeId: HTTP 400, error=invalid_grant, code=invalid_two_factor_token ("The sign-in session is invalid or has expired. Sign in again."), body carries userId null and lockoutSecondsRemaining null. POST /api/mfa/resend with a bogus token answers HTTP 200 with succeeded=false and error.code=invalid_two_factor_token (the failure is in the body, not the status); POST /api/mfa/signin/send anonymously with no Backoffice two-factor session answers the same 200 shape. GET /.well-known/openid-configuration lists grant_types_supported including mfa and otp_email on this stand; on the stand without the module the same bogus mfa grant answers 400 unsupported_grant_type, and /api/mfa/resend and /api/mfa/signin/send answer 404. No account was touched and no email was sent; the real two_factor_required challenge was not observable because MultiFactorAuthentication.Enabled was off.

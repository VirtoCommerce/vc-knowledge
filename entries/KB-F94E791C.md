Read in source, vc-frontend 2.63.0: client-app/shared/account/components/dashboard/recent-orders-widget.vue imports getOrders from @/core/api/graphql/orders and never getOrganizationOrders, while the Orders page composable useUserOrders switches between getOrders (scope private) and getOrganizationOrders (scope organization). So the widget is the user's own orders (Query.orders). Consistent with a live read on a B2B-store stand: a freshly created Purchasing agent of an organization that has orders got Query.orders totalCount 0 on an organization-scoped token. Consequence: a no-orders dashboard case needs a member who never ordered, not an organization without orders; organization-level figures elsewhere on the page may still be non-zero.

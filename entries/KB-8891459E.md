Guest, Chrome via DevTools, 19 compare entries over 8 category tabs. Tab "Configurable Parents" (document height 1685 px) with the viewport emulated to 1280x1385 (page 300 px taller than the viewport). Click the page heading, press PageDown once. scrollY stops at ~240-262 and the compare header row's class compare-table__header-row--stuck starts toggling continuously (a read-only MutationObserver counted 500 toggles every ~2 s, row top fixed at 128 = app header height, docH unchanged). From that moment Runtime.evaluate and Page.captureScreenshot time out (main thread never yields), and the renderer's private memory grows from ~1.7 GB to ~2.7 GB in 8 s; on the PR build of the compare-table restructure it reached 5.4 GB before the tab was closed. Reproduced the same way on a plain-products-only tab (5 entries, 0 configured, 1280x1358, docH 1658), so configured entries are not required. Negative controls on the same page: 1280x900 PageDown lands at scrollY 641 and compacts once, stable; tab with docH 1386 at 900/1050/1100 px tall also compacts once and stays stable. Clearing and restoring the list 6 times did not grow the heap (post-GC 28.9 -> 28.7 MB), so the growth is driven by the scroll-threshold flip, not by list edits. Same behaviour on the compare-table restructure build, so it is pre-existing and not introduced by that change.

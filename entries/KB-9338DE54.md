Anonymous guest on the vc-frontend storefront, B2B-store, en-US/USD. /search?q=<sku> issued SearchProducts (Query.products) with query=<sku> and filter 'category.subtree:<catalog root> price.USD:(0 TO) inStock_variations:true'. A fixture product, minutes after its Product index rebuild, came back totalCount 0 with no errors[] for both its SKU and its name. Its product page meanwhile rendered normally (list price shown, 'In stock 25', Add to Compare available). On its category page the product was missing while 'Show in stock' was on and appeared once that chip was removed (6 results became 7), so the document was in the index but excluded by the in-stock filter. About 3 minutes later the same /search query returned the product (totalCount 1) with no data change by the tester, so the index's stock flag lagged the rebuild. Takeaway: a search miss on a product whose PDP shows stock means the indexed inStock_variations flag, not the index itself; wait and re-check before calling a fixture unsearchable.

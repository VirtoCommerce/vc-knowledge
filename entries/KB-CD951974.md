Tested as a guest at 1920px in Chromium on /products-with-options/cfg-parents/agent-test-config-phone-case. Dark mode was reached by clicking the header 'Theme: auto' toggle once (html.dark). axe-core 4.10.2 color-contrast flags div[data-test-id=section-subtitle].product-configuration__value--selected, the green text showing the selected option under the CASE STYLE section header, at #3b7754 on #0a090b = 3.74:1, below the 4.5:1 that WCAG 1.4.3 requires for normal text. Theme builds 2.60.0-pr-2511-0a23 and 2.60.0-pr-2574-24c9 give the same values. The PR-2574 SKU-colour fix does not touch it. The same #3b7754 on #0a090b pair occurs elsewhere in dark mode.

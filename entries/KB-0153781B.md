Navigating to /account/login on the vc-frontend storefront returns a 404 (Page not found). The actual sign-in page is served at the single-segment route /sign-in, and renders Email/Password fields plus Entra ID and Google SSO buttons. /account/login is a plausible but wrong guess for this route.

On a platform customer address, countryCode is ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 (USA, CAN, GBR), not alpha-2, and countryName for the US is 'United States of America'. regionId and regionName are populated for US states and Canadian provinces and are null for other countries, the United Kingdom included. zip is always null - the postal code lives in postalCode - and only one address per member can be isDefault. isFavorite is not on the platform DTO; it exists only in the storefront xAPI projection and is flipped by the star icon. The address City facet reads the city field verbatim, so a wrong facet value usually means wrong address data.

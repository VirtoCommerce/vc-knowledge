Signed in as a sales rep on /company/tasks at 1440x1080 (Edge). The complementary landmark 'Due dates' has a bounding box 280px wide (inner content 236px). Completed rows render the title as a blue link-coloured button with line-through, the same hue as open titles (not grey). Status chips: Completed = light-green fill + green border + dark dot; Overdue = light-red fill + red border + dark dot; Upcoming = white background + blue border + crescent glyph; Canceled = white background + grey border + dark dot. Rows alternate white/light-grey backgrounds. Observed via accessibility-snapshot boxes and screenshots, not computed styles.

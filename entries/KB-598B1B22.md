2026-11-11, images vc-mcp-onx 1.0.0-alpha.23 (zod 4.1.12) and 1.0.0-alpha.28 (zod 4.6.7, mcp-onx PR #7) run side by side over HTTP (POST /mcp, JSON-RPC) with a platform bearer token. tools/call get-orders createdAtMin and get-products updatedAtMin: 'YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ssZ' and 'YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.SSSZ' succeed on both; 'YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mmZ' (no seconds) succeeds on alpha.23 and fails on alpha.28 with 'MCP error 2001: Validation failed for field createdAtMin: must match pattern ...'. The tools/list inputSchema advertises the pattern (format date-time) on both; on alpha.28 the seconds group is mandatory. Only 'Z' is accepted as offset. The published npm cof-mcp 0.5.2-alpha.2 (stdio) also accepts the no-seconds form.

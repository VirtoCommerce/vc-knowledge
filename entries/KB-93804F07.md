On a decided (Approved / PartiallyApproved / Rejected) return, PUT /api/return changing approvedQuantity, itemState or the reject/decline reason returns 200 and the stored decision is unchanged - those fields are discarded without an error, while an ordinary field such as a line price is saved. Changing a decided line's QUANTITY returns 400 "Line '<id>' has been approved or declined, so its quantity cannot be changed."; adding or removing a line returns 400 "Return '<number>' has been approved or declined, so its lines cannot be added or removed."; moving to Approved / Requested / Cancelled returns 400 "Status '<current>' cannot be changed to '<target>' by an edit.". On a Requested return a PUT that raises a line quantity within the order's availability returns 200 and changes the buyer's requested quantity (the line's available quantity drops accordingly); above it, 400 'LineItem ... has incorrect quantity'. Moving a decided return to Processing and then Completed keeps the quantities its decision released. Observed live 2026-10-08 on Return 3.1004.0 on vcptcore_qa1 and vcst_qa.

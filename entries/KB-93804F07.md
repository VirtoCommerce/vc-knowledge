On a decided return, PUT /api/return changing approvedQuantity, itemState, line quantity or decline reasons returns 200 and the stored decision is unchanged (the fields are discarded without an error); moving to Approved/Requested/Cancelled or adding/removing a line returns 400 with a message. On a Requested return a PUT that raises a line quantity within the order's availability returns 200 and changes the buyer's requested quantity with no notification; above availability it is 400 'LineItem ... has incorrect quantity'. Moving a decided return to Processing and then Completed keeps the released quantities.

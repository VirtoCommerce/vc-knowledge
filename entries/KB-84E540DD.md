The Promotion model has no coupons collection, only a hasCoupons flag. An inline coupons array in a promotion create or update body is silently dropped - the call returns success and hasCoupons stays false. Coupons are a separate entity: add them with POST /api/marketing/promotions/coupons/add (an array body, each coupon naming its promotion), and manage them with coupons/search, coupons/{id}, coupons/delete and coupons/import. Usage limits live on the coupon as maxUsesNumber and maxUsesPerUser.

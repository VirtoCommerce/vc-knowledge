Guest, Firefox 156 and Chromium, 1280x900, theme 2.59.0 (footer 'Ver. 2.59.0'). On /sign-in, clicking Sign in with both fields empty shows 'This field is required' under Email and under Password; the Sign in button moves from top 513 to 545 (32 px; the layout runner measures topDelta=32 three runs out of three) and the vertically centred 'Sign in with Entra ID' button moves 16 px. Typing 'not-an-email' into Email shows 'Enter a valid email address, e.g. johndoe@gmail.com' as you type, before any submit, already pushing the form 16 px; submit then adds the Password message. On /sign-up, typing an invalid email moves Password, Confirm password, the tips box and Sign up down 16 px. Validation is client-side: no /connect/token request is sent. Source at tag 2.59.0: VcInputDetails renders mt-1 + min-h-3 (16 px) and, unless show-empty is set, hides itself with empty:hidden; VcInput passes showEmptyDetails (default false) to it, and sign-in-form.vue and sign-up.vue do not set show-empty-details, so the row collapses until a message exists. The kit offers the reservation as an opt-in; quantity/add-to-cart controls use it, these forms do not. No VirtoOZ page requires reserved space.

On /cart a signed-in shopper typed 999 into a line's quantity field for a product with 69 in stock. Mutation.changeCartItemsQuantity (ChangeFullCartItemsQuantity) saved quantity 999 (line total recalculated), returning cart validationErrors(ruleSet '*') [PRODUCT_QTY_CHANGED, PRODUCT_QTY_INSUFFICIENT] and line error PRODUCT_QTY_CHANGED. The line shows 'You can order maximum 69 item(s)', the increase button is disabled, the summary shows 'Something went wrong. Please try again later.', and Place order stays disabled even with address, delivery and payment filled. Setting the quantity back to 1 re-enables Place order.

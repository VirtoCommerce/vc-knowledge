B2B-store had 115 of 123 pickup locations active. 65 sequential full-object PUT /api/shipping/pickup-locations calls set isActive=false, leaving 50 active per POST /api/shipping/pickup-locations/search. GET /api/search/indexes showed PickupLocation lastIndexationDate at the moment of the last PUT, yet GetCartPickupLocations returned totalCount 58 (6 of the 50 page-1 items were inactive in the DB) and still 58 about 10 minutes later. A POST /api/search/indexes/index [{documentType:PickupLocation}] (no delete) brought it to totalCount 50 within about a minute. Later toggles of 14-65 locations, each followed by the same targeted reindex, gave exact counts (51, 100, 101, back to 115). So the cart pickup list reads the search index, and event-based indexing of a burst of updates can leave some documents stale. Data prep that changes isActive should reindex PickupLocation before relying on the storefront count.

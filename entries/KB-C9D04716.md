On vcst_qa on 2026-10-29 (reproduced 3 times on 2026-10-28..29, two different operators), an operator with loginOnBehalf (role Organization maintainer, @td(IMPERSONATION_ADMIN)) signed in on the storefront and opened /account/impersonate/{userId} for @td(IMPERSONATE_TARGET_BLOCKED): a customer whose account is globally locked (lockoutEnd 9999-12-31, GET /api/platform/security/users/{id}/locked returns locked=true) and whose only organization membership is also locked. The storefront sent POST /connect/token grant_type=impersonate&scope=offline_access&user_id=<target> and got 200 with a 30-minute access token and a refresh token: sub = the locked user, operator claims set, no organization_id claim. The storefront then went to /blocked ('Your account is blocked'; the header shows 'Sign in'), and /account/dashboard redirected to /sign-in?returnUrl=/account/dashboard, so the operator's own session was gone too. VirtoOZ (Log in on Behalf, Issue and Use Access Token) says nothing about locked targets. Odd, but not marked as a defect: the password grant refuses a locked account, and in source, the deployed Customer OrganizationIdRequestValidator checks the global lock only after an organization resolves, which does not happen when every membership is locked. vc-platform PR #3144 (open) adds an IsLockedOutAsync refusal on the target to the impersonate grant. Before VCST-5028 this grant also returned 200; between 2026-07 and 2026-08 it returned 400.

isActive on the ProductConfiguration is the switch, it is honoured immediately, and NO REINDEX is involved. Saving the configuration with isActive false through POST /api/catalog/products/configurations flipped the storefront's Product.isConfigurable from true to false on the very next query, and turning it on had flipped it to true the same way seconds after the configuration was first created -- so isConfigurable is computed from the configuration entity at resolve time, not baked into the search index document, and waiting for or triggering a reindex to make a configurability change visible is wasted work. The product's own index entry is untouched by any of this: price, buyability and stock are unchanged, and the Admin product blade's index timestamp does not move.

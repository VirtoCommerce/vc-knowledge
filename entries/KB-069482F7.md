On vcst, a security role created with PUT /api/platform/security/roles (permissions storefront:organization:view, storefront:user:view copied from Organization employee, plus xapi:my_organization:mfa:manage) was assigned on POST /api/customer/organization-memberships and read back on the membership; a second member of the same organization kept org-employee. The PUT accepted storefront:* permissions although GET /api/platform/security/permissions does not list any storefront:* permission (xapi:my_organization:mfa:manage, mfa:read and mfa:manage are listed). A permission check against that list must therefore skip permissions copied from an existing role.

The storefront robots.txt is store content, not part of the frontend code, and changing it needs no redeploy: in Admin, open the store, use its Assets widget and upload a custom robots.txt, which overrides the system-generated one; separate policies per domain mean separate stores. Editing robots.txt inside a theme through the Content module is the legacy storefront pattern and does not apply to the current Vue storefront. robots.txt crawl control is independent of the per-route robots meta tag.

Browser zone UTC+3, en-US, 13:46 local. Created a task of type Call for today 12:45 PM (createSalesRepTask dueDate 2026-10-27T09:45:00Z, one hour in the past). The row reads 'Due Oct 27, 2026, 12:45 PM · <customer> · Call' with status 'Upcoming'; it is absent from the Overdue tab; the Today chip went from 2 to 3 and Overdue stayed 10; the calendar cell reads '3 tasks. Marked: Upcoming'. SalesRepTaskCounts sends overdue/upcoming filters with today = local midnight (2026-10-26T21:00:00.000Z), so a task becomes Overdue only from the next local day. A seeded non-call task due 09:00Z today behaves the same. Whether a timed call should turn Overdue once its time passes is an open product question.

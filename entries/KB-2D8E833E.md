Signed in as a sales rep, remoteEntry.js under /modules/$(VirtoCommerce.SalesRep)/plugins/vc-frontend/ delayed 15 s client-side, cold load of /company/dashboard. At ~2.5 s the main area shows "Loading page…" (spinner) with the URL unchanged; by ~8.5 s it shows "This page is taking longer than usual to load. You can keep waiting or reload the page." with a "Reload page" button; at ~19 s the plugin has loaded and the Sales Rep hub rail, the dashboard and the localised Corporate "Sales reps" link render. No Not Found frame at any point. Separately, with the plugin un-delayed, cold deep links in fresh tabs to /company/dashboard, /company/my-customers, /company/my-customers/{orgId}, /company/customer-orders and /company/tasks (polled every 100 ms) never showed a Not Found frame for the rep; for an org buyer the same five URLs replaced to /account/dashboard within about 1.0–1.2 s with no Not Found frame and no salesRep* GraphQL operation, and Back from the redirected page left the site (no redirect loop).

You can keep waiting or reload the page." with a "Reload page" button; at ~19 s the plugin has loaded and the Sales Rep hub rail, the dashboard and the localised Corporate "Sales reps" link render. Separately, with the plugin un-delayed, cold deep links in fresh tabs to /company/dashboard, /company/my-customers, /company/my-customers/{orgId}, /company/customer-orders and /company/tasks (polled every 100 ms) never showed a Not Found frame for the rep; for an org buyer the same five URLs replaced to /account/dashboard within about 1.0–1.2 s with no Not Found frame and no salesRep* GraphQL operation, and Back from the redirected page left the site (no redirect loop).

On a cold load of the home page and of a product page, window.dataLayer holds one ['config', <GA4 id>, {...}] entry before any ecommerce event. Guest: {debugMode:true, currency:'USD', language:'en-US', user_id: undefined} - the user_id key is present with value undefined, and the /g/collect hit carries no uid parameter. Signed in: user_id is the platform user GUID, equal to pageContext.user.id from GetPageContext; currency and language match the store defaults (USD, en-US). On the category page the manual view_item_list (category_<slug>_page_1) sends items[] with item_brand (not brand) and no items_skus; filtering dataLayer by e.event finds nothing because entries are gtag argument arrays.

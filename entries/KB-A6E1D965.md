Opened hub URLs (/company/dashboard, /company/my-customers, /company/my-customers/{orgId}, /company/customer-orders, /company/tasks, /company/documents) cold in new tabs and polled the DOM every 50 ms. The redirect itself is correct: signed-in non-reps end on /account/dashboard, anonymous visitors on /sign-in?returnUrl=<hub url>, with 0 requests to /graphql/sales-rep. But in roughly a third to two thirds of loads, a frame of about 50-100 ms renders the host 404 page first, at about 1.0-1.3 s: h1 "404", "Page not found An error occurred. Should we take you back to home page?", document.title "QA & 404 Page not found". Measured: plain buyer 6/18, org maintainer 6/10, second maintainer 8/18, anonymous 6/9. A signed-in rep saw 0/6. Polling every 100 ms usually misses the frame. The likely mechanism is that the plugin's routes register after the first route resolution, so the catch-all matches first.

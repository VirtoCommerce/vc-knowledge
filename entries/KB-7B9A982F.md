Signed-in B2B user, one line seeded via xAPI addItem. On /cart, clicking 'Increase quantity' on the line sends operation ChangeFullCartItemsQuantity (root field changeCartItemsQuantity, command.cartItems[{lineItemId, quantity}]); the response is a CartType with errors absent; the spinbutton goes 1 -> 2, the line total becomes unit price x 2 and the order-summary Subtotal equals the sum of line totals. 'Decrease quantity' from 2 sends the same mutation with quantity 1; at qty 1 the '-' button is disabled, also after reload. On the PDP (/product/<id>) the buy-box '+' from 0 sends UpdateShortCartItemQuantity (root field updateCartQuantity, command.items[{productId, quantity}]), not addItem; a chip titled "in Cart" with the count appears next to the stepper and the header cart badge goes to 1. Observed repeatedly by Playwright specs CART-001/006/007 (3 green runs).

On vcst_qa (theme 2.60.0-alpha.2579, hub from the SalesRep plugin): a rep with the "Advanced Sales Representative" role (sales-rep:access + sales-rep-documents:read) sees "Document library" in the Sales Rep hub rail and as a dashboard widget; /company/documents lists documents with an Open button per row, and Open opens the file in a new tab (blob: URL). A rep with only "Sales Representative" (sales-rep:access) has no Document library link or widget, and a direct load of /company/documents ends on /account/dashboard.

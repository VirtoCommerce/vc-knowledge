Two multi-organization members (3 and 6 orgs) switched organization 10 times via the account menu Organizations listbox on a vc-frontend 2.59 build with theme PR 2458. Each option click sent POST /connect/token (200) and then the page fully reloaded (new gtag _p, navigation type reload). The first GA4 hit (_s=1 page_view) of the reloaded page carried up.organization_id/organization_name of the newly selected org every time; no hit after a switch carried the previous org. A second open tab of the same session also reloaded and picked up the new org, so a stale-identity tab was not observed. A search typed immediately after the switch landed on the reloaded page and was attributed to the new org. Side effect: if the switch happens on a product page, the reload re-sends view_item for that product (also in background tabs), so the product is counted as viewed by the new organization. Manual reload and a fresh tab keep the selected org (it is persisted server-side, also restored at next sign-in).

Signed-in B2B shopper, playwright Chromium, a fresh navigation per load from about:blank with CDP cache disabled, viewport resize only; I summed the layout-shift entries without recent input from a buffered PerformanceObserver ~5 s after load; 2 loads per cell, values identical across loads. Build footer: Ver. 2.59.0-pr-2458 (upstream vc-frontend). /search?q=<nonsense>: at 1920x1080 the CLS is 0.6442 and the largest shift is 0.6275 at ~1.1 s: div.vc-layout__content-container goes from 502,192 1158x888 to 251,192 1404x502 (the filter sidebar is removed), the footer enters at y=816, and the empty-state illustration svg (552x528) enters. At 375x812 the CLS is 0.8097 and the largest shift is 0.7503: div.category-products goes from 19,320 322x492 to 24,222 312x417, the svg enters at 72,439 288x288 and the footer enters at y=727. A search with hits (/search?q=shirt, 11 results) scores 0.0021 at 1920 and 0.0906 at 375, so the shift belongs to the zero-hit transition. The same measurement on vc-frontend-next 3.0.0-alpha.2685 (signed in) gives 0.475 at 1920 and 0.807-0.838 at 375, so the shift is inherited from upstream. The new theme does not amplify it: at 1920 it scores lower, because it has no illustration and a full-width container. Absolute values depend on the store layout, so the stands are compared by mechanism, not by exact number.

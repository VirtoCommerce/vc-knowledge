In the xAPI schema Product.availabilityData is NON_NULL, so a returned product always carries the four flags. A PerSku goal item whose product was deleted from the catalog after the goal item was created keeps its productId, and loyaltyMissionProgress returns that row with currentQuantity/targetQuantity and product: null. Deleting the product does not remove the goal item. An inactive product (isActive false) is NOT null: it is returned with availabilityData isActive, isAvailable, isBuyable and isInStock all false. Observed on Loyalty 3.1009.0, vcst, 2026-10-01.

Observed on vc-frontend PR #2524 build 2.59.0-pr-2524 (Firefox, 1920x1080): VcModal's body (.vc-dialog-content--scrollable) is overflow-y:auto with scrollbar-width:thin, box-shadow none and mask-image none; Firefox reserves 0 px for the overlay scrollbar, and the fixed footer (.vc-dialog-footer) is separated only by a 1 px divider. So the only cue that the body scrolls is a thin scrollbar that may not be shown. On /account/missions the SKU mission modal (sku-mission-modal.vue) renders the 'Totals and cart subtotal are estimates' VcAlert as the last child of that scroll body; with 5 SKU targets plus one quantity-error line the body overflows 34 px and the fold cuts through the alert (12 of 30 px visible). Nothing is covered: scrolling to the end shows the full alert with 16 px clearance.

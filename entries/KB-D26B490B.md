Account addresses (/account/addresses) and company addresses (/company/info, Add new address): the Line 1 and Line 2 inputs have maxlength 2048 (vc-frontend #2642). Saving Line 1 and Line 2 of about 300 characters each fails: updateMemberAddresses returns a VALIDATION error on addresses[0].name, MaximumLengthValidator, "The length of 'Name' must be 256 characters or fewer" (325 and 341 entered). The client builds the address name from country code, region, city and Line 1, so the cap applies to Line 1 indirectly; Line 2 is not part of it. The UI shows only the toast "Some of the addresses are not valid" and keeps the dialog open. Line 1 of 231 characters (derived name 256) with Line 2 of 300 saves and reads back 231/300. Reproduced 3 times on two accounts.

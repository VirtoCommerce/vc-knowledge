On vcst_qa (theme 2.63.0-pr-2676, Firefox), on / and /designer-preview?pageId=<draft page id> with html.dark: getComputedStyle of html, body and .main-layout give background-color rgba(0,0,0,0) and color-scheme dark, and no ancestor of the breadcrumb link has an opaque background. The pixel colour of the page area is #1c1b22, which is Firefox's default dark canvas, while the token --color-neutral-50 resolves to #17181c. Contrast snippets that assume a white canvas when no ancestor is opaque therefore report phantom failures for light text on this page (for example breadcrumb Home #8ed2eb reported at 1.67:1 against white; against #1c1b22 it is about 10:1). The page background colour depends on the browser.

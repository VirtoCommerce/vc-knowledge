Over REST the route is /api/customer/organization-memberships (hyphenated, plural); a membership is keyed by userId, the security-account user id rather than the contact id, and roles are an array of objects with roleId and roleName, not a flat roleIds list. Search by userId returns memberships with isLocked; there are lock, unlock, get, update and delete routes by membership id, plus per-user count and per-user-and-organization lookups. The GraphQL mutations changeOrganizationContactRole, lockOrganizationContact and unlockOrganizationContact take the contact's memberId and derive the organization from the caller's token, so the caller must be authenticated into the target organization.

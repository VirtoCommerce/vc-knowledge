Created a physical product in a catalog via Add > Physical product (POST /api/catalog/products 200), selected its row in 'Categories and items', clicked Delete. A 'Confirm delete' dialog appeared: permanent-change warning, '1 Item(s)', a 'Type yes to delete' textbox, Confirm (disabled until 'yes' typed) and Cancel. Confirm sent POST /api/catalog/listentries/delete -> 204; the grid refreshed without the row and GET /api/catalog/products/{id} then returned 404. The Pricing price-list grid uses the same type-'yes' dialog and sends DELETE /api/pricing/pricelists?ids={id} -> 204. The catalog grid itself loads by infinite scroll (POST /api/catalog/listentries with skip 0, take 50), not pagination controls.

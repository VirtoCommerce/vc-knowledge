In vc-module-order, CancelPaymentOrderChangedEventHandler enqueues a background job for every inPayment when an order goes from not cancelled to cancelled. In CancelPaymentAsync, an Authorized payment is passed to PaymentMethod.VoidProcessPaymentAsync, and a Paid payment to PaymentMethod.RefundProcessPaymentAsync (a RefundPaymentRequest with no amount set). Only payments in any other status get PaymentStatus Cancelled, IsCancelled true and CancelledDate set. In every case Status is then set to PaymentStatus.ToString() and CancelledState to Completed. So whether a Paid payment's money comes back depends entirely on the gateway's refund implementation and on its handling of a request with no amount: Skyflow's RefundProcessPaymentAsync is a success stub that makes no call; Datatrans rejects a refund amount of zero or less before calling the gateway. Either way the payment keeps status Paid and isCancelled false, while cancelledState reads Completed. A later PUT that sets isCancelled true on such a payment queues nothing, because the order is already cancelled and the payment's cancelledState is Completed, not Requested.

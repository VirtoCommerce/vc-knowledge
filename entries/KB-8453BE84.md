vc-frontend renders every useNotifications notice (success, warning, error) as a VcAlert inside a single region teleported to <body>: <section class="notifications-host" aria-live="polite">, with an aria-label from the common.labels.notifications translation. A notice is therefore announced politely when it is added; nothing marks it role=alert or assertive. Read in client-app/shared/notification/components/notifications-host.vue; not verified with a screen reader.

In the Admin SPA the Dashboard Editor's Rules tab lists every Home widget with a "Needs <permission>" line. The pencil on a widget opens its rule with three choices: Everyone with the permission, Hidden from everyone, Hidden from selected roles. With the last choice the role list shows checkboxes; roles that lack the widget's permission are greyed and disabled with "can't see it anyway". Applied rules show in the list as "Hidden from <role>" chips. Cancel leaves the rule unchanged and Save stays disabled until a change is applied. The rule is stored per role (PUT /api/admin-shell/dashboard/roles/{roleId}).

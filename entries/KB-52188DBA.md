Signed in as a sales rep, Edge 1920, waited 3s after load. On theme 2.59.0-pr-2536: /company/tasks, /company/dashboard and /company/my-customers all keep document.title 'Virto Commerce' (the h1 reads Tasks / Dashboard / My customers), whereas /company/customer-orders becomes 'QA & Customer orders' and /account/dashboard 'QA & Dashboard' ('QA' is the store name on this stand). The deployed 2.59.0 build shows the same for /company/calendar, /company/dashboard and /company/my-customers. In source those hub pages do not call usePageHead; customer-orders and customer-profile do. So the missing title predates the Tasks redesign and is shared by the other hub pages.

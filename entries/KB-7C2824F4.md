Signed in, Chromium, theme 2.61.0-pr-2598-9b6d-9b6db3d9 against the vcst_qa API, store B2B-store. On /account/lists/{id} every product row has a trailing X icon button (data-test-id remove-item-button) whose accessible name in the Playwright accessibility snapshot is 'Remove from cart', also on a row whose product is not in the cart. Clicking it opened a 'Confirm Delete' dialog asking whether to remove the product 'from the list'. Delete removed the line from the list, and the list showed 'Your list is empty'. So the control removes from the list while its accessible name says cart. Not checked against a pre-PR build, nor whether this is a shared line-item component label.

XCatalog 3.1022 (PR 113 build) with a store configured for exact barcode match on [code]: Query.products(filter: barcode:"<code>") for an active product that has no prices at all returns it (totalCount 1, price.actual 0, isBuyable false, no errors). The storefront's own products request for /search?barcode=<code> sends filter 'category.subtree:<catalogId> price.USD:(0 TO) barcode:"<code>"' and gets 0, so the shopper sees the barcode empty state. The same composed filter still finds a priced product (1). So 'unpriced is not found by scan' is produced by the price term the storefront adds, not by the barcode middleware; a direct xAPI caller that omits the price term will find unpriced products by barcode.

On vcst_qa (theme 2.60.0-alpha.2579, hub from the SalesRep plugin): Logout from the account menu on /company/dashboard (or /company/my-customers, /company/sales-reps) reloads the app on /sign-in?returnUrl=/company/dashboard (the hub path is carried). Signing in from that form as an Organization maintainer or a Purchasing agent ended on /account/dashboard, with no Sales Rep hub section in the menu and no request to /graphql/sales-rep; signing in as a rep ended on the returnUrl hub page. A different rep (Advanced) signing in on the same tab after a buyer saw its own rail (incl. Document library). Sign-out itself is a full page reload, not an in-SPA switch.

Admin Shell on vcst_qa (vc-shell framework v2.10.4, Firefox 156, 1440x900) on #/mcp-agents and #/mcp-audit. Cells of VcColumn type="date-ago" (Last used, Connected) render a span with a title attribute. Cells of type="datetime" (audit Date: span.vc-table-cell-date, white-space nowrap, 151 px of text in 99 px) and default text cells (User, Client) render no title on the cell or any ancestor; on the audit page 162 of 350 cells were truncated and none had a title. VcDataTable wraps every cell in a 2-line clamp by default (--line-clamp: 2); VcColumn accepts width, minWidth and lineClamp, so a module can avoid the cut per column. Source at vc-shell v2.10.4 agrees: cells/CellDateAgo.vue binds :title, cells/CellDate.vue and cells/CellDefault.vue only apply tw-truncate.

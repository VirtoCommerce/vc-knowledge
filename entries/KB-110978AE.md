The line-item component's container-query container is the .vc-line-items element (container-type inline-size); no other ancestor is a container. Measured widths on vcst_qa (Chromium): cart 1038px at 1920 viewport, 922px at 1366, 835px at 1280; account pages (wishlist /account/lists/{id}, quote /account/quotes/{id}/edit) 1086px at 1920, 970px at 1366, 883px at 1280. Above 896px the .vc-line-item__price column is visible (display flex) and .vc-line-item__property-price is display none; at 883/835px it flips (column display none, property slot display grid). On a wishlist item with listPrice 268.00 and salePrice 264.00 the visible price was 268.00 at 1920 and 1366 but 264.00 at 1280: the same item shows a different unit price depending on container width, because the column shows the list price when showPlacedPrice is not passed while the property slot shows actualPrice or listPrice.

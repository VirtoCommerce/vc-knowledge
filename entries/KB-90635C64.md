Yes. A rep holding the Sales Representative role composed with the Sales Rep Documents Manager role gets a token whose permission claim includes sales-rep:access, sales-rep-documents:read and sales-rep-documents:write (decoded 2026-10-05), and the storefront shows both the Document library dashboard widget and the Document library hub nav entry. The Documents Manager role as seeded carries read as well as write, so this composition cannot test a write-only rep; a write-only check needs a role with write and no read.

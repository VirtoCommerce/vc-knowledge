Nowhere. Wishlists live entirely in the XCart module's GraphQL schema - the resolver stack names VirtoCommerce.XCart.Data.Schemas.PurchaseSchema - and that module publishes an OpenAPI document with an empty paths object, so it contributes not one REST route. There is consequently no wishlist blade, no wishlist widget on the contact blade, and no platform endpoint to read a stored list from. Anyone trying to check a list 'from the platform side' is looking for a surface that does not exist: the only readers are Query.wishlist by list id, Query.wishlists by owner, and Query.sharedWishlist by sharing key, all of which authorize as a storefront principal. This also means a list cannot be audited or corrected by an administrator through the back office at all.

On a Rejected (decided) return the Line items blade renders Quantity as a disabled input, Approved and Decline reason as plain text, and Approve / decline disabled; the Price input stays editable and the Line items toolbar Save (enabled after the field blurs) persists the new price - GET /api/return/{id} shows the edited price while approvedQuantity, itemState and rejectReason are unchanged; the return-blade Save stays disabled.

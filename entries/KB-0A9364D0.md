On theme 2.60.0-pr-2523-466f-466fa0a8 (Firefox 151, Windows), /account/orders at a 375 px viewport renders order cards inside the VcTable mobile container, which computes word-break: break-word. The order-number link computes white-space: nowrap, overflow: hidden and text-overflow: ellipsis (12 px, weight 700), so it never wraps: 0 of 2 numbers on 'My orders' and 0 of 10 on 'All orders' took a second line; the long test-order numbers on the stand (36 to 54 characters, 252 to 347 px wide in a 103 to 151 px box) are cut with an ellipsis instead. The /account/returns cards differ: their RMA value has no nowrap and splits mid-token on Firefox up to 385 px (KB-986C1D33).

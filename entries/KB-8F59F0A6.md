Theme 2.63.0-pr-2602 (vc-frontend #2671), B2B-store. Signed-in: a one-time line of a product with a plan (PEPSI, LIPTON) shows an icon-only Save for later plus a 'Subscribe' button; clicking it opens the 'Purchase options' modal with Subscribe & Save checked and the plan's 'Get it every' tiles; Save sends Mutation.setCartItemSubscription and the line becomes 'Subscribed · Every <interval>' + Change + 'Next delivery around <date>'. Change reopens the modal; choosing One-time purchase + Save removes the subscription (line back to list price, 'Your subscription' terms block gone) and the Subscribe button returns. A product without a plan (OREO) shows no Subscribe. Guest: no Subscribe button on the line; on the PDP Subscribe & Save can be chosen but shows 'Sign in to subscribe.' and add-to-cart adds a one-time line. Source condition: isAuthenticated && offer && !subscription && !readonly.

POST /api/push-message with status Sent and memberIds [] (no memberQuery) returns 200 and the message keeps status Sent: GET /api/push-message/{id} still reads Sent after the recipients job window, and POST /api/push-message/search-recipients for it returns totalCount 0. It is not held back as Draft and nothing refuses it, so a Sent message with no audience exists and can never be edited or deleted. Observed on PushMessages 3.1005.0.

Created a fresh AGENT-TEST contact via POST /api/members without an organizations field, a Customer security account bound to it, and two rows via POST /api/customer/organization-memberships (TechFlow, BuildRight, role org-employee, no status). Signed in on the storefront with password: the account menu showed only the name and Logout, with no Organizations listbox. Querying xAPI me.contact with that user's token returned organizationId null and organizations.totalCount 0. So a membership row by itself does not link the contact to the organization; the contact also needs organizations set on the member record, which is what the working multi-org fixtures have.

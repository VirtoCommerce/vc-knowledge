2026-11-11 (day 11 of 30), store B2B-store, USD. GetMonthlyOrderStatistics returns the full last-year month (ly period from-to = whole Nov 2025). Org buyer with Nov 2026 $2,442.51 and Nov 2025 1 order $1,251.39: the Nov bar badge and the tooltip line 'vs Nov 2025' read +432.3% (= 2,442.51 / (1,251.39 x 11/30) - 1), while the same tooltip shows 'Revenue, Nov 2025 $1,251' - the base shown is not the base used. The Current year tiles use the same proration for counts: 4 orders vs 5 + 1 x 11/30 = 5.367 -> -25.5%, whereas the YTD stat card (GetOrderStatistics, last-year window Jan 1 - Nov 11 2025) shows -20%. Projection = month-to-date x 30/11; '36.7% of the month'.

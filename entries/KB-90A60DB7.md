Theme 2.63.0-pr-2602-6507ff26 replaced the subscription detail page with delivery cards on /account/subscriptions (deep link ?subscription=<id> scrolls to the card; the old /account/subscriptions/{id} URL now renders the 404 page). Frequency change (line Change -> Purchase options modal) sends changeProductSubscription {subscriptionId, intervalUnit, intervalCount}; the server sets nextOrderDate = lastOrderDate + new interval and keeps skipCount, so after skipping Dec 18 (-> Jan 18) a switch to every 2 weeks gave Dec 2, before the skipped date, with no warning. Change card sends only {subscriptionId, savedPaymentMethodId} and left nextOrderDate and skipCount unchanged from a stale tab. A stale Skip sends expectedNextOrderDate and is refused with "The next delivery has changed since this page was opened..." but the dialog shows "Something went wrong. Please try again." and stays open. The InputChangeProductSubscription schema still accepts nextOrderDate with no expected-date guard.

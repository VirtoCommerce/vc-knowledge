The Marketing module's Swagger (/docs/VirtoCommerce.Marketing/swagger.json and the PlatformUI aggregate) exposes no promotion-usage endpoint: only promotions, coupons/search, coupons/add, coupons/delete, coupons/import. A usage is written by the module's CouponUsageRecordHandler when an order is ADDED: it enqueues a background job that records one usage per distinct (promotionId, coupon) discount on the order. Observed: POST /api/order/customerOrders with items: [] and discounts: [{ promotionId, coupon: <code>, currency, discountAmount: 0 }] (no organization, a non-account customerId) raised the coupon's totalUsesCount in POST /api/marketing/promotions/coupons/search from 0 to 2 for two such orders within a few seconds, and xAPI salesRepCoupons then reported the same count (totalUsesCount 2, maxUsesNumber 5). A seeder that needs N recorded uses must create N orders and poll the coupon search until the count arrives.

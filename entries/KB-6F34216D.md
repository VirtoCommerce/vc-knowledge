Read in vc-module-customer PR 324 (OrganizationIdRequestValidator): IsOrganizationFallbackAllowed returns true when the grant is not refresh_token OR no organization_id was requested, replacing the earlier test SignInResult != null. So password, otp_email, mfa, passkey, impersonate, external and a refresh_token without organization_id fall back; only a refresh_token carrying organization_id keeps failing. When the validator falls back or clears the organization it also rewrites the organization claim on the principal; a rejected request is logged with failureReason NotAllowed; the global lockout check moves out of the customer module into the platform base handler. Not exercised live for the impersonate leg.

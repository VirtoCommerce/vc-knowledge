A quote created through POST /api/quote/requests in 'Proposal sent' with expirationDate three days in the past was approved by its buyer through the xAPI approveQuoteRequest mutation: the call returned an orderId, the quote became 'Ordered' and a CustomerOrder was placed. The handler checks only status == 'Proposal sent' (vc-module-quote ApproveQuoteCommandHandler), not expirationDate, and there is no 'Expired' quote status in Quotes.Status. So expiry is not enforced at the API; whether the storefront hides Approve for an expired quote is a separate, UI-level question.

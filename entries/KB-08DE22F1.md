Store B2B-store (default en-US). Product A (a bottle) has SEO records: slug S in en-US, S-de in de-DE, S-wt (inactive). Product B (a keg variation) has only slug S in en-US. Query.slugInfo(permalink "<category path>/S", cultureName en-US) returns product A; with cultureName de-DE it returns product B (languageCode en-US). On the storefront, opening A's PDP in English and switching to Deutsch keeps the path (/de/<path>/S) and renders product B's name in the H1. The storefront language switch does not use the de-DE slug S-de. A clash in the catalog data (two products, one slug) is enough to make the language switch land on another product.

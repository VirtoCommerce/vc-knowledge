B2B buyer (TechFlow), store B2B-store. A second session was minted by password grant at the storefront POST /connect/token at 06:41:55Z; the browser session then changed the password on /account/change-password at 06:42:16Z (success toast 'Your password has been successfully changed. Changing your password will sign you out of all other devices and browsers.'). Polling Query.me with the second token every 5 s until 06:46:58Z: it resolved the real userName on all 55 polls (never Anonymous), and Query.orders with it returned data with no errors[]. So on this stand the other session is NOT terminated, which matches Authorization:EnablePersistentStorageTokenValidation being off (the setting itself was not read). The browser (caller) session also stayed signed in. The toast's promise of signing out other devices does not hold on this stand configuration.

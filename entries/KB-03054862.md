Signed-in VIP loyalty customer, B2B-store. On /loyalty-catalog clicking '+' on a PTS-priced product card (Autoclave, PTS8) put it in the cart (header Cart: 1). /cart then rendered a level-4 heading 'Products in PTS' group with its own 'Select all items' checkbox, the line priced PTS8.00 and a group subtotal 'PTS 8.00'; the Order summary kept USD rows ($0.00) and added a 'Total in PTS' block (Subtotal PTS8.00, Discount PTS0.00). Adding a USD product from /alcoholic-drinks afterwards created a separate 'Vendor: N/A' group line in USD ($123.00); badge counted 2. The PTS add never merged into the USD line. Clear cart removed both groups (an Apollo 'cache data may be lost when replacing cartTotals' warning was logged).

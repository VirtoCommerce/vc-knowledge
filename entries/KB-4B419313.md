Signed in, with a line item in the cart and Bank card (CyberSource) selected, /cart renders #cardNumber-container and #securityCode-container each holding an iframe[title='secure payment field']; typed digits appear only inside the iframe frames, not in the storefront page DOM. On Place order the storefront POSTs a createToken to testflex.cybersource.com/flex/v2/tokens, then Mutation.authorizePayment with parameters = a single {key: token, value: JWT}; the JWT carries only a masked PAN (last 4), bin and expiry, securityCode empty. Response isSuccess true.

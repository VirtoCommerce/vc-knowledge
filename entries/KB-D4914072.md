On vcst_qa 2026-10-08, users/create for a Customer with a plain user name and no email returned succeeded:false with "Email '' is invalid.". Creating it with a placeholder email and then PUT /api/platform/security/users with email null left the email on the account. The return module resolves the buyer address as order address, then contact email, then account email, so a storefront buyer always has a last-resort address; "a buyer with no email anywhere" cannot be provisioned for a storefront return.

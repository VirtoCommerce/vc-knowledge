On vcst (VirtoCommerce.OTP 3.1002.0-pr-4-83d4, store OTP on) a disposable Customer account created with emailConfirmed=false and lockoutEnabled=true: POST /api/otp/request answered 200 succeeded=true and journaled a code; POST /connect/token grant_type=otp_email with it returned 200 with an access_token and the account stayed emailConfirmed=false. So an unconfirmed email is not an eligibility refusal for OTP sign-in. After the email was then confirmed, the same code returned 400 invalid_code (consumed) and raised accessFailedCount to 1.

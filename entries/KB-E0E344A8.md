On vcptcore (platform 3.1091.0-pr-3144-e637, VirtoCommerce.Customer 3.1032.0-pr-324-b30b, store B2B-store), a seeded multi-org buyer with Approved, unlocked memberships in org A (Organization maintainer, 11 permissions) and org B (Organization employee, 2 permissions) and contact.currentOrganizationId=null signed in with grant_type=password, storeId and organization_id set to (1) a freshly generated random GUID and (2) a real organization discovered via POST /api/members/search of which the user is not a member. Both returned HTTP 200 with no error; the decoded access token's organization_id claim was org A (11 permissions) in both cases, not the requested id. Explicit organization_id=A and =B controls returned their own org (11 and 2 permissions); no organization_id returned A. No 5xx and no DB identifiers in either response; no membership row was created (POST /api/customer/organization-memberships/search before/after identical). After the grants the contact's currentOrganizationId read back as A (it was null before), so a credential sign-in persists the resolved org. A caller cannot tell the substitution from a correct grant without decoding the JWT; requesting the low-privilege org with a wrong id yields the high-privilege org. Whether this is by design is contested: BL-AUTH-013/016 describe substitution on credential grants, while suite 027 CUST-112 specifies refusal.

Storefront /cart at a 375x812 viewport, scrolled to the very bottom. A fixed checkout bar (about 103 px tall, "Total: …" plus the main button) sits at the bottom of the viewport. With a password sign-in the button is Place order; in punchout mode it is Create requisition; the bar and the overlap are identical in both modes. The page height ends exactly at the footer bottom with no bottom padding for the bar, so the footer's last ~90 px — the legal strip with "Ver. … All rights reserved" and the platform link — stays under the bar at maximum scroll and can never be scrolled clear; only the footer logo part above it is visible. Not compared against a released theme (both observations on the same PR theme build).

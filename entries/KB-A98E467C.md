sendCustomerCommunication(organizationIds, title, message, sendPush, sendEmail, storeId, cultureName) returns SalesRepCommunicationResult {succeeded, pushSent, emailSent, warnings}; the result needs a sub-selection. A 1001-character message is rejected with errors 'Message must not exceed 1000 characters.' and data null, while the wishlist sharing message accepts up to 1024, so a stored share message of 1001-1024 characters cannot be forwarded as-is. A valid call returns succeeded, pushSent and emailSent true, and the recipient org member's pushMessages gets a new item whose shortMessage is the custom message text. A share sent from the storefront list dialog arrives as a push whose text is the message followed by the /shared-list/<key> link, while the older storefront client leaves sharingSetting.message null on the list.

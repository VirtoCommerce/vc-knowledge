An order created with languageCode de-DE, then a return created through xAPI createReturn with command.cultureName fr-FR and submitted: GET /api/return/{id} reports languageCode fr-FR, and the ReturnRegisteredEmailNotification journal row for it has languageCode fr-FR. The submission culture wins over the order language. CreateReturnCommandType carries cultureName; UpdateReturnCommandType and SubmitReturnCommandType do not. Observed on VirtoCommerce.Return 3.1003.0-pr-27-51fc.

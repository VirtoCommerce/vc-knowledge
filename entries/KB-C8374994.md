On /account/missions, the SKU modal of a per-SKU mission renders one quantity stepper per target. For a target with trackInventory=true and stock 5 (product maxQuantity 0), the input has max="5" and aria-valuemax="5", and Increase is disabled at 5. For targets with trackInventory=false (maxQuantity 0, availableQuantity 0), the input has no max attribute and no aria-valuemax, and Increase stays enabled (stepped 0->3 freely). If you type 7 into the tracked row and blur, the value 7 is kept (not clamped) and the input is marked invalid with the hint 'Order from 1 to 5 item(s)'. Increase is disabled, Decrease is enabled and Add to cart is disabled. The modal summary still counts the invalid 7 (Total units 7, Targets met 1/1, footer 'All targets met — 285 points unlocked', $84.00 estimate). The same behaviour was seen on theme 2.60.0 and on the 2.60.0-pr-2542 build.

Order summary before removal read Subtotal $10.00, Discount -$2.00, Tax +$1.60, Shipping cost $0.00, Total $9.60 (subtotal - discount + shipping + tax = total holds; the vendor-block Subtotal showed $8.00, the sale-price sum).

Anonymous loads of / (twice, cache-busted), /catalog, a category, a product page, /search, /brands and /cart on vc-frontend-next 3.3.0: the console logs CSP violations for the App Insights SDK config fetch (js.monitor.azure.com/scripts/b/ai.config.1.cfg.json) and its telemetry POST (dc.services.visualstudio.com/v2/track) under connect-src, and for ProductGuide's dynamic imports of micromark from cdn.jsdelivr.net under script-src (ProductGuide then logs a fallback warning to esm.sh). The response CSP header's connect-src lists neither App Insights host; script-src allows fastly.jsdelivr.net but not cdn.jsdelivr.net. Pages, GraphQL (no errors[]) and shopper flows are unaffected; what is lost is front-end telemetry. It is the stand's CSP config, not the theme.

Fresh sign-in as a sales rep on a vc-frontend-next 3.3.0-pr-22 build (host core facade 0.2.5) after SalesRep was pinned to 3.1017.0 (manifest requiredHostVersion ^0.2.4). The browser fetched mf-manifest.json (200), then the plugin's remoteEntry.js and its assets (200, incl. dashboard and link-my-customers chunks). The header shows a "Sales Rep Hub" link, the account menu has a Sales Rep Hub section (My customers, Tasks), and /company/dashboard renders the hub dashboard instead of the 404 page. Together with KB-93E22C47 this shows the gate is the semver check of requiredHostVersion against the host facade: same host, satisfied range = hub loads, unsatisfied range (^0.2.9) = skipped.

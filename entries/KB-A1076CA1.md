Signed in as a sales rep on storefront theme 2.59.0 (PR build 2536), first full load of /company/dashboard fired SalesRepCustomerOrderStatistics, SalesRepCustomerCartStatistics, SalesRepCustomerCounts, SalesRepOrderFilterRules, SalesRepOrderSortRules, SalesRepOrders, SalesRepTopSellerSortRules, SalesRepTopSellerFilterRules, SalesRepTopSellers, SalesRepTasks x2 and SalesRepOverdueTaskCount. After in-app navigation to /company/my-customers (left rail) and back to Dashboard (left rail), the remount fired SalesRepLayout, SalesRepOrders, SalesRepTopSellers, SalesRepTasks x2, SalesRepOverdueTaskCount and the three statistics ops again, but NOT SalesRepOrderFilterRules, SalesRepOrderSortRules, SalesRepTopSellerFilterRules or SalesRepTopSellerSortRules; the order-status chips (All, Cancelled, New, Payment required, Processing) rendered identically on both mounts. A full page load (typing the URL) in between resets the in-memory cache, so the rule queries refire in that case — a test that wants to observe the cache must navigate in-app.

2026-11-18, vcst B2B-store, signed-in B2B buyer (ORG_USER), Query.product(id, currencyCode). Product ALCE0128 priced 59.99 USD and given a 10 PTS row in the store's 'Loyalty PTS price list': with currencyCode PTS the product returns price list/actual 10 PTS and loyaltyPoints null; with currencyCode USD it returns price 59.99 USD and loyaltyPoints {amount 59, currency PTS}. Same pattern on LT-001 (1299.99 USD -> loyaltyPoints 1299 PTS; with PTS: price 0, loyaltyPoints null) and QA-LOY-PRIO-001 (60 USD -> 60 PTS). LT-001 carries a ProductPoints factor of 500 in an active program, yet its loyaltyPoints is 1299, not factor-derived. So: a PTS price does not make loyaltyPoints non-null under currencyCode PTS; the field is answered when the request currency is a money currency, and its value tracked the floored actual price on these products.

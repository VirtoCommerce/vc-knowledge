Query.quoteAttachmentOptions reports scope quote-attachments, allowAnonymousUpload false, 10 MB max. A file the buyer uploads to POST /api/files/quote-attachments reads 200 for anonymous and for unrelated buyers while it is not attached. After Mutation.addQuoteAttachments links it to a Draft quote created with Mutation.createQuote, it reads 200 for the quote's buyer and admin, and 403 for a buyer of another organization, a customer with no organization, and anonymous. Observed on Quote 3.1004.0-pr-159 and FileExperienceApi 3.1007.0-pr-25. It was not compared with a pre-fix build.

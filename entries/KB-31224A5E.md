On storefront 2.59.0-pr-2524, signed in as an org maintainer, /account/orders shows two toggle buttons All orders and My orders (My orders pressed on first load; the choice survives a reload and the All tab was active on the next visit). My orders columns: Order number, Purchase order, Invoice, Date, Status, Total. All orders columns: Order number, Buyer name, Invoice, Date, Status, Total, i.e. the Purchase order column is replaced, not added to. The Invoice column carries the payment document number (PI...), not a separate invoice. The Filters dialog offers Created date (Custom date, Last day, Last week, Last month, Last year), a Start/End date range, a Buyer name combobox (All tab) and per-status checkboxes with counts; the list pages at 10 per page. Signed in as an org employee of another org who had placed no orders, the same URL shows no tab buttons, a Filters button and search box, and the empty state 'There are no orders yet' with a Continue browsing link, even though that employee's organization has many orders. Anonymous GET of /account/orders or /account/orders/{id} redirects to /sign-in?returnUrl=... ; a signed-in user opening another organization's order id lands on /403 Access denied.

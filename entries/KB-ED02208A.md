Anonymous, desktop 1920px. Home / renders a main-navigation menubar of top-level categories. Clicking "Soft drinks" navigated to /soft-drinks (SEO slug, not /category/{id}); the page has an H1 "Soft Drinks" with a "16results" count superscript, a grid of product cards each with price, quantity stepper and In stock/Out of stock count. All console errors on /, /soft-drinks and the soft-drinks PDP were image loads failing with net::ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED for catalog images whose URLs point at a starmarket demo platform host under /cms-content/assets/catalog/ (plus one 404 for a seeded AGENT-TEST fixture image on the stand's own cms-content). No JS exception, no GraphQL errors[]. This is catalog image data referencing a host that no longer resolves, not storefront code.

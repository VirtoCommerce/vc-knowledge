On theme vc-frontend-next 3.0.0-alpha.2685 (2026-10-09), opening {storefront}/oauth/authorize with no query parameters while signed out redirected to /sign-in?returnUrl=/oauth/authorize showing the normal password sign-in form. After signing in as an org buyer the app returned to /oauth/authorize and rendered a heading 'We could not continue sign-in', the text 'Please restart the connection from your shopping assistant. If the problem persists, contact your store administrator.' and a Home page link - no console error, no crash.

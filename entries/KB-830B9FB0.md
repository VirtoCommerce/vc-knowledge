Cold, cache-disabled guest loads of /search?barcode=<unknown code> on a vc-frontend-next 3.0.0-alpha.2685 build with store barcode fields configured, layout-shift PerformanceObserver (buffered) read ~5 s after load, 3 loads at 1920 and 2 at 375. During the fetch the page shows 'Your search for barcode <v> returned the following', the Grid/List + sort controls row and a page of product skeleton cards (no sidebar: the barcode lookup never shows one). On the 0-hit answer the controls row and skeletons are replaced in one frame by 'No products found for barcode <v>' and the short empty view, and the footer enters the viewport. Largest shift at 1920x1080: div.category-products moves up 62 px (y 442->380) and shrinks 638->222 px tall, footer enters at y=778, value 0.409 (total 0.410-0.413). At 375x812: div.category__body 287->220 and 525->330 tall, footer enters at y=532, value 0.729 (total 0.758). The barcode route is a lighter case of the generic zero-hit keyword shift (KB-8DDFC7BA, ~0.47 / ~0.81) because there is no sidebar to remove. Same source gate: emptyViewSearchOnly (category.vue) includes isBarcodeLookup and requires !fetchingProducts, and hideAllControls is that value. A build without the barcode feature (upstream 2.59.0-pr-2494) ignores ?barcode= and lists the whole catalog, so it shows no barcode empty state to compare.

On the storefront Select items to return step, typing a quantity above the line's Returnable value (e.g. 4 when Returnable is 3) and leaving the field resets the spinbutton to the maximum (3); the footer then reads '1 selected · 3 items' and Continue is enabled. No error message is shown; the over-return is prevented by clamping, not by a validation error. Observed on vcptcore_qa1 2026-10-01.

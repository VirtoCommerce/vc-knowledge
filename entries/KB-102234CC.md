On AdminShell 3.1095.0 with VirtoCommerce.Subscription 3.1007.0-pr-129 and ProductSubscription 3.1000.0-pr-1-595d, GET /api/admin-shell/menu as admin returns: Orders & Customers > Sales > "Subscriptions" (moduleId VirtoCommerce.Subscription, route /subscriptions, permission subscription:access; columns Number/Subscriber/Store/Period start/Status/Balance) and More > "Subscription plans" (route /product-subscription-plans, permission product-subscription:plans:manage) and More > "Subscriptions" (route /product-subscriptions, permission product-subscription:access; columns Number/Status/Product/Quantity/Frequency/Customer/Next order). Both blades are titled "Subscriptions"; the new module's has Renewals and Run scheduler toolbar buttons but no Plans button. A user created with no roles got 403 on POST /subscriptions/search, /plans/search, /renewals/search, /scheduler/run and /import/legacy, currentuser canAccessAdminUI=false, and its menu held no subscription entry.

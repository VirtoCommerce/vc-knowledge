Sales rep signed in on B2B-store, theme 2.61.0-pr-2458, language switched with the header language control and also cold-loaded on the prefixed URL. On /de/company/activities every row date is German ('20. Okt. 2026, in der Stunde von 14:00', '13. Okt. 2026, 16:02:52'). On /fr, /ru, /pl and /pt the same rows keep an English month-name date ('Oct 20, 2026, dans l'heure de 14:00', 'Oct 20, 2026, в течение часа 14:00', 'Oct 20, 2026, w godzinie 14:00'; pt even keeps a 12-hour '11:18:54 AM'), while the surrounding sentence, hour (24h), tabs, chips, tooltips, footer, empty state ('Aucun client attribué pour le moment') and money ('1 152,00 $') are localized. The customer profile Customer activity widget does the same in fr ('Dernière recherche le Oct 20, 2026', '3 vues · Oct 20, 2026') while its numeric dates are French (14/07/2026), and the theme's own /fr/account/orders shows 13/10/2026. Plurals are right: ru 1 вход / 2 входа / 5 входов / 11 входов; pl 1 logowanie / 2 logowania / 5 logowań; de 1 Anmeldung / 5 Anmeldungen; fr 1 connexion / 1 vue / 3 vues. No raw i18n keys appeared after an in-app switch or on three cold reloads of /de/company/activities. So only the month-name date format falls back to English outside de.

On B2B-store, category 'Home Appliances' has seoInfo.pageTitle='home-appliances'. Query.categories returns its breadcrumbs last item title='home-appliances' (the SEO page title), while name='Home Appliances'. The storefront page /home-appliances shows h1 'Home Appliances' (name) but breadcrumb 'home-appliances' and document title 'QA & home-appliances'. Category 'Accessories' has pageTitle='Accessories', so its breadcrumb equals its name. A lower-case breadcrumb label is therefore configured SEO data, not a theme defect.

vcst_qa 2026-11-16, theme vc-frontend-next 3.3.0-pr-23-7be3. A multi-organization contact (Organization maintainer, 12 orgs) opened in an operator's login-on-behalf session: /company/info showed the current org's name; switching organization in the header Organizations dialog re-rendered /company/info with the new company name and its addresses, and /company/members listed exactly the new org's members (matched against POST /api/members/search by organization); switching back while on /company/members re-rendered the previous org's members, none of the other org's left. No console errors. Neither org showed a company two-factor widget for this user. Separately, two customers with no organization opening /company/info directly were redirected to /account/dashboard without console errors. Signing out in a second tab redirected an open /company/info tab to /sign-in?returnUrl=/company/info at once.

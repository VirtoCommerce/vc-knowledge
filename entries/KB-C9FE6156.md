As an organization member whose org has 22 addresses, the /cart Select address popup showed 6 rows and numbered pages 1-4 with Previous/Next. Next sent GetCurrentOrganizationAddresses first=6 after='6' (numeric offset; page 1 sent after='0'), sort isFavorite:desc, totalCount 22 on both calls, and the 6 visible rows were replaced by a disjoint set. The last page held 4 rows including a Billing-only address, so the modal does not filter to shipping-capable addresses. Country/State/City facets send countryCodes/regionIds/cities together and narrow the other facets' term lists. Personal accounts use GetCurrentUserAddresses (currentCustomerAddresses, default sort lastName:asc) with a different column set.

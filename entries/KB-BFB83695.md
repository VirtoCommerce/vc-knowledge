The "Add specific recipients (people or whole companies)" picker in the Push Messages admin blade renders each option as a type badge (literal text "Person" or "Company", an ARIA status element) plus the display name plus the account email where one exists. It shows no per-row count and no id. This discriminates a person from a company and distinguishes entries whose emails differ, but NOT duplicate member records that share both name and email: searching "John Mitchell" returns two options both reading "John Mitchell / test-john.mitchell-20260310@test-agent.com", and searching "AcmeWest" returns four options all reading "AGENT-TEST-Org-AcmeWest-20260310 / acme-west@test-agent.com". Some company rows carry no email at all and are then bare name + badge. Observed on VirtoCommerce.PushMessages 3.1006.0-pr-28-21fe with vc-shell 2.6.1.

Electronics, AGENT-TEST page: with an unsaved edit in the designer (banner 'Has unsaved changes'), opening Version history showed every row's Restore disabled, for page and theme rows alike; the only explanation is the button title 'Save or undo your changes first'. After Discard, Restore on an older page version -> confirm 'Make version <id> your draft?' -> POST /api/site-builder/history/{versionId}/restore?storeId=Electronics&item=page:{groupId} -> 200, the preview showed the older content, a reload kept it, and 'Restored <id> · Page' became the latest version. Restore from a version's Preview banner did the same. Restore of a header version sent item=theme:default/sections/header-group.json -> 200. Preview/Restore take no room until a row is hovered or focused.

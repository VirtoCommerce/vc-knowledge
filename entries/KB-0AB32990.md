In Pricing > Price lists > a price list > item prices (and the product's Prices widget), editing the List price cell to 0 left the Save toolbar button disabled even though Reset became enabled (form dirty). Entering 0.01 enabled Save, so the list-price field rejects 0 in the UI. Entering 0 in the Sale price cell with list price 49.99 enabled Save and saved; the item then showed a price range 0.00-49.99 and the storefront product page showed 0.00 as the actual price with 49.99 as list. A literal "set price to $0.00" step is therefore only reachable through the UI as a $0 sale price.

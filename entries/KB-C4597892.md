The .missions__cards grid mounts empty (0 children, 0 px, no skeleton; only the points-balance amount has a skeleton) until GetLoyaltyMissionProgress returns about 350-510 ms after navigation, then fills to ~1.8k px (missions.vue renders MissionCard with no loading branch). What that does to CLS depends on how the page was reached. On a FULL load the arrival of the cards displaces the account shell's decorative background svg (.vc-container__bg): on vcst on 2026-10-12 (theme 2.60.0-pr-2511-81f1, Chromium, a buyer account with a short sidebar, 3 runs each) total CLS was 0.189 at 1280x900 (0.1607 from that svg) and 0.0994 at 1920x1080 (0.0836), so BL-UI-001 (CLS <= 0.1) is exceeded at 1280 by the unreserved grid; the footer and the grid themselves are never a shift source. On SPA navigation (Points history -> Missions) the same shift lands within ~0.5 s of the click with hadRecentInput=true and counts 0 (6/6 runs). With a sales-rep account whose long sidebar keeps the svg and footer out of view, no grid-caused shift is recorded at all. The earlier reading (2.59.0-pr-2524, Edge, SPA navigation) of CLS 0.115 with the footer as the dominant source summed shifts that the CLS definition excludes. Missions exist only where the store setting Loyalty.Missions.Enable is on (vcptcore: off, /account/missions is 404).

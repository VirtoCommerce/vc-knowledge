Build 2.59.0-pr-2524, Edge, 1280x900, SPA navigation Points history -> Missions & challenges. A MutationObserver logged: the points-balance widget shows a skeleton (points-balance__amount-skeleton) but the .missions__cards container has 0 children and 0 height with zero skeleton nodes until the cards arrive (~430 ms after the click). The footer top then moves 1051 -> 2263 px. A layout-shift PerformanceObserver installed after load recorded CLS 0.115 over 6 shifts; the footer is the dominant source (impact 0.1138, maxMove 1051 px). So BL-UI-001 (CLS <= 0.1) is exceeded on this page by the unreserved card grid.

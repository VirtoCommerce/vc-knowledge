Five REST exports with passwordProtect:false as admin. Manifest.json echoed exactly the flags requested. handleSecurity only, no modules: PlatformEntries has Roles, Users, UserApiKeys and nothing else; each UserApiKeys row has ApiKeyHash and ApiKeyHint (plus UserName, UserId, IsActive, audit fields) and no plaintext apiKey field. handleDynamicProperties only: DynamicProperties and DynamicPropertyDictionaryItems only. handleBinaryData only, no modules: PlatformEntries is {}. handleSettings only with an empty module list: {"Settings":[]} even though the stand has overridden settings; handleSettings with VirtoCommerce.Catalog selected: Settings has 11 entries in PlatformEntries.json, and the VirtoCommerce.Catalog.json part has only PropertyGroups, Properties, PropertyDictionaryItems, Catalogs, Categories, Products, ProductConfigurations, Measures. So settings export follows the module selection and travels in PlatformEntries, not in the module part. Every job finished with errorCount 0 and a downloadUrl.

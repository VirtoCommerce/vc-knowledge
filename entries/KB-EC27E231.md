On vcst_qa 2026-10-08, an organization was given one organization-level role (PUT /api/organizations with roles = [a role holding only xapi:my_organization:return:view]). A member whose membership carried NO role and who had no global role got an org-scoped token with exactly [xapi:my_organization:return:view]. A second member of the same organization whose membership role was Organization employee got 3 permissions: the employee's two storefront view permissions plus return:view. So the org-level role is a union source for every member; a fixture that needs a non-holder in the same organization cannot use an org-level grant there.

On Quote 3.1004.0-pr-158 + XCart 3.1040.0-pr-147 (2026-10-09): a buyer added a printer-catalog product (list 349 from its price list) on the storefront product page; Query.cart returned listPrice/salePrice/placedPrice 349, but the stored cart line read through POST /api/carts/search (responseGroup Full) held listPrice, salePrice and placedPrice 0. Mutation.createQuoteFromCart then produced Query.quote items with listPrice 0, selectedTierPrice 0 and proposalPrices 0, totals 0 — the handler loads the stored cart and converts it, it does not re-evaluate prices. Reproduced on two B2B buyers in different organizations. In the same session a product from another catalog/price list persisted 233 in the cart and entered the quote at 233. The automatically added free-gift line is stored and quoted at 0 by design and appears as an extra quote line. Earlier the same day, before this build pair was deployed, a cart line of the same printer product persisted at 189, so the 0 persistence is newer than that deploy; root cause not isolated.

Guest, Chromium, store B2B-store. Opened /product/c94d730a-a1e3-41d3-bba6-8e9eb9fe45e8 in en-US (URL stays /product/{id}), switched language to Deutsch: page moved to /de/products-with-options/dreamy-cakes/vintage-wedding-cake and rendered the PDP. Switched to the first 'English' entry: URL /en-GB/products-with-options/dreamy-cakes/vintage-wedding-cake, PDP still rendered client-side. Switched to the second 'English' entry (en-US): URL /products-with-options/dreamy-cakes/vintage-wedding-cake rendered the '404 Page not found' view (title 'QA & 404 Page not found'). A direct load of /en-GB/products-with-options/dreamy-cakes/vintage-wedding-cake on the pre-fix theme 2.61.0-pr-2570 also shows the 404 view, so it is not introduced by PR 2599. The language list also shows two entries named just 'English' (en-GB and en-US), indistinguishable by name. Cause not isolated: the slug appears to exist only for some cultures and the switcher keeps the current slug.

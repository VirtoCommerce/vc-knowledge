Signed in as a sales rep serving several test organizations, clicked an organization name on /company/my-customers: the URL became /company/my-customers/105c2c4e-23be-4258-8691-568a0ff190be (a GUID) and the page title and H1 were the organization name. 'All orders' in its Recent orders block went to /company/my-customers/<guid>/orders; opening an order went to .../orders/<order-guid> with H1 'Order #<number>'. The seed business key (ORG-001) never appears in these URLs, so a case asserting a URL equal to /company/my-customers/ORG-001 cannot pass and should resolve the id from the fixture (@td(ORG_ACME.id)).

On vcst_qa 2026-10-08 (Return 3.1005.0-pr-28): POST /api/order/customerOrders with storeId, customerId and customerName null is refused 400 ("must not be empty"); PUT /api/return without an orderId is refused 400 "LineItem ... has incorrect quantity" because every line is measured against the order's available quantity (and a return needs at least one line); and with an orderId the save copies storeId, customerId, customerName and organization from the order. So the legacy store-less rows can only be reused, never seeded.

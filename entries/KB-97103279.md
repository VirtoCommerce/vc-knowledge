2026-10-13, platform 3.1076.1, admin token. GET /api/catalog/categories/search?keyword=Ladders -> 404; POST /api/catalog/categories/search -> 405. POST /api/catalog/search/categories {searchPhrase:"Ladders",take:10} -> 200 {totalCount, items, aggregations} with the matching category; the body field `keyword` is ignored there (returned all 186 categories). Also: POST /api/catalog/search/products ignores a categoryId body field (returned unrelated products); direct children of a category come from POST /api/catalog/listentries {categoryId, catalogId}.

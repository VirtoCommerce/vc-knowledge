On UCP 3.1007.0-pr-9-91ca an update_cart refused with insufficient_stock (duplicates 4+5 against stock 5) is not atomic: get_cart afterwards returns ucp.status success with the line at quantity 9, line-level requested_quantity 9 / available_quantity 5 / inventory_status insufficient_stock, and cart.inventory_errors populated, while cart messages[] stay empty. create_checkout, update_checkout, checkout_and_handoff and REST POST /ucp/v1/checkouts/{id}/handoff all refuse with insufficient_stock and give no continue_url. Correcting the line to an in-stock quantity clears inventory_errors, and checkout_and_handoff then succeeds. The update_cart refusal lists the same error twice in details.errors[].

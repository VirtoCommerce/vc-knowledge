POST .../content-json without eTag -> 428 "Read the page content and send its ETag in If-Match or in the content-json body before saving.". GET .../content?draft=true returned the header ETag as W/"<hash>"; sending that value -> 400 "If-Match must contain the single strong ETag returned by the content GET.". GET .../content?draft=true&includeVersion=true returns {content, eTag:"\"<hash>\""} (strong); sending that eTag -> 204 and a new Draft page is added next to the Published one (hasChanges true). Stripping the W/ prefix also worked.

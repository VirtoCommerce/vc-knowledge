On the product page (not the cart) of a product with a subscription plan the quantity stepper is followed by two cards, One-time purchase (selected by default) and Subscribe & Save, without prices. Choosing Subscribe & Save reveals "Get it every" with a 2x2 grid of the plan's intervals (default interval preselected), the benefits and a "How it works" link. After adding, a status line reads "In your cart as a subscription · Every <interval>" or "In your cart as a one-time purchase". A product with no plan shows only the quantity stepper.

Signed in as a loyalty member on B2B-store, /account/missions page 1 rendered 12 mission cards; the accessibility tree shows 12 icon-only buttons all named 'Open mission' (French: 'Ouvrir la mission'). The card heading (h3, mission name) is a sibling, not part of the button name. vc-frontend mission-card.vue binds :aria-label to the static key pages.account.missions.card.open_mission at 2.59.0 and on dev, so no newer theme differs. Screen-reader users get a list of identical controls.

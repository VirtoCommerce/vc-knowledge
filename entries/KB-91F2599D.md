Read in vc-frontend client-app/shared/catalog/components/category/category-products.vue (master branch; no commit pinned). The products list and its loader render only while products.length > 0 or fetchingProducts is true. Otherwise the component renders the ui-kit VcEmptyView (icon outline-stock) inside div.category-products. Without filters or a keyword it shows the text pages.catalog.no_products_message with variant 'empty'. With active filters or a keyword it shows pages.catalog.no_products_filtered_message with variant 'search' and a reset button (pages.catalog.no_products_button). VcEmptyView gets no data-test-id there. The project's suites reach it by the .vc-empty-view class; I took that class from repo usage, not from the component's source. Not established here: whether choosing a currency that has no price list empties the product list. That is the premise of 048c LAYOUT-CNT-008 and was not checked live in this session.

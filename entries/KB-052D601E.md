Signed in as a personal storefront user on theme 2.62.0-alpha.2642. The accessibility snapshot of /account/profile shows placeholder "Enter your first name" on all three inputs: First name, Last name and the disabled Email. vc-frontend client-app/pages/account/profile.vue binds $t('common.placeholders.first_name') to all three inputs, even though locales/en.json defines common.placeholders.last_name ("Enter your last name") and common.placeholders.email ("Enter your email address"). The wrong key has been in place since vc-frontend #485 (2023-03-22), so the defect is long-standing and not a regression in 2.62.

On VirtoCommerce.Sanity 3.1004.0-pr-4-f7a8 with two secrets in Sanity:WebhookSecrets, POST /api/pages/sanity returned 200 only when the sanity-webhook-signature header was t=<unix ms>,v1=<base64url HMAC-SHA256 of "<t>.<raw body>"> signed with either configured secret (so a second secret allows rotation). It returned 401 with an empty body for: no header; a signature from an unknown secret; t 10 minutes in the past or future (t 4 minutes old passed); t in seconds instead of milliseconds; a body changed after signing; a header without v1, garbage, v1 only, or two v1 values; the legacy URL with api_key and storeId and no signature; a correctly signed body over 10 MB. A signed request for a document id that exists in no configured source still returns 200. Before the secrets were configured, every request returned 401.

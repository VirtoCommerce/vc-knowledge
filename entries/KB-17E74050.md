On storefront 2.59.0-pr-2540, /account/orders -> Filters -> Created date presets compute local-midnight bounds, then take the date part of their UTC ISO string. In a UTC+3 browser every preset chip lands one day early: Last week on Tue Oct 6 2026 shows Start 9/27 (Sunday) - End 10/4, and GetOrders sends createddate:["2026-09-26T21:00:00.000Z" TO "2026-10-04T20:59:59.999Z"]. Last month shows 8/31 - 9/30, Last year shows 12/31/2024 - 12/31/2025. With the same build in a UTC browser on Oct 2 the chips show 9/21 - 9/28 (Monday start), and on Oct 31 they show 9/1 - 10/1. The end chip is inclusive to 23:59:59.999 of that day, so in UTC the first day of the current period (e.g. Oct 1 for Last month) is included. A Custom date range (09/15 - 09/22) sends local-midnight bounds with no shift.

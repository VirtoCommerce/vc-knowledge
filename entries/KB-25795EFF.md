B2B Org Admin, B2B-store, single-page /cart. Account cart held WH-001 x2; signed out from the account menu; as guest added QA-SUBFIVE-001 x1 and QA-LOW-001 x3 via /bulk-order. On sign-in the storefront sent Mutation.mergeCart (MergeCart, errors[] empty) and /cart showed three lines A=2, B=1, C=3 with no duplicates, subtotal the sum of line totals. A second run with one account line + one guest line merged the same way. When the guest cart was empty, sign-out then sign-in restored the account cart exactly (lines, quantities, totals), and while signed out the guest /cart showed the empty state.

On vcst (theme 2.61.0-alpha.2605), a personal user placed a CyberSource card order at checkout; the order stayed at 'Payment required' and its GA4 purchase was already pushed at /checkout/completed. Opening the order from /account/orders, clicking Pay now, paying on the payment page with the CyberSource card reached 'PAYMENT SUCCESSFUL' and pushed ONE more purchase event (value 79.12, the same order's transaction_id) to window.dataLayer. So the same order is reported as a purchase twice. Suite 043 GA-027 (VCST-4800 non-duplication guard) asserts 0 purchase on the re-pay path and failed. VCST-4800 (Done) concerned purchase not being capturable at all, not this duplicate. Not yet cross-checked against docs; treat as a candidate defect.

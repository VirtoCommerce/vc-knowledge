Approved is pre-filled with the requested quantity; one click on Approve / decline commits at once with no confirmation dialog, sets return status Approved/PartiallyApproved/Rejected, locks Quantity/Approved/Decline reason read-only, and writes a ReturnApproved/ReturnPartiallyApproved/ReturnRejectedEmailNotification journal entry within ~1s (7 of 7 decisions observed on 2026-09-29; with SMTP failing the entry has status Error). Decline reason input enables only when Approved < Quantity. Approve / decline is disabled while the blade is dirty after a Quantity edit.

Signed in as a sales rep (Edge, B2B-store, theme 2.62.0-pr-2622, SalesRep 3.1018.0-pr-33-04e4) I opened /company/tasks and measured the desktop table with getBoundingClientRect. The table is table-layout fixed; Priority, Status and Actions are fixed (112, 144, 96 px) and Task, Customer and Notes split the rest equally. Customer header and text widths: 1280 px 95.7 / 63.7; 1366 px 124.3 / 92.3; 1440 px 149 / 117; 1920 px 163.3 / 131.3 (cell padding 16 px each side, 14 px font, 18 px line height). The cell wraps (break-word, mid-token) and is clamped to 2 lines with a trailing ellipsis (line-clamp 2, overflow hidden). A 32-character name needed 7 lines at 1280, 4 at 1366, 3 at 1440 and 3 at 1920, so it stayed clipped at all four widths. The span carries a title attribute with the full name (none for the em dash) and the cell's accessible name is the full name. The SalesRepTasks response carries the full customerName.

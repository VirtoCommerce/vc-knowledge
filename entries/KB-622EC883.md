The storefront page /how-subscriptions-work, titled "How subscriptions work", has a lead, three how-it-works steps, six rule cards and a "Manage your subscriptions" block with a "Go to my subscriptions" button that opens /account/subscriptions. It is opened by the "How it works" link under the benefits of a product's Subscribe & Save block (same tab, no target) and by the same link in the cart's Purchase options popup. The browser title carries the store name first, as on every storefront page.

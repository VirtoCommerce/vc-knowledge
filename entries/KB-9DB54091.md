The stored order keeps the product and the quantity of a configuration: GET /api/order/customerOrders/{id} returns, per Product-type configuration item, productId, sku, quantity, imageUrl, catalogId and categoryId alongside name and type, so the order does identify the catalog product that was chosen and how many were taken. In the modules' own swagger, CartConfigurationItem carries 16 fields and OrderConfigurationItem 15, and the only field the crossing loses is sectionId. The loss of product and quantity is in the storefront GraphQL read surface, not in the record.

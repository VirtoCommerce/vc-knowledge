---
With TaskManagement.TaskTypes at its default, as a rep on /graphql/sales-rep: createSalesRepTask with type "Other" or with no type plus a served customerId saves the link (customerName returned); a type absent from salesRepTaskTypes ("AGENT-TEST unlisted") and "CALL" are both saved verbatim. On /company/tasks a non-call task with a customer reads "Due <date> · <customer> · Other" (no time); its Edit task form has no Customer field and an unchanged save keeps the link and the unlisted type. "CALL" is rendered as a call (time and customer on the line). A task whose WorkTask carries another record's link (ObjectType CustomerOrder, made through POST /api/task-management) reads customerId null ("Customer not assigned" when typed Call); updateSalesRepTask with any customerId on it answers "This task is linked to another record, so it cannot take a customer." and keeps ObjectType/ObjectId, while an update without customerId succeeds. The storefront's Edit call still offers the Customer picker there and, on Save, shows only "Something went wrong. Please try again later."

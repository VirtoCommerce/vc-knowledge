Global MultiFactorAuthentication.Enabled off, disposable stores. Customer of a StoreMode=Enabled store: password grant with no storeId, with storeId of a Use-global store, and with a nonexistent storeId -> all three 400 invalid_grant two_factor_required with a twoFactorToken. Customer of a Use-global store: no storeId and unknown storeId -> 400 user_cannot_login_in_store, no code; naming the OTHER (Enabled) store -> 400 two_factor_required and a code email although that customer belongs to another store. User mode Enabled in an own CustomerChoice store and organization Required in an own AdminManaged store are also asked on a request naming an unknown store; back to Off -> user_cannot_login_in_store.

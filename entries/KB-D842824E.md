No. The order totals calculator builds one bucket per currency, uses the order itself as the primary-currency bucket, and computes each bucket's Total only from that currency's lines. So the order's top-level total equals its primary-currency orderTotals entry, and a loyalty-currency line's amount appears only in that currency's own orderTotals entry. A mission OrderValueGoal accrues order.Total, so loyalty-currency lines cannot advance it; a PerSkuGoal counts line quantities with no currency filter.

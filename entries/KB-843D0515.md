DELETE /api/members?ids=<orgId> on an organization that a Sales Rep serves returns success, but GET /api/sales-rep/{id} still lists that organization in organizations[] (with its membershipId), and the OrganizationMembership rows in the deleted org (the rep's and any customer's) survive, visible via POST /api/customer/organization-memberships/search {organizationId}. A read-modify-write PUT /api/sales-rep that keeps the deleted org entry fails with 500 'INSERT statement conflicted with the FOREIGN KEY constraint FK_MemberRelation_Member_AncestorId'; the same PUT succeeds once the deleted org entry is dropped from organizations[], and that removal also deletes the rep's orphan membership. Removing a live org from organizations[] via PUT /api/sales-rep likewise deletes the rep's membership in it while the other entries keep their membershipIds.

Chrome 155, 1440x900, real Tab/Shift+Tab. Schedule call popup opened with Enter on the tile: Tab from Title focuses Customer and its list opens (aria-expanded=true); Tab to Clear, then Due date: the Customer list is still open and elementFromPoint over all five sample points of the focused Due date input (and of Open calendar, and of Time) returns the list item 'Contoso Ltd.', i.e. the focused control is entirely hidden. The Time list opens on focus too (it covers Priority partly), the Priority list opens on focus and covers the Notes textarea (5/5 points). After a full cycle three comboboxes report aria-expanded=true at once and three listboxes are visible. New task with Type = Call: four lists open at once (Type, Customer, Time, Priority) and the focused Customer input is hidden under the Type list. The same happens in the non-call New task popup: the Type list covers Due date and Open calendar, the Priority list covers Notes, so it predates the call mode; the new Customer and Time selects add exposure. Mouse use is unaffected (a click elsewhere closes every list), Escape closes the dialog and returns focus to the trigger button (Schedule call tile / New task) even with lists open, and Escape with focus in a combobox or on an option closes only that list. Violates BL-A11Y-001 / WCAG 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured. Screenshots in the ticket run folder (V1-schedule-call-tab3-due-date-obscured-by-customer-list-1440.png, V1-new-task-call-four-lists-open-after-tab-walk-1440.png).

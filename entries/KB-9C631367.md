As a signed-in storefront buyer, a request {a: contact(id:<own contact id>){id} b: contact(id:<own contact id>){id}} sent 20 times sequentially returned an error on one alias in 19 of 20 runs: "Error trying to resolve field 'a'" or "'b'", extensions.code ARGUMENT, data for that alias null, the other alias resolved. Same for two aliased organization(id:<own org>) lookups (15-19 of 20). A contact(id) plus an organization(id) in one request never failed (20/20), and a second alias with a nonexistent id answers Forbidden instead. Observed identically on ProfileExperienceApi 3.1022.0 (vcst_qa) and 3.1022.0-pr-149-2242 (vcptcore_qa1), so it is not introduced by PR #149; it matches the duplicate-key write the PR body names as out of scope (root member resolvers adding "<name>Aggregate" to UserContext).

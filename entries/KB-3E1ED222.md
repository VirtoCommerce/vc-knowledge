On vcst_qa (PageBuilderModule 3.1033.0-pr-170, 2026-10-08), with the designer opened from a page-builder page (groupId in the URL), the toolbar 'Pages' dropdown shows Blog / Pages / Product template; 'Pages' lists Contacts, Home, test demo landing from GET /api/pagebuilder/search?storeId=B2B-store&theme=default&type=pages. Choosing 'test demo landing' adds path=/test_demo_landing.en-US.page to the URL and changes the label to 'Choose template', but the designer re-fetches GET /api/page-builder-pages/grouped/{current groupId}/content and keeps showing the current page's sections and preview; no content of the selected page is requested.

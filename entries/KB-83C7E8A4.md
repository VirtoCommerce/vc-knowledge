Guest, 1920px, Chromium, axe-core 4.10.2. On the Vintage Wedding cake PDP (/product/c94d730a-a1e3-41d3-bba6-8e9eb9fe45e8) with the four optional sections (Buttercreme style, Filing, Flowers, Message Tag) expanded, axe rule image-alt (critical) reports 4 nodes: img.vc-image--fallback.vc-product-image__img inside .option-product-none[data-test-id=none-option] .vc-product-card__media, an inline SVG data-URI placeholder with no alt and no role=presentation. On the Bed with Additional Options PDP (Pillow expanded) it reports 1 node. Collapsed sections do not render it, so a default-state scan shows 0. Seen identically on the pre-fix theme 2.61.0-pr-2570 and on the PR 2599 build, so it is independent of the radio-naming fix. Being decorative, it would need alt="" rather than a name.

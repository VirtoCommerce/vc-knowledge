Electronics has no storefront URL; GET /api/site-builder/designer/storefront?storeId=Electronics answered url https://<B2B storefront> (the installation-wide SiteBuilder.General.StoreUrl override wins over the store's own URL), so the preview iframe is the B2B-store storefront. The designer's pickers list Electronics' link lists (GET /api/cms/Electronics/menu). Picking Televisions, footer or main-menu for Header Icons, or Camcorders / main-menu for a footer Menu block, made the preview send GetMenu with storeId B2B-store and that name; every answer was {"menu":null}. The theme turns null into an empty list (getMenu returns items ?? []) and an empty list is not a fallback, so the icon row went empty (no Bulk order/Lists/Orders/Cart) and the footer column lost even its inline Links (menu wins over links). Clearing the picker restored both. The designer and the preview disagree on the store whenever StoreUrl points at another store's storefront.

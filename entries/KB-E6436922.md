Sales rep, own list, storefront Share dialog only (theme 2.59.0-pr-2476-0abb over a vcst_qa backend). NO confirmation, saves at once: Specific customers -> Anyone with link (3/3 runs), My organization -> Anyone with link, Private -> Specific customers (toast 'List shared with 1 customer.'), adding a second customer with nobody removed (toast 'List shared with 2 customers.'). Confirmation 'Change who can access?' / 'The link stays the same. Some users may lose access.' / Cancel - Change access: Anyone with link -> Specific customers, Anyone with link -> My organization, Specific customers -> My organization, and removing one of two recipients while staying in Specific customers (new on this build; after Change access the toast is 'List saved. Now shared with 1 customer.'). Shared -> Private: 'Stop sharing this list?' / 'Everyone the list is shared with will lose access. You can share it again any time.' / Cancel - Stop sharing. Cancel, X and Esc on a confirmation save nothing and return to the Share dialog; after Cancel on a recipient removal, reopening Share shows both recipients. The sharing key (sharingSetting.id) stayed identical through every change; after the widen, anonymous Query.sharedWishlist(key) returns the list with access Read and a signed-out /shared-list/{key} renders it read-only.

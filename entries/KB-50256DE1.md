GET /designer-preview?ep=... and other storefront routes answer 200 with no Content-Security-Policy and no X-Frame-Options header (only COEP unsafe-none and CORP cross-origin), and index.html has no CSP meta tag. A page served from http://127.0.0.1 with an iframe of /designer-preview?ep=https://example.com loaded the frame (200, theme JS loaded); it was not refused. Opened in its own tab, /designer-preview shows the storefront 404 Page not found view, with no console errors. The admin designer preview iframe on this stand also shows "refused to connect" (designer sends COEP credentialless; storefront does not opt in).

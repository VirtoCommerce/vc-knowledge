No. The New promotion blade prints 'Your promotion must have at least one eligibility criterion' and 'Your promotion must have at least one reward' in red inside Promotion conditions, but neither is wired to the Create button: Create is enabled by the NAME alone and the POST succeeds, leaving a promotion with BlockCustomerCondition and BlockReward both empty, isActive true, no store scoping, and the two red warnings still on the blade after the reload. The same is true of Save on an existing one. So a promotion that grants nothing is a perfectly reachable state reached by one distracted click, and it is indistinguishable in the promotions grid from a working one -- the grid has Name, Active, Has Coupon and dates, and nothing about whether the promotion carries a reward. When a promotion 'does nothing' on a cart, read its dynamicExpression tree before reading its conditions: the reward may simply not be there.

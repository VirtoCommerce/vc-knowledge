A rep whose per-org membership in one served organization is locked: salesRepActivities (no organizationId) listed only the other served org, and salesRepActivities / salesRepCustomerActivitySummary / salesRepCustomerInsights with organizationId of the locked org returned null; the storefront /company/activities?organizationId=<locked org> and /company/my-customers/<locked org> showed "Customer not found or not in your customers." and the org was absent from the My customers list. A rep whose account is bound to the Electronics store got user_cannot_login_in_store on a B2B-store token, and with an Electronics token the queries returned null for storeId=B2B-store but data (isAnalyticsAvailable=false) for storeId=Electronics. A B2B-store rep who can also obtain an Electronics token got Electronics data. storeId=no-such-store returned null. So the store gate follows sign-in eligibility, not the token's store.

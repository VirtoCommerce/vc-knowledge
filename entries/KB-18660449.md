Read-only check on two reps, every served org live (GET /api/members/{id} echoes the id). Rep A has 12 organizations in GET /api/sales-rep/{id} organizations[]. Signed in as rep A (password grant, storeId set), salesRepCustomers(first: 100, storeId) on POST /graphql/sales-rep returns totalCount 12 with no errors and lists the same organization ids. Rep B has 5 organizations in REST and gets totalCount 5 from salesRepCustomers; none of rep A's organizations appear in rep B's list. So when every served org exists, the REST organizations[] matches the rep's own salesRepCustomers, and an org served only by rep A is absent from rep B's list. Caveat (KB-843D0515): REST organizations[] keeps a DELETED org, so REST alone does not prove the org exists. Check GET /api/members/{id} as well.

The Customer module's organization roles whitelist does filter the Admin SPA organization role picker: the choices are the whitelist intersected with the org-assignable roles, minus roles already assigned. An empty whitelist means no restriction - the picker shows every role, BY DESIGN, because the filter only runs when the list is non-empty; the org-level picker and the per-member membership editor share that logic. A whitelist edit shows in the picker only after Settings > Reset cache and a full reload. Whitelist entries that are not org-assignable roles never appear, so their presence proves nothing. Clearing a dictionary setting to empty now persists after reload.

2026-11-28, vcptcore, Catalog 3.1055.0-alpha.2598, platform 3.1106.0, 3 fresh runs. Catalogs Owner/Src/Dst; product P owned by Owner (no category) linked into category C (Src), P2 owned by Owner linked into C2 (child of C), product O owned by Src inside C and also linked into category U of Owner. POST /api/catalog/listentries/move of C into Dst root returns 204. Afterwards GET /api/catalog/products/{P}?respGroup=Full: catalogId = Owner, categoryId null, single link now catalogId=Dst/categoryId=C; P2 likewise (link Dst/C2); Owner listentries still lists P and P2; Dst root lists only categories. C, C2 and O report catalogId = Dst and O keeps its link to Owner/U. A follow-up intra-catalog move leaves all catalogIds unchanged. Admin Cut/Paste gives the same result.

Guest, /catalog in de, pt and ru at a 320x700 viewport. The count beside the "Catalog" H1 is a sup.category__products-count with computed white-space: nowrap and no ellipsis. With the real translations ("3.599 Ergebnisse", "3597 resultados", "3 599 результатов") the page is exactly 320 wide: they fit. With every visible text doubled (layout-runner [STRESS:TEXT] x2, a translation twice as long), document scrollWidth becomes 353 (de), 341 (pt) and 375 (ru), and the widest element is that sup: it cannot wrap, so it widens the document. el, fi and pl stay at 320 under the same stress. In short: the label has no headroom beyond the current translations. BL-UI-004 asks long content to wrap, truncate with an ellipsis or scroll inside its container.

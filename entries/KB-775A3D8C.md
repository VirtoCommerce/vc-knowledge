Read at vc-module-sales-rep@31e85c6. xAPI: Phone = primaryContact.Phones.FirstOrDefault() ?? organization.Phones.FirstOrDefault(); Address = Addresses.FirstOrDefault(IsDefault) ?? Addresses.FirstOrDefault(). Storefront buildCustomerPhones() (StorefrontApp/src/phones.ts) lists every primaryContact.phones entry, then `phone`, then `address.phone`, deduplicated by normalized digits. So the org's 2nd..Nth phones never reach the client and other addresses' phones are ignored. Consistent with live admin data on vcptcore_qa: an ownerless org with 6 org phones, one non-default address with a phone, a rep member (skipped) and a phone-less buyer as primary contact shows exactly 2 entries.

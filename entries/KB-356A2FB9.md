The storefront Role facet on /company/members is filled 1:1 from xAPI organization.contactRoles (GetOrganizationContactRoles); it is not the assignableRoles whitelist. xAPI builds contactRoles from the roles of every OrganizationMembership row with that OrganizationId, plus org-level roles, plus the global roles of those users. organization.contacts only lists contacts linked to the org (member search by org id). On vcst_qa, TechFlow had 29 membership rows, and for 10 of them the user's contact had organizations: []. Store manager was held only by such a row, so it was offered as a facet option, and GetOrganizationContacts(roleIds:["store-manager"]) returned totalCount 0. Roles that also have linked holders (org-employee, purchasing-agent) still filter correctly.

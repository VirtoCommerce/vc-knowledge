Different scopes, not a disagreement. A promotion whose reward is order-level (e.g. a percentage off the cart subtotal) is recorded on the resulting CustomerOrder as exactly one entry in the order-level discounts collection, and nowhere else: every line item keeps its full placedPrice with an empty discounts array and items[].discountAmount/discountTotal of 0, the shipment discountAmount is 0, and subTotalDiscount is 0.00 as well - only the order-level discountAmount/discountTotal carry the reduction. Round one's arm B read the Admin Line items blade header as Discount 0.00 and Total 1,612.97 while the order header on the same order read Discount 241.95 and Total 1,371.02, the whole 241.95 sitting in the order's own discounts collection. So the Line items header is the untouched sum of the lines and the order header is the sum after the order-level reward, and both are right. Reading the blade header as the order total is the mistake, and it is easy to make because the two screens sit one click apart and neither says which scope it is showing.

With the plugin locale chunk (plugins/vc-frontend/assets/en-*.js) held for 6 s on a cold load, the rep's /company/dashboard shows raw keys from about 1.3 s until the chunk lands (about 7.5 s): SALES_REP.HUB.TITLE, Sales_rep.hub.dashboard.navigation.link, Sales_rep.my_customers.navigation.link, Sales_rep.tasks.title, SALES_REP.HUB.DASHBOARD.PAGE.TITLE, SALES_REP.HUB.DASHBOARD.WIDGETS.*, sales_rep.hub.dashboard.stats.*. A buyer sees Sales_rep.navigation.link as the Corporate sidebar link, and on /company/sales-reps sees SALES_REP.PAGE.TITLE and sales_rep.table.name/email/phone. With remoteEntry.js held for 15 s, the Corporate link reads Sales_rep.navigation.link for the whole wait. Unthrottled cold loads in en/de/es/el show the same keys for about 0.1-0.4 s. Settled state is correct: de and es translated, el (no plugin locale file) falls back to English.

Guest, 1920x1080, theme 3.3.0. On /cables-docks-adapters the header searchbox is named 'Search within Cables, Docks & Adapters' and carries a removable chip 'Remove: Cables, Docks & Adapters'; typing 'UGREEN' shows a Products group with 1 item and Enter navigates to /cables-docks-adapters?q=UGREEN with h1 'Search for "UGREEN" in Cables, Docks & Adapters' and '1 result found'. From '/' the searchbox is 'Search products and parts'; typing 'UGREEN' shows groups 'Hints' and 'Products' (4 items, each with a price), Enter opens /search?q=UGREEN with '4 products found'. Typing 'Belkin' shows 8 Products items (variation products show no price in the dropdown) and /search?q=Belkin says '8 products found'. In neither case is a 'View all N results' button rendered in the dropdown. The Hints group listed the previously searched term ('UGREEN') while 'Belkin' was typed.

On vcptcore_qa1 (platform 3.1086.0-pr-3137-8aa4, VirtoCommerce.AdminShell 3.1000.0-pr-1-f481), 2026-10-23, three Manager accounts (isAdministrator=false) were created and each called GET /api/admin-shell/menu with its own password-grant token. (1) Role admin-shell:access + order:access + order:read: HTTP 200, sections [dashboard, orders, insights], items [home, orders, reports] — every item gated by a permission the user lacks (tasks, catalog, returns, marketing, platform.settings, platform.security, platform.menu, …) is absent; home and reports carry no permission and are always present. (2) Same order permissions WITHOUT admin-shell:access: /connect/token 200 but the menu call is HTTP 403 (not 401, not 200 with an empty list). (3) Two roles — the role in (1) plus admin-shell:access + marketing:access + marketing:read: HTTP 200 with items [home, orders, marketing, marketing.dynamicContent, reports], i.e. the union of both roles' permissions. The admin's own menu shows each item's gate in its `permission` field (orders → order:access, marketing and marketing.dynamicContent → marketing:access). A section is omitted when none of its items survive. No AdminShell.Menu.Role patch existed for these roles.

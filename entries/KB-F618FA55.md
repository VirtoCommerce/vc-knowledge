Platform 3.1100.0, admin. Opening a product from Catalog > <catalog> > <category> > product row (Admin route #!/workspace/catalog?productId=<id>, blade loads GET /api/catalog/products/{id}): the product blade shows name, SKU, GTIN, MPN, Vendor, Tax type, ID, flags and widgets (Properties, Variations, Links, Fulfillment Centers, Price, Configuration...) but no Category field and no category link. The category path is only the breadcrumb on the 'Categories and items' blade to its left; clicking the category crumb closes the product blade and re-lists that category's items (?categoryId=&catalogId=), it does not open a stacked category detail blade.

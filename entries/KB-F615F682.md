A sales rep's serving relationship makes the rep a member of each served organization, and the storefront account menu lets the rep select it. Observed on the wire: rep sessions are tagged up.organization_id=<served org>, up.session_kind=self, up.is_sales_rep=true (seen for two reps, two different served orgs). Opening a customer's search term from the rep hub (new tab /search?q=<term>) sent view_search_results tagged that way. In the sales-rep module PR for GA reports, SalesRepAnalyticsScope.CreateScopeFilters applies only session_kind=Self and organization_id IN served orgs; is_sales_rep is collected but never filtered on. So the rep's own searches, product views and sign-ins on a served org are reported as the customer's (the Summary's last search also reads view_search_results). The module's own comment states the intent that the rep's browsing must not be reported as the customer's.

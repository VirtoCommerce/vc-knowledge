On vcst_qa 2026-10-08, four single-organization contacts were seeded whose membership role granted xapi:my_organization:return:view, differing only in the membership: permanently locked (status Approved), or status Invited, Rejected, Deleted. Password grant with storeId AND organization_id = that organization: the locked member got 200, but the token carried no organization_id claim and zero permission claims; Invited, Rejected and Deleted got 400 invalid_grant. Password grant WITHOUT organization_id: all four got 200 with no organization and zero permissions. So a blocked member can still obtain a token, it is just empty; a test that needs a token issued BEFORE the block must sign in first and block afterwards. Unblocked twins in the same organization got 200 with the organization and [return:view].

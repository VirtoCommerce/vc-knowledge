On vcptcore_qa1 B2B-store, a cart whose availableShippingMethods lists FixedRate (options Ground and Air, rate 10) rejected Mutation.addOrUpdateCartShipment {shipmentMethodCode: FixedRate, shipmentMethodOption: Ground} with only "Error trying to resolve field 'addOrUpdateCartShipment'", extensions.code VALIDATION, no per-rule detail. The same call with deliveryAddress (US, CA) and price 10 (= the rate) succeeded for both Ground and Air. Both fields were added at once, so which one the validator needed was not separated (x-cart's shipment validator compares the shipment price with the method rate). BuyOnlinePickupInStore could be set without them. A generic VALIDATION here is therefore not evidence that FixedRate is broken on the stand.

Placing an order with exactly one product added to the cart produced an order containing TWO line items. The cart page shows a "+ Add a gift" panel below the product table offering "Canon Imageclass WiFi MF232W Monochrome Laser Printer/Scanner/Copier" with its checkbox ALREADY CHECKED on arrival, a disabled quantity of 1, and no price. Nothing else on the cart reflects it: the product table lists one row, the cart badge reads 1, "Subtotal" is the single product's price, and the GA4 view_cart event reports items_count=1. After Place order, the GA4 purchase event reports items_count=2, with pr2 = the printer at pr0 (i.e. $0.00), qt1, carrying promotion "Auto Gift (no coupon)" (promotion id 782b9d77-fb67-4a4b-a80f-5e86998fdfb3). The placed-order page likewise renders the printer in its own "+ Add a gift" panel, outside the product table, so the order's product table and subtotal still show only the one purchased product. Net effect: a shopper who never interacts with the gift panel still receives an extra order line. Two adjacent UI defects on the same panel: the gift row's checkbox carries aria-label "Toggle vendor select" (copied from the product table's select-all), and the panel offers no visible indication that the item is an applied promotion reward rather than an optional add-on.

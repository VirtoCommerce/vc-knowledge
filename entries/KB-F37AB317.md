Order: endDate ascending, null endDate last, ties by id (ordinal), identical across two requests. Paging: after is a numeric offset string ("0","10","20"); pageInfo.endCursor is the next offset; pages of 10 union to totalCount with no repeat; after beyond the end returns 0 items with the same totalCount.

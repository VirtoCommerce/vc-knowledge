Signed in, Chromium 1920, seen both on the deployed vcst_qa storefront and on unmerged PR 2598 build 608d (local frontend proxied to vcst_qa). Each flip of /cart between empty and non-empty (by Save for later of the last line, Move to cart, Remove from cart, or + on a recently browsed card) pushed another view_item_list recently_browsed_products with the same 6 items. Move to cart from the saved-for-later block pushed no add_to_cart (only the saved-for-later impression on the PR build); + on a recently browsed card pushed add_to_cart. Remove from cart on the last line pushed add_shipping_info with 0 items, remove_from_cart and clear_cart for the same line.

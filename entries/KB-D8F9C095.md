On virtostart two administrator accounts (isAdministrator=true, role claim __administrator) were members of three organizations with role Advanced Sales Representative plus a role holding only xapi:my_organization:return:view. Logged in on behalf (impersonate grant by an operator holding loginOnBehalf), then refresh_token with organization_id: each token carried organization_id and the xapi:my_organization:return:view permission claim, and organizationReturns returned the org's returns (5, 4 and 2). This complements KB-74682777, where an administrator who was NOT a member got no xapi:my_organization claims and was refused: membership plus a granting role is what opens it, administrator status alone does not.

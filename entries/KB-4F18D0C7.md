The Designer resolves settings.json entries marked init:true by requesting their url and storing `result || fallbackValue || null` (initConfigProperty). A selector that evaluates to false is therefore stored as null. Observed with a non-admin user lacking platform:asset:create on PageBuilderModule 3.1033.0-pr-166: currentuser returned the permission list without platform:asset:create, the picker hid New folder (check === true) but still showed Upload (check !== false), and the upload then failed 403. Any capability flag tested with `!== false` is effectively always true for users without the permission.

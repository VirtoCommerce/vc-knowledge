Same Proposal-sent quote, same session, same GetQuote data (listPrice 349.00 / 189.00, selectedTierPrice.price 314.10 / 170.10). At 1920px the Price per item cell shows 349.00 and 189.00 (list); after resizing to 375px the per-line 'Price per item' property shows 314.10 and 170.10 (proposal). Line totals 628.20 and 510.30 are the same at both widths. Source (vc-frontend-next d67b0e0a and dev; identical in vc-frontend dev/master): VcLineItem renders the narrow-layout property as actualPrice || listPrice, while the wide-layout cell renders actual-price = showPlacedPrice ? actualPrice : listPrice; quote-line-items.vue passes actual-price = selectedTierPrice.price and does not pass show-placed-price, so the two layouts disagree. The switch is a container query at 56rem (containers.4xl), not the window width.

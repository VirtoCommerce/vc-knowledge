On vcst_qa (theme 2.62.0-pr-2596) a public single-coupon promotion whose coupon has MemberId = an organization was typed into the cart's 'Discount & coupons' Custom code field. Signed in as a buyer of that organization the card showed "Coupon <code> applied. Discount $0.60, new total ..." and the order summary Discount line became -$0.60. Signed in as a buyer of a different organization, same code: the field was marked invalid with the alert "This code is not valid" and Discount stayed $0.00. All /graphql responses were 200. So binding a coupon to an organization limits redemption to that organization's members; the same coupon is not listed by salesRepCoupons on the rep pages.

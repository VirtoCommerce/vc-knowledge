On vcptcore_regression 2026-10-14, a configurable parent with 3 real variations (mainProductId = parent, each with Variation-type properties Color/Size) got a configuration whose single section had type Variation and options [{productId: <variation id>, quantity 1|2|1}] — sent once on POST /api/catalog/products/configurations (create) and again on a POST upsert of the whole configuration with allowPredefinedOptions true. Both calls returned 200 and the section came back with allowPredefinedOptions true but options [] — on the POST response and on GET /api/catalog/products/configurations/{id}. PUT /api/catalog/products/configurations/{id} answered 405. So a Variation section cannot carry per-option quantity or an explicit option subset through REST on this build; its choices come only from product.variations, and Query.productConfiguration returns options [] for it (x-cart builds options for Product and Text sections only). A case that needs a Variation option with quantity > 1 is not reachable by seeding.

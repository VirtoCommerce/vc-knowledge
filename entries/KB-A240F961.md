On the storefront Sales Rep hub (2.59.0), the customer orders page /company/my-customers/:organizationId/orders, the all-customers page /company/customer-orders and the Orders widget on /company/dashboard and on the customer profile render orders as mobile cards up to 1023px viewport width and as a table (Order #, Date, Status, Total; Date and Total sortable) from 1024px. Each card shows the order number link and the total on one flex row; the link targets /account/orders/:orderId, the buyer order-details page. Order numbers wrap (word-break: break-word), so long or unbroken numbers break inside the link instead of overflowing.

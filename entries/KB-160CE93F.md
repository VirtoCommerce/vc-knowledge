GET /api/platform/settings/Stores.SeoLinksType (descriptor, group Store|SEO) lists allowedValues None, Short, Collapsed, Long and defaultValue Collapsed. In the Admin store Settings blade the setting is group "Store > SEO", label "SEO links" (a single control). Store-scoped values read through /api/platform/settings/v2/tenant/Store/<id>/values and the public xAPI store(storeId){settings{seoLinkType}} agree on vcptcore_dev: B2B-store Long, Clothing, Electronics and store-acme Collapsed; the platform-global record reads Long. No store on this stand uses Short or None, so those two shapes were not observed. With Long links a nested category or product permalink is the full parent chain (seed-electronics/seed-cable-ties-100pack); no published guide lists the four values (the store-settings guide shows the SEO group as a screenshot, the xAPI reference says only 'The type of SEO link used for the store').

On theme 2.60.0-pr-2523-466f-466fa0a8, signed in as an organization maintainer, the account sidebar on /account/returns (both tabs) and /account/dashboard is hidden below 768 px, 208 px wide from 768 to 1279 px and 240 px from 1280 px. Each sidebar link label sits in a .vc-menu-item__content box (UI-kit VcMenuItem) whose computed word-break is break-all; in the 208 px sidebar that box is 124 px wide. 'Coupons & promotions' needs 140.7 px (Lato 14 px; its widest word 'promotions' is 70.9 px), so it wraps as 'Coupons & promoti' / 'ons'. Measured with per-character Range rects on Firefox 151 at 768, 960, 1023, 1024 and 1279 px (both pages); the same break is visible on Chrome at 768, 960, 1023 and 1024 px. At 1280 px the box is 156 px and the label is one line. The other 13 English labels fit on one line. A temporary local override to word-break normal (with overflow-wrap break-word or anywhere) makes it wrap as 'Coupons &' / 'promotions'. The loyalty 'Missions & challenges' link goes through the same component but is not enabled on this stand.

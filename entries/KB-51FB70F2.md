On vcptcore_qa1 a disposable buyer in orgs A and B was locked in A (POST /api/customer/organization-memberships/{id}/lock) and signed in by emailed code; the sign-in fell back to B. The account menu's Organizations listbox showed B selected and A as a greyed, disabled option with a padlock icon (not clickable). With A's membership status Deleted instead, A was absent and, with one accessible org left, the menu showed no Organizations list at all.

In the Admin SPA Push Messages audience builder, mode 'Specific people or companies', the picker chip and the estimate headline agree and expand the whole subtree. A parent organization holding one contact directly and one child organization holding two login contacts plus one contact with no security account previewed 3 recipients (a non-recursive expansion would read 1; counting the no-login contact would read 4). The child organization alone previewed 2. The no-login contact picked alone previewed 0 recipients. The chip itself annotates the count ('Company | <name> | 3 recipients'), and the hint under the picker reads 'Adding a company includes everyone in it, and in the companies under it.' Sending to the child organization then showed Recipients = 2 on the sent message row and in the Recipients blade, listing exactly the two login contacts and not the no-login one, and one of those two saw the message body in the storefront header notification bell within seconds. So previewed count = Recipients blade count = delivered. Estimate panel also breaks the number down as 'members matched' / 'N companies expanded' / 'people in scope'.

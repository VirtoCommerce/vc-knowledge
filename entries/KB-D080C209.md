Assigning a rep to a customer makes the rep a member of that customer's organization, and the storefront grants full buyer rights on an order to any member of the organization that placed it. So a rep can open any order of a customer they serve on the buyer-facing /account/orders/{id} and act on it, payment included, while orders of organizations they do not serve are refused with 403. This is BY DESIGN: the order access rule allows an administrator, the order's customer, or a contact belonging to the order's organization, and a serving rep is the last. The rep hub's read-only order view omitting the pay action is presentation only. Whether a rep should act as buyer on a colleague's customer is an open product question, not a defect.

On /search?q=tablet (anonymous, Chrome 1920): clicking the prefilled header search box opened the dropdown and pushed one view_item_list with item_list_id search_bar (8 items, same SKUs and order as the dropdown's Products list). Escape closed the dropdown AND cleared the box. Clicking the empty box again (after blurring) reopened the dropdown, which still listed the previous phrase's Pages, Categories and Products, and no new impression was pushed. Typing a new phrase pushed exactly one search_bar impression for it after the debounce. Closing by clicking outside and reopening with the phrase still in the box pushed one more search_bar impression per open. At 390 px, tapping Toggle search bar opened the overlay with the q phrase and pushed one search_bar impression.

During regression REG-2026-11-17-1054 the PAY-DECLINE-001/002/006 runner paid a storefront order with CyberSource using 4000000000000002 (a commonly cited decline test number); the payment was AUTHORIZED and order CO261117-00032 was created. No card that produces a decline was verified on this stand, so decline cases were recorded BLOCKED. Whether any number declines depends on the CyberSource sandbox account configuration, which is not visible from the storefront.

On /search?barcode=<v>&q=<term> the term is ignored by the product request (SearchProducts sends query "" with a barcode:"<v>" filter). What the header shows depends on the theme build.

On an early vc-frontend 2.59 barcode build (observed 2026-09-28) the header search box displayed the ignored term and the Barcode scan button was hidden, so the page offered no way to scan again and suggested a text search that was not running.

From the later 2.59 barcode build, the header bars read q through a composable that returns empty while a barcode param is set: the box is EMPTY and Barcode scan is visible, on desktop and in the 390 px search overlay; Enter in the empty box goes to /search (no q, no barcode) and a new term goes to /search?q=<term>. Observed live 2026-10-08 on the released theme 2.59.0 (vcst_qa) and on 2.59.0-pr-2506 (vcptcore_qa1), with the barcode scanner enabled and the store's barcode search in Full-text mode (fields []); the 2026-09-30 observation of the same was in exact mode.

On a vc-frontend 2.59 barcode build with exact mode configured, /search?barcode=<v>&q=<term> listed the barcode's results (the products request sent an empty query) while the desktop search box and the 390 px overlay box were both filled with <term>, and the Barcode scan button was absent because the box was not empty. Submitting the box navigated to /search?q=<term>, dropping barcode.

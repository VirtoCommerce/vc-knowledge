On theme 2.59.0-pr-2476-0abb with sales-rep 3.1012.0-pr-21-8964, both the customer profile QUICK ACTIONS 'Send email' tile and the My customers row envelope ('Send email') open the 'Send a message' modal with subtitle 'Reaches every member of <org>', Title (optional), an unlabelled Message* textarea and Send via* (Email, Push notification). Send stays disabled until the message has non-whitespace text AND at least one channel is checked. Send posts Mutation.sendCustomerCommunication to /graphql with organizationIds=[the row/profile org id] (single element, legacy organizationId not sent), sendEmail/sendPush per checkbox, title omitted when blank, storeId, cultureName. Success shows toast 'Your message has been sent.' and closes the modal; opening another row after cancelling resets the form and subtitle (no stale draft). Sending to an organization whose only member is the rep returns no delivery and shows 'This organization has no contacts to notify.' with the modal kept open. Members of the org receive a pushMessages item whose shortMessage is exactly the message; the rep does not.

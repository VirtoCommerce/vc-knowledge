On POST /graphql/sales-rep with a plain rep token, Query.salesRepActivities returned identical categoryCounts for categories null, [], ["searches"], ["orders"], ["productViews"], ["logins"], ["customers"] and ["bogus"] in the same period; an unknown category returns totalCount 0 and no items without an error. totalCount equals the sum of the requested categories' counts. For searches/productViews/logins a count is the number of GA hour-bucket rows, not the number of events: 5 search rows whose per-row count summed to 7 gave searches=5. take=51 and take=1000 both returned 50 items; take negative returned 0 items; skip 500/501/520 returned 0 items while totalCount and categoryCounts stayed unchanged; negative skip is treated as 0. Every returned row's organizationId was within the rep's served organizations, the orders category only held orders whose customerId is the rep user, and the customers category returns one row per served organization dated at the membership createdDate. An organizationId the rep does not serve, or a storeId neither the rep's own nor trusting it, returns null for salesRepActivities, salesRepCustomerActivitySummary and salesRepCustomerInsights with no errors; a store whose trustedGroups lists the rep's store is allowed.

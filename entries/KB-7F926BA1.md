On theme 2.60.0-pr-2523-466f (Return 3.1005.0-pr-28-fb4f), Chromium 375x812, with a document-start PerformanceObserver: clicking a return card on /account/returns replaces the list with the details page's short loading shell (footer jumps up into view about 37 ms after the click), then GetReturn arrives and the taller content pushes the footer, decorative svg and the non-fixed spinner down. On an unthrottled connection all of these shifts fall inside the 500 ms hadRecentInput window, so CLS counts none of them (list page total 0.0398) even though the footer visibly flashes. Under Fast 3G emulation GetReturn landed 649 ms after the click and the swap shift of 0.2393 was scored. A cold load of the same details page (deep link or reload) scores 0.24-0.28 regardless.

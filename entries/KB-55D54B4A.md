As a sales rep serving two test organizations, with 18 own tasks spread over priorities, due dates, completion and links (customer-linked, CustomerOrder-linked, unlinked), on /graphql/sales-rep: the stand offers exactly three task filter rules, upcoming, overdue and completed. Each rule's answer contained every priority and the CustomerOrder-linked tasks, so no rule restricts priority or linked record type. salesRepTasks(filter, priorities, customerIds, period, keyword) returned the intersection of the single-filter answers in all 33 combinations tried and 27 rule+single pairs: a priority absent from a rule's set gave 0, customerIds never returned CustomerOrder-linked or unlinked tasks even when the order carried that organization's id. filter "nope" returned 0 tasks, "UPCOMING" equalled "upcoming", and an empty filter "" returned all 18.

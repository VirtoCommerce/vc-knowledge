Read window.dataLayer (gtag 'event' entries) as a guest, Chromium 1920, theme 2.61.0-pr-2598-9b6d-9b6db3d9 against the vcst_qa API, store B2B-store. /printers: view_item_list category_printers_page_1 (13 items, index 0..12). Title clicks at positions 0, 2 and 6 pushed select_item whose single item had index 0, 2 and 6, currency USD and item_list_id/item_list_name equal to the event's; the event payload had no top-level index. Scrolling to the end loaded page 2 and pushed a second view_item_list category_printers_page_2 with 8 items, index restarting at 0..7. With page 2 loaded, clicking a page-1 product pushed select_item with category_printers_page_1 and its page-1 index (9); clicking a page-2 product pushed category_printers_page_2 and its page-2 index (5). Header search 'epson' 3rd result: search_bar, index 2. PDP 'Customers bought together' 2nd card: index 1. Home products_block 3rd card: index 2. /compare 2nd column: compare_products, index 1. After switching to EUR the category list changed (11 items) and a click on the item at EUR-list index 4 reported index 4 and currency EUR. Earlier builds (pr-2570, pr-2598-fd57) carried no index on select_item items.

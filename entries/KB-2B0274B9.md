On a vc-frontend 2.59 build, opening the Barcode scan modal from the header search bar (desktop 1920 px and the 390 px mobile overlay) in a browser with no camera device showed the video skeleton and a spinner on the Browse button indefinitely (still after 10 s). The dialog's accessibility tree held button "Cancel" and an unnamed disabled button (Browse). No error text or notification appeared and the console held no warning or error. In source, loading is cleared only by the video canplaythrough event and startCamera's catch only logs, while Browse is bound to :loading, so the upload-a-photo fallback is unreachable without a camera. Only Cancel closes the modal.

On vcst with SalesRep 3.1028.0-pr-40-87bf: rep served orgs D and E and opened the /company/my-customers page 'Send message' popup once. ADMIN then changed the rep's served set to E and N (PUT /api/sales-rep). Without a reload, My customers listed E and N, but the popup's Customers dropdown still offered D (removed) and E, and searching N gave 'No results found'. Picking D and E and sending with Push was refused (toast 'The message could not be sent. Please try again.', nothing delivered per the admin push-message search) and the page behind the popup switched to /403. After a hard reload (Ctrl+Shift+R) the popup offered E and N and not D, so the options are fetched once per page load and reused.

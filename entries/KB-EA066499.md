Read in vc-module-pagebuilder at commit 5e6ccbf (PR #179, build 3.1041.0-pr-179-5e6c): MultilanguagePageService.AfterSaveChangesAsync, for every modified root, loads its language versions and calls MultilanguagePageInheritance.Apply(root, group). The inheritable fields are Permalink, Visibility, UserGroups, OrganizationId, StartDate and EndDate; a field in the version's override mask is not changed. When Apply changes a version, the service saves it through groupedPageContentService.SaveGroupSettingsAsync. Neither step calls IPageBuilderPublishConflictService. The conflict check runs only in grouped/publishing (PublishDraftAsync) and in MultilanguagePageService.PublishAll (PublishLanguageVersionAsync). Not observed live: this does not show whether a PUBLISHED version's live page row takes the new permalink or audience at once, so it does not show that two equally specific pages end up live.

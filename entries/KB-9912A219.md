It deletes nothing. The storefront's Delete member action calls removeMemberFromOrganization and removes the organization membership and nothing else: the row leaves the roster, but in Admin the contact still exists with its status unchanged, its security account still exists, is still Locked or Unlocked exactly as before, and STILL HOLDS the organization role it was given - an org-scoped role now assigned to an account that belongs to no organization. On the contact blade the only thing that changed is that 'Member of company(ies)' and 'Default company' are empty. Two consequences. First, removal-from-organization is invisible from the storefront, because the only representation of it is the row's absence; there is no removed state to display and no way to see a former member. Second, the email address stays taken by the surviving account, so re-inviting the same address later is not the clean slate it looks like. The storefront cannot delete the account or the contact, which means a maintainer cannot create the deleted state at all - only the detached one.

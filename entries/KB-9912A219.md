It deletes nothing. The storefront's Delete member action calls removeMemberFromOrganization; the contact keeps its status and its security account survives, Locked or Unlocked exactly as before, with the email address still taken - re-inviting the same address is not a clean slate, and the storefront cannot delete the account or the contact at all. What happens to the membership depends on the build.

On the vcptcore_stable generation (Customer 3.1011, ProfileExperienceApi 3.1009; observed live 2026-10-08, and in September on the earlier build): the membership row is removed, contact.organizations becomes empty ('Member of company(ies)' and 'Default company' empty in Admin) and the row leaves the roster.

On the vcst_qa generation (Customer 3.1029, ProfileExperienceApi 3.1020; observed live 2026-10-08): the membership row is KEPT with status "Deleted" (its role kept), contact.organizations STILL contains the organization, and the removed contacts are still returned by xAPI organization(id){contacts} and by the REST member search under that organization.

A role held on the ORGANIZATION MEMBERSHIP leaves with it; a role assigned GLOBALLY on the account (no membership row) stays on the account after removal on both generations - an org-scoped role on an account that belongs to no organization.

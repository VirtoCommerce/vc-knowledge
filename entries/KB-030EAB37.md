On vcst B2B-store (USD, Stores.TaxCalculationEnabled=true, FixedRate tax provider 20%), a product with list=12.5549 and sale=6.52 and no promotion returns, via Query.product: list 12.55, sale 6.52, saleWithTax 7.82, discountAmount 6.03, actual 6.52, actualWithTax 7.83. The same product added to a cart (Mutation.addItem) returns placedPriceWithTax 7.82. So the catalog shows a price incl. tax 1 cent higher than the cart and than sale*rate. Cause, read in source: ExpProduct.ApplyStaticDiscounts / ApplyRewards and RewardExtensions build DiscountAmount from (ListPrice - SalePrice).Amount, which is rounded to the currency's decimals, while ActualPriceWithTax = ListPriceWithTax - DiscountAmountWithTax works on unrounded InternalAmount: (12.5549 - 6.03) * 1.2 = 7.8299 rounds to 7.83. It only happens when the list price has more than 2 decimals; whether it shows up also depends on the tax rate. The tier price discount line has the same pattern.

Signed-in buyer, xAPI. addItem of a configurable parent (base 200) with a Variation section option Red (sale 45) gave price 245. removeConfigurationItem with lineItemId and configurationSection {sectionId, type:"Variation", option:{productId: Red}} left 0 configuration items and price 200. updateConfigurationItem with option Blue (80) then gave exactly one item (Blue) and price 280. The cart aggregate looks up an existing item by Type+SectionId+ProductId for Variation sections ("multiple variations can exist"), and by Type+SectionId for Product/Text/File sections. A different variation productId therefore never matches and is added, while Product sections are replaced. The keying was introduced with Variation-type support and has been unchanged since. Read it as the designed semantics, not a defect. The xAPI docs have no page for updateConfigurationItem(s).

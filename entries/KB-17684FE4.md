At the Admin SPA route #/ (Home), with a rule hiding the "Alerts and notifications" widget (shell-alerts) from the roles Order manager and CSR, the administrator's Home still shows "Alerts and Notifications". The editor's in-page help text says administrators see every widget a role rule hides. "Customize for everyone" (route #/?customize=1) shows the banner "Customizing Home for everyone", a "Reset users' own layouts" checkbox, Cancel and "Save for everyone"; hiding is allowed in that mode.

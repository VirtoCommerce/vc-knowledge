No. Observed on a build carrying x-catalog PR 113 and vc-frontend PR 2501 with barcode fields configured. The storefront escapes only backslash and double quote before building barcode:"<value>", and the search provider still interprets * and ? inside the quoted term. /search?barcode=<first 10 of 13 GTIN digits>* returned totalCount 1 and the single-hit redirect opened that product's page; /search?barcode=<GTIN with one digit replaced by ?> also opened it; /search?barcode=* listed 3,722 products under 'Your search for barcode *'. A scan or a typed partial code containing a wildcard therefore opens or lists products that do not carry that exact code.

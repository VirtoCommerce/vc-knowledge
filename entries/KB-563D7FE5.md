On theme 2.59 the /account/quotes/:id page renders the Price per item column from listPrice: quote-line-items.vue passes actual-price=selectedTierPrice.price but not show-placed-price, so VcLineItem shows listPrice. The line Total is selectedTierPrice.price x quantity. A quote with list 21.00 and proposal 18.90 x 10 shows 21.00 and total 189.00. The summary shows Subtotal as list x qty (totals.subTotalExlTax 330.00) and the proposal difference as Discount (adjustmentQuoteExlTax 30.30); the line-items footer subtotal is 299.70.

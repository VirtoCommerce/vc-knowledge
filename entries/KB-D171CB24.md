Signed in as a B2B organization maintainer on B2B-store, cart with two simple products ($215 x1, $200 x1; subtotal $415, header badge 2). Clicking 'Save for later' on the $200 line sent mutation MoveToSavedForLater (moveToSavedForLater, command.lineItemIds=[line id]) with errors[] empty; the response returned cart {itemsQuantity 1, items [the $215 line], subTotal 215} and list {itemsQuantity 1, items [the $200 product]}. The UI removed the line from its vendor group, rendered a 'Saved for later' block (name, price, 'Move to cart', 'See all'), badge 1, Order summary Subtotal $215.00. State persisted across a page reload. Clicking 'Move to cart' sent MoveFromSavedForLater (moveFromSavedForLater) with errors[] empty, returning cart itemsQuantity 2, subTotal 415, list itemsQuantity 0; the 'Saved for later' block disappeared and the badge went back to 2.

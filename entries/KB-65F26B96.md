Two password-grant sessions (s1, s2) of one Customer. s2 called Mutation.deleteSignInKey(signInMethodName Passkey, keyId) -> succeeded true. Afterwards s1's access token still answered Query.signInMethods (200, key gone from the list) and REST currentuser (200), and s1's refresh_token grant issued a new token (200). The REST sibling, per KB-4E12E506, ends other sessions; the xAPI door does not.

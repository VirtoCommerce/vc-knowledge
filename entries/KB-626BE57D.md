On Customer 3.1033.0-pr-325-9487 + Profile xAPI 3.1024.0-pr-151-b010 (Platform 3.1094.0), disposable contacts/organizations created via POST /api/members with address Line1 of 128, 129 and 2048 chars, Line2 of 2048, and an organization with Line1=Line2=2048 all returned 200 and read back untruncated; PUT /api/members raising Line1 to 1000 returned 204 and stored 1000. Line1 or Line2 of 2049 returned 400 with body {"addresses[0].Line1":["The length of 'Line1' must be 2048 characters or fewer. You entered 2049 characters."]} on both POST and PUT. xAPI updateMemberAddresses accepted Line1 300/2048 and Line2 2048 (admin token) and Line1 500 for a storefront buyer on their own contact; Line1 2049 returned code VALIDATION with the same length message. Other address limits are unchanged (xAPI City 129 still VALIDATION, max 128). Identical results on 3 consecutive runs. The same script on the pre-fix build (Customer 3.1029.0 / Profile xAPI 3.1020.0) returned 500 from 129 chars up and xAPI VALIDATION above 256 — see KB-7317D180. The vc-frontend storefront address form still caps Line1/Line2 at 128 client-side.

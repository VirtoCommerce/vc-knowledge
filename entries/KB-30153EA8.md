On Return 3.1005.0-pr-28-fb4f with theme 2.60.0-pr-2523-466f, a contact who is a member of two organizations and holds xapi:my_organization:return:view through a global role switched the account-menu organization to organization B. The organization tab then listed only organization B's returns, but a deep link to a submitted return of organization A still rendered its details: Requested by, Purchase order reference and the comment, without Cancel or Edit. The list follows the selected organization; the details read does not.

Signed-in personal user, theme 2.61.0-alpha.2605, Firefox. Adding 7 products to compare from /catalog (1 printer, 1 soda, 5 chairs in category 'Aliexpress'): each add shows a toast 'is added to compare list (items left - N)'; after the 5th chair it reads 'items left - 0'. A 6th chair add (from the catalog card and from its PDP) shows the toast 'Only 5 products from the same category can be compared' and is not added. The header Compare link badge counts all categories (7 with 3 categories), unchanged by the rejected add. /compare shows one tab per category ('Laser Printers 1', 'Soda 1', 'Aliexpress 5'), an All/Differences switch, 'Clear all' (opens a confirm dialog with OK/Cancel) and 'Clear category', and 'Remove from compare' per column; removing a column decrements the badge. There is no floating compare bar and no checkboxes on cards.

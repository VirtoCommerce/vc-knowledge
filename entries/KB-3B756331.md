Edge 1920x1080, signed-out browser, opened /account/impersonate/{userId} for a sales-rep user. The page 'Security verification' / 'Confirm your identity to continue' / 'You are logging in on behalf of a customer. The session will be audited.' shows fields 'Email*' (placeholder 'Enter your email') and 'Password*', buttons 'Verify and continue' and 'Cancel', plus Entra ID / Google / Passkey sign-in. Typing the platform administrator's login name (a plain user name, not an email address) into Email and the password into Password marks Email invalid with 'Enter a valid email address, e.g. johndoe@gmail.com' and leaves 'Verify and continue' disabled; nothing is sent. So an operator account whose login name is not an email cannot use this re-prompt with a password; it needs an operator with an email-shaped login (or the external/passkey buttons). Whether the backend would accept a user name is not tested, since the client never submits.

On theme 2.60.0-pr-2523-4263 (Chromium, Coffee preset), signed in as a B2B user, the Marketing group of the account sidebar renders 'Missions & challenges' and 'Coupons & promotions' in a 124 px wide .vc-menu-item__content between 768 and 1279 px: each wraps onto two lines and splits mid-word ('challeng' / 'es', 'promoti' / 'ons'). Seen identically on /account/returns?scope=organization and /account/orders at 1024 px, so it is the shared account shell and not the returns page. At 1280 px and wider and at 375 px (sidebar hidden in the drawer) no label wraps. No overflow, the text stays inside the sidebar card.

Signed in as a sales rep, Chrome. (1) Delaying only the GraphQL request with operationName SalesRepMonthlyOrderStatistics by 12 s (page.route) and opening /company/my-customers/<org GUID>: the block rendered with the period tabs, and a spinner over the KPI tile group, the Monthly sales chart and the Average order value chart; under the translucent overlay the tiles read $0, 0, —, $0 and the chart captions "Average $0 / closed month" and "AOV — over the period" (also exposed in the accessibility tree; no aria-busy or role=status on the block). When the response landed the real figures replaced them. The header KPI tiles (Purchased MTD, Orders placed YTD, Avg order value) are NOT fed by that operation and render immediately. (2) Fulfilling only that operation with HTTP 500: the block kept its header, Open in Reporting link and period tabs and showed an illustration with "Couldn't load the report. Try again later."; header tiles, My recent orders (filters still work), Top sellers, Available coupons, Quick actions, Customer information and Customer activity all rendered; console shows only the 500 resource error and Apollo warnings, no uncaught exception. Removing the route and reloading recovered the figures. (3) The selected period is stored in localStorage key vc-sales-rep-customer-sales-period (values seen: 12m, year). Setting it to an invalid value (banana) and reloading rendered Current year with full figures and no console error; the invalid value stays in storage untouched until the user picks a period.

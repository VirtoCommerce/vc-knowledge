On vcst_qa (theme 2.62.0-alpha.2647), as TechFlow maintainer: the Filters panel Role list showed Store manager / Organization employee / Purchasing agent / Organization maintainer / Sales Representative. After appending org-manager to the organization's Organization.Roles (PUT /api/organizations, full body) and reloading, 'Organization manager' was added to the list, and filtering by it returned every contact (all inherit the org-level role) including a fixture whose own membership role was org-employee. 'Store manager' stays listed although no contact in the org holds it (xAPI rolesInOrganization and securityAccounts.roles); applying it alone gives the generic empty state 'There are no results found' with 'Reset search'. The Status facet 'Blocked' is sent as statuses:['Locked'].

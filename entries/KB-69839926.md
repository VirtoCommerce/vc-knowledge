Member searches, product views and sign-ins were made on the B2B-store storefront 17:29-17:45 UTC on 2026-10-19. At ~9 h (02:30 UTC 2026-10-20) none were visible (KB-87F24950). At 18:10 UTC on 2026-10-20 (~24.5 h), a plain rep token on POST /graphql/sales-rep (and identically on POST /graphql) read Query.salesRepActivities for day ranges not read before: the 2026-10-19 17:00Z hour bucket now held the planted member searches (count 1 each), product views with product name, code and image, and logins; the newest GA bucket was 2026-10-20T05:00Z, and a period starting 2026-10-20T00:00Z returned searches/productViews/logins all 0. The admin POST /api/sales-rep/analytics-diagnostics processedData line went from search=1 (last 7 days) to search=11 over the same interval. So the GA Data API surfaced this traffic somewhere between 9 h and 24.5 h after it was sent; a row missing within 24 h is processing lag, and the current property-local day is not returned at all until GA processes it. Not every planted event surfaced at 24.5 h (one member's search and product view from the same hour were still absent), so per-row lateness beyond 24 h also occurs or the row is lost.

In the B2B storefront the organization role gates CONTROLS, not ROUTES. An Organization employee still gets the Corporate navigation group and still loads /company/members, where the full roster - every colleague's name, email and role - renders read-only; what the role removes is the 'Invite members' button and the entire per-row Actions column (Edit role / Block user / Delete), and on /account/orders the 'All orders / My orders' toggle, leaving only the member's own orders. So a section being visible in the nav says nothing about what the member may do in it, and 'the nav is hidden' is never a safe assumption to build a permission test on - assert on the absence of the specific control instead.

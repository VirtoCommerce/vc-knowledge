In Storybook story components-organisms-vcstatwidget--basic (card 256px wide, label, value, description and delta set) the card is 155.5px high when ready, 105.5px with loading=true (dash + spinner, description and delta not rendered) and 92px with errorText set (error row only). A standalone card therefore moves following content by 50px (loading to ready) or 63.5px (error to ready) when data resolves. Inside the Row story the cards stay level only because flex stretch gives every card the tallest sibling's height.

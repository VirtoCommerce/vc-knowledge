Firefox, rep serving 5 organizations (4 seeded plus its RepOnly org). /company/my-customers listed 5 rows on one page. On /company/my-customers/{id} Schedule call opened the popup, which loaded options with Query.salesRepCustomers (operation SalesRepCustomerOptions, storeId B2B-store, first 100, keyword '', sort name:asc), totalCount 5. The Customer field is a typeable combobox (Clear + Toggle dropdown); its listbox offered exactly those 5 names, alphabetically, the page's organization selected. Typing the full name of an existing organization the rep does not serve showed one disabled option 'No results found'; typing 'Tech' narrowed to the served TechFlow org. Typing sent no request: the filter runs client-side over the loaded options. Escape twice (list, then popup) closed it with no createSalesRepTask.

The catalog search routes are backed by the search index, which lags behind writes and returns nothing at all while a full rebuild reindex is running. A lookup-then-create step that relies on search can therefore miss an entity created moments earlier and create a duplicate. The database truth is GET /api/catalog/products/{id}, whose outlines also show whether the product is reachable through the store's virtual catalog - and a product is only visible on the storefront once its category is linked into that virtual catalog.

On vcst_qa B2B-store /catalog and /drinks-and-food/soft-drinks/mineral-water, Query.products range_facets returns both the buckets (ranges[] with label/count/from/to) and statistics{min,max}. The sidebar shows every range facet that has statistics min/max (price with 46 buckets and Volume_ml with one) as the same control: a slider with two number inputs, no bucket labels and no bucket checkboxes. Source: vc-frontend products-filters.vue picks SliderFilter when facet statistics min/max are numbers and theme setting range_filter_type is slider (default in settings_data.json), otherwise FacetFilter with a checkbox per bucket. The buckets feed the slider as histogram columns. With one product (value 0.1, min = max) the slider scale is floor(0.1)..ceil(0.1) = 0..1. So bucket labels are not expected in slider mode; a test asserting clickable bucket labels for a Range facet is wrong unless range_filter_type is default.

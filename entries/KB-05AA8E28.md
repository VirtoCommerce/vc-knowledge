On vcst_qa (theme 2.63.0), signed in as a member of an organization whose white-labeling record names the Coffee preset, 1920x1080 light mode, keyboard Tab: every focused element (logo link, main-menu link, footer link, language selector button) shows a 2px solid outline rgb(68,125,156) = #447d9c with 2px offset, matched by :focus-visible. On white backgrounds that is 4.51:1. The first button on the page (language selector in the header top bar) and the first footer link sit on #3d2b24 (Coffee color_header_top_bg / color_footer_top_bg = color_primary_800), where the ratio is 2.97:1, below the WCAG 1.4.11 3:1 minimum for a focus indicator. Coffee and Watermelon both set color_accent_500 #447d9c in the bundled preset JSON.

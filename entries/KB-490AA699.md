A grep of the whole vc-module-quote source at tag 3.1005.0 finds ExpirationDate only in the QuoteRequest model, the entity mapping, EF migrations, the QuoteType GraphQL field and the admin quote-detail blade, whose date picker sets min-date="today". There is no validator, no scheduled job and no status change tied to the date, and the Quotes.Status setting has no Expired value (live on vcst: Processing, On hold, New, Declined, Proposal sent, Ordered). So the date is informational: a quote past it stays in its status and remains approvable through Mutation.approveQuoteRequest.

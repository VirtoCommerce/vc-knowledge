On vcst_qa (CustomerReviews 3.1008.0-pr-86-6eca), for a product with 13 reviews (10 Approved, 2 New, 1 Rejected per admin POST /api/customerReviews/search), Query.customerReviews(storeId, entityId, entityType Product, filter "reviewStatus:New" or "reviewStatus:Rejected") returned the 10 Approved reviews and no others, both anonymously and with a buyer token (3 runs). filter "rating:[4 TO 5]" returned 5 (= Approved with rating 4-5 per admin), a keyword alone narrowed to the 1 matching review. On pre-fix CustomerReviews 3.1008.0 (vcptcore) reviewStatus:New returned the New review and reviewStatus:Rejected the Rejected one, to anonymous callers too.

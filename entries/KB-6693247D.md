As a Sales Representative, createSalesRepTask with dueDate "2026-10-23T19:30:00Z" returned dueDate "2026-10-23T19:30:00Z"; salesRepTask(id) returned the same instant on both /graphql/sales-rep and the main /graphql, and the admin GET /api/task-management/{id} carried the same value. In a browser at UTC-06:00, /company/tasks with Friday Oct 23 picked on the Due dates calendar sent SalesRepTasks to POST /graphql (the main endpoint, not /graphql/sales-rep) with period {from:"2026-10-23T06:00:00.000Z", to:"2026-10-24T05:59:59.999Z"} and showed the row line "Due Oct 23, 2026, 1:30 PM · <customer> · Call". A second call at "2026-10-24T03:30:00Z" (UTC Oct 24, local Oct 23 9:30 PM) was listed under Oct 23 as "Due Oct 23, 2026, 9:30 PM", while Oct 24 listed only a call at 16:00Z as 10:00 AM. The task counts and markers queries (SalesRepTaskCounts, SalesRepTaskMarkers) also go to POST /graphql.

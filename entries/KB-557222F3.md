On Tax 3.1008.0-pr-61, CatalogPersonalization 3.1007.0-pr-93 and CatalogPublishing 3.1008.0-pr-82 (PR builds, not released), observed 2026-10-13: anonymous requests to POST /api/taxes/search, GET /api/taxes/{id}, POST /api/taxes/{storeId}/evaluate, PUT /api/taxes, GET /api/personalization/taggeditem/{id}(/tags/count), POST /api/personalization/search, PUT /api/personalization/taggeditem, POST /api/personalization/outlines/synchronize and synchronization/cancel, GET /api/completeness/evaluators and POST /api/completeness/channels/{id}/products/evaluate return 401 with no Location header and an empty body (3 runs). On the earlier builds the tax search, personalization search and evaluators endpoints returned 200 anonymously. For a non-administrator Manager: tax reads need tax:read and PUT /api/taxes needs tax:update (both registered in permission group Tax); personalization reads accept any of catalog:read, catalog:categories:read or catalog:products:read, and a catalog-scoped assignment passes whatever catalog the item belongs to; personalization writes need personalization:update; completeness evaluators and products/evaluate need channel:read. Without them the answer is 403. Bare /api/completeness returns 404 for every verb, anonymous or admin.

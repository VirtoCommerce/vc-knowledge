2026-11-10 19:20-19:26Z, signed-in B2B buyer, PKCE token for /ucp/mcp, flat checkout_and_handoff on one cart. A shipping_address carrying id = the shipment's delivery_address.id (as returned by get_cart; the cart's addresses[] did not hold that id) fails every time: raw XCart envelope 'Error trying to resolve field addOrUpdateCartAddress' code INVALID_OPERATION, server log InvalidOperationException at EF IdentityMap.ThrowIdentityConflict (operation UcpAddOrUpdateShippingAddress). Source: UcpCartService.WithAddressId keeps a caller-supplied id; XCart AddOrUpdateCartAddressCommandHandler maps it into a new cart address with that Key, and CartAggregate.AddOrUpdateCartAddress (unlike AddOrUpdateCartAddressByTypeAsync and AddPaymentAsync) does not reset Key, so two address entities share one primary key. After such a failure, the same call that had succeeded minutes earlier (address without id, so UCP reuses the cart's own shipping address id) also failed 3/3, even after a successful update_cart in between; all 5 failures were logged on one platform instance and none on the other. A checkout_and_handoff with no address, or with a new address while no call had failed, succeeded. The instance-local persistence is observed, its cache layer not proven.

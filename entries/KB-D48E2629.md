On a PDP with a 6-product 'Customers bought together' block (markup-tracked, data-list-id recommended_products_bought-together), Chrome: cold load at 1920 pushed view_item_list with 6 items; at 390 the cold load pushed 4 items (only 4 cards rendered). Resizing 1920->800->1920 on one page pushed two extra view_item_list events (5 items, then 6 items again) with the same list id and related_id. A cart quantity update on the same PDP (update_cart_item) re-sent neither view_item nor view_item_list. The pre-markup implementation sent the full recommended array once per change of the array (source), so impression count and size now depend on viewport.

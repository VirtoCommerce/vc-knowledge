Guest, Chromium, cache-busted full loads, CLS read by a buffered PerformanceObserver('layout-shift') after about 7 s, no input, no scroll. Vintage Wedding cake (7 sections, 5 visible checklist rows): 1920x1080 CLS 0.1015 on 3/3 loads, 1280x900 CLS 0.0996 on 3/3 loads (deterministic to 4 decimals). Every load has the same shifts: about 0.014 (1920) / 0.032 (1280) when GetProduct renders and pushes the in-viewport page footer down, then the dominant 0.0867 (1920) / 0.0654 (1280) about 20-30 ms after GetProductConfigurations returns: footer#footer moves out of the viewport (pushed by the 'Configure the parameters' sections in the main column) and div.vc-widget__footer-container of the Price and delivery widget (the action-icons row) moves down 228 px (y 554 to 782 at 1920; 550 to 778 at 1280) because ul.product-configuration-checklist (212 px + 16 px margin) is inserted above it. The checklist template is v-if on its computed items, which are empty until the configuration loads, so it has no placeholder. From the layout-shift formula the widget-footer part of the dominant shift is about 0.015 at 1920 and 0.031 at 1280; without it 1920 would be about 0.087. AGENT-TEST-Wedding-Cake-Cond (2 checklist rows, 94 px, widget footer moves 110 px) measured 0.0623 on 3/3 loads at 1920, and a simple PDP 0.0363 (footer push only).

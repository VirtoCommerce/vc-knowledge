To actually delete someone after a storefront Delete member, delete the account under Security > Users and the contact under Contacts.

On vcptcore_stable on 2026-09-12 (platform 3.1007.x, Customer 3.1000.x) that remedy was not available for one case: after an un-accepted invitation was walked through Edit role, Block, Unblock and Delete, the surviving account was absent from POST /api/platform/security/users/search (by keyword, by memberId and unfiltered), so the grid could not show it, and DELETE /api/platform/security/users?names= failed with ConcurrencyFailure on every attempt, while control accounts behaved normally (see KB-4A8606CA). That build is no longer deployed anywhere.

On current builds the same walk-through (invite -> change role -> lock -> unlock -> remove) leaves an account that search finds by keyword and by memberId and that DELETE /api/platform/security/users?names= removes (succeeded true, GET returns null) - observed live 2026-10-08 on vcst_qa and on the upgraded vcptcore_stable, for an invitee, a membership-role member and a global-role member.

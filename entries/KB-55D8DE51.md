To actually delete someone, delete the account under Security > Users and the contact under Contacts. That remedy is not always available: after an un-accepted invitation was walked through Edit role, Block, Unblock and Delete, the surviving account could not be deleted under Security > Users - it is absent from POST /api/platform/security/users/search by keyword, by memberId and unfiltered, so the grid cannot show it at all, and DELETE /api/platform/security/users?names= fails with ConcurrencyFailure on every attempt. Control accounts in the same store behave normally; see KB-4A8606CA.

Multi-org contact (12 orgs) on B2B-store. Own PRIVATE lists (organizationId null, no sharingSettings) showed identically under three active orgs. The same user's own list with sharingSettings scope=Organization and organizationId=<org X> appeared on /account/lists and in the sidebar ONLY while org X was active; under two other active orgs it was absent. Under another org, lists of OTHER members shared to that org appeared (one with the Shared badge). So only private lists are user-scoped; an Organization-scope list is bounded to its org for the owner too, not only for other members.

Guest, Edge 1920, Page Builder HTML page with a data-vc-track product-list whose cards carry only data-product-name/price. These did not add events: scrolling to the end of the page (End key), opening the header All products menu (shown with v-show, so only the style changes), and 10 s idle. These did add events: opening the Currency dropdown (+1), typing 4 to 7 characters into the header search while its suggestions dropdown renders (+8 to +10), and closing that dropdown with Escape (+4). The load-time count alone ranged from 1 to 7 across three fresh loads with no input. A list whose cards carry data-product-id stayed at 1 throughout. This matches a MutationObserver on body with childList+subtree and an attributeFilter limited to data-vc-track and the rule data-* attributes.

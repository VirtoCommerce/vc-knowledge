No. Measured on vcst-qa 2026-09-19 on fixture AGENT-TEST-TfOnlyStatus BE (contact 1330aead-4419-42be-a412-56dba4c81965) in org 96f109a7-9010-4691-b6a1-bef25cca3d04, membership unlocked and membership status "Approved". POST /api/members with the contact's status set to "Locked" read back as status="Locked" on the contact. In xAPI organization.contacts the contact then reports status="Locked" but statusInOrganization="Approved" and isLockedInOrganization=false, because the effective status resolves as membership.Status ?? contact.Status and the membership's own "Approved" wins. The storefront /company/members row still rendered the green "Active" badge. Restored to "Approved" afterwards. Consequence: the contact-level Locked value from the Customer.ContactStatuses dictionary is invisible on the storefront roster for any member whose membership carries its own status, which is the normal case. Whether contact-level Locked actually blocks that user's access was NOT tested (no password for the fixture), only that the roster does not surface it.

Store B2B-store with Loyalty.Mode = Mixed Cart and Loyalty.LoyaltyBalanceCalculationMode = Organization. A seeded zero-balance contact (GET /api/loyalty-program-operation-log/balance/user/{userId} = 0) who is a member of an organization whose pooled balance (GET .../balance/organization/{orgId}) is 688568 put 1 PTS line + 1 cash line in the cart; the cart carried organizationId = that org, and Query.cart validationErrors was [] with Place order enabled. Source at Loyalty tag 3.1009.0, LoyaltyCartValidator.cs:54-61: when store.IsOrganizationBalanceCalculationMode() and cart.OrganizationId is set, balance = GetOrganizationBalanceAsync(orgId), else GetUserBalanceAsync(cart.CustomerId); the error is raised only when balance < points total. So in Organization mode a personal zero balance cannot trigger the error; a test of the insufficient-balance guard needs Customer (user) mode, or a member of an org whose pool is below the points total.

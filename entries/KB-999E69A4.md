vcst_qa, theme 2.63.0-pr-2676, Firefox, 384x800, light and dark scheme. As a guest on B2B-store (store white-labeling enabled with a logo, Mercury preset): the header bar img alt "B2B-store" loads cms-content/assets/customization/logo_B2B-store_*.svg, while the main-menu drawer opened with the "Main menu" button (always a dark panel in both schemes) shows assets/logo-white-*.svg, the theme's bundled white Virto Commerce logo. Signed in as a buyer whose current organization has its own white-labeling (logo_organization-*), both the header and the drawer show the organization logo. So the drawer falls back to the theme's white logo when the effective white-labeling supplies no logo for dark backgrounds; whether the store record leaves that field empty was not read.

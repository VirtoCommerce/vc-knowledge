Signed-in B2B buyer on /cart, simple product line at quantity 1 (unit 59.99). The line quantity spinbutton was set to 234 in one input event and blurred with Tab. ChangeFullCartItemsQuantity returned 200 with errors[] empty in 0.7-1.4 s. The line total then showed 14,037.66 (234 x 59.99), so a wait for that text resolved; the very next read showed the line total back at 59.99 and the Order Summary subtotal at its old value; 3-8 s after the commit both settled on the new amounts (line 14,037.66, subtotal increased by 233 x 59.99). Seen in two separate setups in one firefox session (Playwright). A test that waits for the new line-total text and then reads totals can record the stale value; wait for the line total AND the subtotal to agree, or for the value to hold for a few seconds. Whether this is intended is unknown; not filed.

Signed in as an organization maintainer, Chromium 1920x1080, /company/members > Invite members. The emails field is a textarea with native required, no aria-label, named only by <label for=textarea-id> "Enter email addresses of people you want to invite*" (span.vc-label__asterisk). Theme 2.62.0-pr-2622: the ARIA snapshot names it 'Enter email addresses of people you want to invite*' (3 of 3 loads) and the asterisk span has no aria-hidden. Theme 2.62.0-pr-2586 (VCST-6215 fix, same backend): the name is 'Enter email addresses of people you want to invite' (3 of 3 loads), native required stays, and the asterisk carries aria-hidden=true while staying visible. The Role combobox in the same dialog carries aria-required=true on both builds; on this stand it is disabled with 'There are no roles available. Please contact your store administrator.', so Send stays disabled. axe-core 4.10.2 scoped to the dialog reports no violations on either build.

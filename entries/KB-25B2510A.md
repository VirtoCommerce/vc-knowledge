Signed in as administrator on 2026-10-23 and opened the Admin Shell Home. The Total Sales widget (Year) sent start=2025-10-24T00:00:00&end=2026-10-21T23:59:59 and a previous-period call 2024-10-24..2025-10-21; switching it to Month sent 2026-09-24..2026-10-21. Sales Trends (Month) sent one call per month whose end is the month's last day minus two: 2025-11-01..11-28, 2025-12-01..12-29, 2026-02-01..02-26, 2026-10-01..10-29; Quarter sent 2025-01-01..2026-12-29. Every end date is two days before the period's true end, so orders on the last two days of each month and on today and yesterday fall outside the widget totals. The parameters carry no timezone.

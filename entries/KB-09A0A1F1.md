Guest, de locale, 320x700 viewport. A category whose name is "111111111111111111111111111111 11111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111" (two unbroken tokens of 30 and 53 chars, test data) renders its H1 (h1.category__title, VcTypography) and breadcrumb without breaking the tokens: the document scrollWidth is 887 against innerWidth 320 and the page scrolls sideways; the results count is pushed off-screen. A 101-char multi-word name on the same stand wraps cleanly and the page stays 320 wide. Source at vc-frontend 2.59.0: the title span and VcTypography carry no overflow-wrap/break-words. The token here is synthetic; a real long compound word long enough to exceed ~272px at the H1 size would hit the same path. BL-UI-004 asks long content to wrap, truncate or scroll in its own container. Distinct from KB-06A3A50F (the nowrap results count).

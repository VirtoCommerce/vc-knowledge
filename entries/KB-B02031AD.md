Admin signed in to the embedded Sales Rep app (workspace route embedded-app/vc-sales-rep). Created a rep (POST /api/sales-rep 200), edited it (PUT /api/sales-rep 200), uploaded a document (POST /api/files/sales-rep-documents 200 then POST /api/sales-rep/documents 200), edited its metadata (PUT .../metadata 200), pinned/unpinned it (204) and deleted single and bulk (DELETE /api/sales-rep/documents?ids=... 204). A viewport screenshot taken immediately after each click and the accessibility snapshot showed no success toast in any case; the grid refreshed and the blade updated instead. Failures DO render a toast: a duplicate-email create (POST /api/sales-rep 400 'Username ... is already taken') and an invalid category (POST /api/sales-rep/documents 400 'Invalid category name') each showed a red toast plus a blade error banner. Same convention as the Tasks app (KB-0AF2E9CF). A case asserting a success toast after these actions does not match this build.

Production build of vc-frontend PR 2600 at a4f29534 with design soft, proxied to the vcst backend, Chromium 1920x1080. On a cold load of the home page, <head> holds the index.html preconnects and the Lato css2 stylesheet. It also holds exactly one runtime-added <link rel=stylesheet data-design-fonts href=https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:ital,wght@0,400..900;1,400..900&family=Geologica:wght@400..900&display=swap>. That is 2 stylesheet requests to fonts.googleapis.com: Lato at about 7 ms and Inter+Geologica at about 250 ms. Only the Inter and Geologica woff2 files are fetched, so the Lato faces stay unloaded. The body uses Inter, h2 uses Geologica. With the Inter stylesheet held 6 s, the page had rendered its header, hero, Daily Deals and footer (2,456 characters of text) by 3.6 s. The swap when the stylesheet arrived added a layout shift of only 0.0013 (home) and 0.0019 (PDP). With the stylesheet aborted, the page rendered fully in the generic sans-serif fallback (Arial on Windows). It had no horizontal overflow and no clipped text, and the console showed only the failed request: no warning from the app in this production build.

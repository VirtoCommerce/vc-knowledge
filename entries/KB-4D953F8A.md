On the VCST-5989 build (Seo 3.1006.0-alpha.101, Catalog 3.1049.0-pr-910), fixtures were made in a store's own catalog and explained in Admin (Stores > store > Debug SEO > Stage 0) and via REST. Code to text: NotInStoreCatalog = "The category or product isn't in the store's catalog"; StoreMismatch = "The SEO record is assigned to another store"; Inactive = "The SEO record is inactive"; ObjectInactive = "The category or product is inactive"; LanguageMismatch = "The SEO record's language matches neither the requested language nor the store's default language"; NoSeoPath = "The store can't build a link to it: a parent has no slug, or it's a linked category with Collapsed links"; PermalinkMismatch = "The store's link to it is different: <path>" (details holds the path). Multiple reasons are joined with '; '. NoSeoPath came along with StoreMismatch and with LanguageMismatch when that record was the only candidate, but not with Inactive or ObjectInactive. With Long links and two categories sharing a slug under different parents, requesting parent1/slug resolved the first and rejected the second with PermalinkMismatch; requesting the bare slug rejected both. A resolved record shows a check mark in Resolved and an empty reason.

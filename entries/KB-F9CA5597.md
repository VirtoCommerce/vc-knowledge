Rep signed in on the vc-frontend-next 3.2.0 theme with the SalesRep plugin header link; measured with getBoundingClientRect/getComputedStyle at 1920 and 1280 px. The link label span (class bottom-header-link__label) has max-width 128px, overflow hidden, white-space normal and line-height 14px; for a caption longer than 128 px the text wraps but the span box is 16 px tall (scrollHeight 28), so only the first line shows and there is no ellipsis or tooltip. Visible text: de 'Vertriebsmitarbeiter-' (full 'Vertriebsmitarbeiter-Hub'), fr 'Espace représentant' (full 'Espace représentant commercial'); it, pt, pl, es and ru are clipped the same way; en, en-GB, zh, ja and nl (English fallback) fit. The accessible name keeps the full caption. The hub link becomes 152x44 vs 42 px siblings and its icon centre sits 1 px above the row. Same at both widths, so it is not a breakpoint effect. Host labels in the row (e.g. 'Commande en gros', 'Mengenbestellung') are under 128 px and not affected.

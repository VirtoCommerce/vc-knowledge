Signed-in personal user, B2B-store, single-page cart. USD cart with QA-MULTICUR-001 (has a EUR price) and ALCE0128 (no EUR price). After switching the header currency to EUR (Mutation.changeCartCurrency, response validationErrors CART_PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE + PRODUCT_PRICE_INVALID, errors[] empty), the ALCE0128 line shows EUR0.00, a disabled stepper and 'The product is no longer available for purchase', the subtotal excludes it, Place order is disabled, AND the Order summary additionally shows the generic text 'Something went wrong. Please try again later.' under 'Complete all required information to proceed.'. No GraphQL errors[] and no console error accompany it. Removing the unavailable line makes the generic text disappear; the USD view of the same cart (no unavailable line) never shows it. So the generic text is the summary's rendering of the unavailable-line validation state, not a failed request.

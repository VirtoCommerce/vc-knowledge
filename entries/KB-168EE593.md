Sales rep on store B2B-store, Chromium. On /company/my-customers/{orgId} the left column starts with a 'Sales statistics' block (above My recent orders) holding the period tabs 6 months / 12 months / Current year / Q1-Q4 (Current year pressed by default), KPI tiles, the Monthly sales and Average order value charts and a footnote; there is no 'Show as table' control (the Reporting page has one). For one customer, tiles, chart badges and every bar tooltip matched /company/customer-sales?customer={orgId}&period=<key> exactly for year, 6m, 12m and q3 (both pages send SalesRepMonthlyOrderStatistics with that organizationId). The choice is stored in localStorage 'vc-sales-rep-customer-sales-period' (e.g. 'year', 'q4'): switching to another customer via My customers (SPA) and a full reload keep it; Reporting opened from the sidebar still starts on Current year with All my customers. The header link 'Open in Reporting' points to /company/customer-sales?customer={orgId}&period=<current key> and opens with that customer and period selected. Switching A->B showed no A figure under B (only loading-time $0 placeholders).

On /apps/admin-shell#/user-profile ("Changes are saved automatically"), toggling Show relative dates three times about 1.4 s apart sent three POST /api/platform/profiles/currentuser, all 204, each body carrying 6 Platform UserProfile settings (TimeZone, FullDateThreshold, Language, RegionalFormat, UseTimeAgo, FullDateThresholdUnit) with ids from the copy loaded by GET profiles/currentuser; MainMenu.State, ShowMeridian and the AdminShell Appearance/Theme rows were not in the body. A double-click on the switch sent no request. After a full reload the UI showed the last value. No 409 and no error toast was observed.

On virtostart (VirtoCommerce.Loyalty 3.1009.0, store Loyalty.Missions.Enable=true, LoyaltyBalanceCalculationMode=Customer), six Published missions with AnyUserGroupCondition were created, then nine orders were POSTed by admin with status Completed, customerId = the buyer's user id, currency = store default, no shipping/tax/discount. Within the polling window POST /api/loyalty-mission-progress/search returned, per buyer, exactly: OrderCountGoal currentValue = number of orders; OrderValueGoal currentValue = sum of order totals (not capped at the target: 5515/5000 on a completed one); PerSkuGoal (all=true) currentValue = units of the goal-item products bought, capped per item (6/15 for 6 of item1). Missions reaching the target went Completed and their FixedAmountReward appeared as an Earned row in loyaltyPointsHistory. Orders placed BEFORE a mission existed did not count toward it. Every Published mission on the store, including pre-existing ones, received the same contributions. Storefront loyaltyMissionProgress lists every Published mission for the buyer, with status InProgress and 0 percent for one never contributed to.

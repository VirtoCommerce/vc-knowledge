With Catalog.Search.EventBasedIndexation.Enable=false, products deleted and recreated with new ids kept their old documents in the Elasticsearch8 product index (observed 50+ minutes later). xAPI products hits and totalCount exclude them, but term_facets count them: a facet bucket reported 2 per product (6 for 3 hits), and the admin POST /api/catalog/search/products totalCount was 24 for 12 returned items. A facet on createddate shows the two docs apart. It does not reproduce on untouched catalog products. An incremental edit was also not picked up by the scheduled indexing job within 10 minutes; a targeted POST /api/search/indexes/index for the document id fixed that one document.

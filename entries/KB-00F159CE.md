Firefox 1440x1080, B2B-store, en-US. Signed in as a rep holding sales-rep:access who serves 1 organization and owns 0 tasks, and separately as a rep holding sales-rep:access as a global role with 0 served organizations and 0 tasks. For both, /company/dashboard rendered the Sales Rep hub rail with a 'Tasks' link (href /company/tasks) and the 'Tasks & due dates' widget whose 'All tasks' link points to /company/tasks (widget calendar '0 tasks'). Clicking the rail link opened /company/tasks with no redirect to /account/dashboard, 'New task' present and no error toast. The link and widget follow the sales-rep permission, not task count or organization membership; what the served-organization scope changes is the chip set on /company/tasks (Query.salesRepTaskFilterRules).

POST /api/seoinfos/search without storeId returns 500 'Store with ID not found'. With storeId and languageCode, the slug field returned no matches even for slugs known to exist; the permalink field works, but it is resolved as a path under the store's SEO link type. With Long links, a category under a top-level category matches only as parent-slug/child-slug, and a product only by its full path. The bare slug of a nested object returns nothing. A freshly written product SEO record is found immediately through its full path, so the search reads the database, not the index. GET /api/seoinfos/duplicates?objectId&objectType returned [] even for a category whose slug is shared with another category in the same store and language.

Opened two published Builder.io pages with a products block (/predefined-products at 1920 px, /product-section at 800 px) on a theme with design classic. The products-block heading is an h2 carrying the kit class vc-typography--variant--h1 inside .builder-block. Computed: 24px / weight 700 / line-height 32px at 1920 px and 20px / 700 / 28px at 800 px, text-transform none. The CSSOM shows the matching rules by layer: the kit h1 variant in vc.kit (1.5rem, 1.875rem at the breakpoint), the reset h1..h6 font-size inherit in base.tw, .builder-block h2 in vc.app (1.25rem, 1.5rem at lg), and the products-block scoped .vc-typography--variant--h1 text-transform none in vc.app. The vc.app layer is declared after vc.kit and base.tw before vc, so the Builder size wins and the heading is not flattened to inherited size.

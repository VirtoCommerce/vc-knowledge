The 'Active / Blocked' column on the storefront's /company/members is NOT a faithful read of whether the account is locked. Block user and Unblock user do round-trip correctly: blocking a member there flips the Admin account's 'Locked state' to Locked and unblocking flips it back to Unlocked, so the two surfaces are wired to the same flag when the storefront is the one writing it. But an account that is Locked in Admin without having been blocked through that dialog still renders as 'Active', with the green tick, to a maintainer looking at the member list. The maintainer is therefore shown a colleague as able to sign in when the platform will refuse them, and there is no second indicator anywhere on the page. Verify a member's real sign-in status on the account's own blade (Security > Users > account > Locked state), not from the storefront roster.

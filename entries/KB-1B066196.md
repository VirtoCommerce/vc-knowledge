Yes. After an admin PUT /api/sales-rep that changed the organizations list, the rep's previously valid storefront access token (password grant, 30 min lifetime, minted 5 minutes earlier) was treated as anonymous: me.userName returned Anonymous and wishlists / salesRepCustomers returned Unauthorized. A fresh password grant worked and showed the updated served set. A PUT that failed with 500 did not have this effect.

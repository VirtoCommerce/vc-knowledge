XCatalog 3.1022 (PR 113 build, observed on 3.1022.0-pr-113-f4a8 with Catalog 3.1046.0-pr-909-2839 and ElasticSearch8 3.1007.0): when the store has Catalog.Search.BarcodeSearchFields configured, barcode:"v" becomes an OR of term filters on those index fields, reported in filters[] with isGenerated:true and no barcode entry (the generated filter carries the raw value, e.g. gtin "2945100000*"); default scope widens to product+variation (explicit is:product keeps products only). Matching is case-insensitive but whitespace-sensitive (leading/trailing space -> 0) and not prefix. BUT * and ? are honoured as wildcards, in exact mode too: barcode:"*" on [code] returned 4714 products; on [gtin] (exact mode) the full 13-digit GTIN -> 1, "*" -> 313 (every product holding a gtin), first 12 digits + "*" -> 1, first 12 digits + "?" -> 1, first 12 digits without a wildcard -> 0; HTTP 200, no errors[]. The storefront /search?barcode=<10-digit prefix>* therefore redirects to that product's page, because it opens the PDP on a single hit. Cause: EvalBarcodeFilterMiddleware builds a TermFilter from the raw value, and the ElasticSearch8 filters builder turns any single-value term containing * or ? into a WildcardQuery; the filter-syntax grammar has no escape for * or ?. Tracked as VCST-6094. With no fields configured the term is left as a literal field named barcode (0 hits). A second barcode: term stays literal (AND -> 0); comma-separated values in one term OR together. scannerEnabled:false does not stop the expansion.

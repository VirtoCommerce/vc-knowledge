XCatalog 3.1022 (PR 113 build) with Elasticsearch8: when the store has Catalog.Search.BarcodeSearchFields configured, barcode:"v" becomes an OR of term filters on those index fields, reported in filters[] with isGenerated:true and no barcode entry; default scope widens to product+variation (explicit is:product keeps products only). Matching is case-insensitive but whitespace-sensitive (leading/trailing space -> 0) and not prefix. BUT * and ? are honoured as wildcards: barcode:"*" on [code] returned 4714 products, a GTIN prefix followed by * returned the product, ? substituted one character. With no fields configured the term is left as a literal field named barcode (0 hits). A second barcode: term stays literal (AND -> 0); comma-separated values in one term OR together. scannerEnabled:false does not stop the expansion.

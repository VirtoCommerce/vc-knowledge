The security-code field of the CyberSource card form on /cart is a CyberSource-hosted Flex Microform iframe, and the digits show in plain text while typing. The field is rendered by the processor's hosted script, not by storefront markup, and the unmasked rendering is accepted as expected behaviour - the requirement to mask it was retired. This covers only masking; it says nothing about when the order is created relative to card tokenization.

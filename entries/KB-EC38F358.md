A plain rep opened /company/my-customers/{id}/activities while the GA reads were failing (the dashboard and /company/activities had listed tracked rows minutes earlier; salesRepCustomerInsights then answered isAnalyticsAvailable:false in ~1.3 s, HTTP 200, no errors[]). The page showed tabs All (–), Orders (9), Customers (1), Searches (–), Product views (–), Logins (–); the All tab listed the 9 orders and the "New customer assigned" row, with the notice "Some tracked activity couldn't be loaded, so these figures may be incomplete." above the list; the Searches tab showed "Web activity isn't available for this store right now". The profile's Customer activity widget showed the same message in Searches and Product views, while its Summary (salesRepCustomerActivitySummary, isAnalyticsAvailable:true, possibly served from the 60-minute cache) still showed tracked values. No error page and no toast. The failure persisted past a 65 s wait; the cause on the stand was not isolated (a parallel lane may have lowered the report-request timeout).

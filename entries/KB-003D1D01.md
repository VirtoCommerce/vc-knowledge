In the Admin SPA Push Messages blade (New push message / Push message details, Audience section) the recipients combobox relabels itself with the mode. In 'Specific people or companies' it reads 'Add specific recipients (people or whole companies)'; in 'Match by conditions' and 'Advanced query' it reads 'Also add specific recipients (people or whole companies)'. The control is otherwise identical, with the same hint 'Adding a company includes everyone in it, and in the companies under it.' The 'Also' prefix marks union semantics: in the conditions modes what is picked is added ON TOP of the condition match rather than being the whole audience. A picked company survives the mode switch from 'Specific people or companies' into 'Match by conditions' with its chip and per-chip count intact, which is what makes the union observable (company alone 3 recipients, plus 'Customer type is Employee' 4). Observed on VirtoCommerce.PushMessages 3.1006.0-pr-28-8de4, commit 8de424a9.

On vcst_qa, theme 2.61.0-pr-2577-4c17 (design classic, store B2B-store with preset red), on the home page, a product page and /cart, anonymous and signed in: <html> carries lang, data-design="classic" and style, and no data-preset attribute in light or dark mode. All 131 runtime --color-* tokens equal the red preset's bundled light map (--color-primary-500 #e52121) and, after the header Theme toggle switches to dark (html.dark), all 131 equal the red dark map (--color-primary-500 #d34247); switching back restores the light values. The theme source removes the data-preset writer on purpose (the attribute had no reader).

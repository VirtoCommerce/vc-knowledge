On a placed order read with GET /api/order/customerOrders/{id} (vcst-qa, VirtoCommerce.Orders 3.1015.0), a single FixedRate/Ground shipment carried price 150, priceWithTax 180, taxTotal 30, taxPercentRate 0.2. The order header reported shippingTotal 150 and shippingTotalWithTax 180 — so the header itself implies 30 of shipping tax — yet shippingTaxTotal on the same header was 0. The shipping tax is nevertheless included in the header's aggregate taxTotal: subTotalTaxTotal 271.798 + shipment taxTotal 30 = 301.798, and taxTotal read 301.8. The grand total is unaffected and correct (subTotalWithTax 1630.79 + shippingTotalWithTax 180 = total 1810.79 = sum). The same header does NOT show this defect for goods: subTotalWithTax 1630.79 - subTotal 1358.99 = 271.80, matching subTotalTaxTotal 271.798. So shippingTaxTotal alone is unpopulated. Consequence: any report, integration or assertion that reads shippingTaxTotal to answer "how much tax was charged on shipping" gets 0 and is silently wrong; derive it from shippingTotalWithTax - shippingTotal, or from the shipment's own taxTotal, instead. Not to be confused with the separate, known and correct paymentTotal/PaymentIn.sum split described in KB-0DD47BD1.

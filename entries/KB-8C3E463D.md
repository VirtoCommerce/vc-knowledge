Admin formats an order timestamp in the timezone stored on the SIGNED-IN OPERATOR'S PROFILE when there is one: GET /api/platform/profiles/currentuser carries VirtoCommerce.Platform.UI.TimeZone beside UI.Language and UI.RegionalFormat, and the order blade and the orders grid render createdDate through it, printing no offset. With the profile set to America/New_York and the browser at UTC+4, an order created at 11:31:29Z showed "Oct 8, 2026 7:31:29 AM" on the blade and the grid (UTC-4), not the browser's 3:31 PM (vcptcore_stable, 2026-10-08; also in September). With UI.TimeZone null the browser's timezone is used ("3:31:15 PM" for 11:31:15Z at UTC+4 on vcst_qa and vcptcore_qa1). So two operators looking at one order can see two clock times according to a per-user setting neither of them is looking at, and neither matches the API: do not reconcile a screen time with a payload time by assuming the reader's timezone; open the profile first.

Two more traps on the same blade. The indexation widget prints the same kind of value in raw UTC ("Indexed Oct 8, 2026 11:31:32 am"), or as "Indexed a minute ago" when the profile's UseTimeAgo is on. And a cold deep link to the blade (loaded before the profile) falls back to the browser's timezone and, on vcptcore_stable, a ja-JP pattern ("2026年10月8日 PM3:31:29") - a different reading of the same order from the same operator.

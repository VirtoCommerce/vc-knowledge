subject: Admin renders order timestamps in the operator's profile timezone (UI.TimeZone), not the browser's
Admin formats an order timestamp in the timezone stored on the SIGNED-IN OPERATOR'S PROFILE, not the browser's: GET /api/platform/profiles/currentuser carries VirtoCommerce.Platform.UI.TimeZone alongside UI.Language and UI.RegionalFormat, and the order blade and the orders grid both render createdDate through it, printing no offset. Here the profile said America/New_York while the machine running the browser was Asia/Yerevan (UTC+4), so an order created at 2026-09-15T08:17:18Z displayed as Sep 15 2026 4:17:18 AM and one created at 2026-09-10T19:51:56Z as Sep 10 2026 3:51:56 PM - both UTC-4, eight hours from the viewer's own wall clock (would have been 12:17 PM and 11:51 PM). An earlier reading (Sep 15 2026 5:51:39 AM for 09:51:39Z, browser at UTC-4) had attributed the offset to the browser's timezone; that attribution is disputed by this observation. The consequence is that two operators looking at one order see two different clock times according to a per-user setting neither of them is looking at, and neither reading matches the API. Do not reconcile a screen time with a payload time by assuming the reader's timezone; open the profile first. Two more traps on the same blade. The indexation widget at the bottom prints the SAME KIND of value in raw UTC instead (Indexed Sep 15 2026 8:21:52 am against modifiedDate 08:21:51Z), so one blade shows two clocks four hours apart and labels neither. And a blade reached by a cold deep link, before the profile has loaded, falls back to the BROWSER's timezone and a ja-JP date pattern (2026-09-15 PM12:17:18), so the same order can print two different creation times in the same session depending on how the blade was opened.

**Anchor corrected.** `CustomerOrder.createdDate` → `GET /api/order/customerOrders/{id}` — The REST DTO field name is not a coordinate this base projects; the route that serves the UTC value the claim compares against is.

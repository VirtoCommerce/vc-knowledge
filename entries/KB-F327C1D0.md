Product with two unconditional USD price lists assigned to the store catalog (priority 1000 = 100, priority 999 = 240). Pricing evaluation picks the higher-priority list (shopper sees 100), but the index document holds price=[240] and price_usd=[240] plus per-list fields price_usd_<listA>=100 and price_usd_<listB>=240. The Pricing module ProductPriceDocumentBuilder writes the generic field as Max or Min of all prices per setting Pricing.Indexing.PriceIndexingValue (default Max); assignment priority and conditions are ignored. xAPI price filters/facets/sort on 'price' use the generic field, so a product with several list prices filters and sorts by the max price unless the per-list syntax price.usd.<pricelist> is used. Same on Pricing 3.1003.0 and 3.1008.0.

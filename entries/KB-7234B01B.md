As admin on Electronics (theme folder default): a multipart POST to /api/content/themes/Electronics?folderUrl=%2Fdefault%2Fcustom-sections&draft=true with a part named custom-agent-test-<x>.json answered 200 with published=false and hasChanges=true; the search listed the file under url .../custom-sections/custom-agent-test-<x>.json-draft with published=false, and GET /api/site-builder/theme/status listed custom-sections/custom-agent-test-<x>.json under drafts and newDrafts. The same upload with a part NAMED <x>.json-draft and no draft flag answered 200 but was listed as <x>.json with published=true and no draft (run REG-2026-11-26-1859, SB-108). DELETE /api/content/themes/Electronics?urls=/default/custom-sections/<x>.json answered 204 and removed the never-published draft (theme/status drafts []); a following DELETE of <x>.json-draft answered 500 because nothing was left.

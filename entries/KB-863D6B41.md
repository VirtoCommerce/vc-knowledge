From the admin Search index blade, the Product row was selected, Build index clicked and "Build" (not "Delete and build") chosen; this sent POST /api/search/indexes/index with [{"documentType":"Product","deleteExistingIndex":false}], answered 200 with an IndexProgressPushNotification (processedCount 0, errorCount 0, "Starting indexation..."). The Indexation process blade showed progress "Product: N of 4865 have been indexed" with "Errors: 0" throughout and ended in about 2 minutes with "Indexation completed successfully" / "Indexed 4865 of 4865 records". The stored notification read back through POST /api/platform/pushnotifications had documentType Product, totalCount 4865, processedCount 4865, errorCount 0, errors [] and a finished timestamp. GET /api/search/indexes then reported the Product index active with indexedDocumentsCount 5111 (more documents than the 4865 processed records) and lastIndexationDate equal to the finish time.

As admin: raised the own product's list price 35 -> 40 (PUT /api/products/prices), then PUT /api/product-subscription/subscriptions/{id} nextOrderDate = tomorrow 15:11Z (inside CutoffHours=24), then POST scheduler/run (processed 1). In that one run the renewal went Scheduled -> Calculated preview 46.00 -> ReminderSent -> Notified -> Ordered -> Authorized: listPrice 40, item total 36, notifiedTotal 46, orderTotal 46, reviewReasonCode null, lastOutcome Succeeded. The subscription's referencePrice stayed 31.50 (35 less 10%) and was not used as a cap. So the price-review trigger is a rise above the total the reminder announced (notifiedTotal), not above the subscription's reference price; to provoke PRICE_INCREASED the price must rise AFTER the renewal reaches Notified (the earlier passing proof flipped the price after Notified). Price was restored to 35 afterwards.

On vcptcore_qa1 (2026-09-29), POST /api/platform/security/users/search with keyword set to an account's EMAIL returned no match for two accounts whose userName is not their email (a SiteAdministrator with userName katalonOperator / email katalonOperator@mail.com, and a Manager with userName agent-test-sr-adm-noread / email agent-test-sr-adm-noread@example.com), while keyword set to the userName found both. For accounts whose userName equals the email the email keyword works, which hides the gap. A lookup by email must therefore search by userName when known, or page the unfiltered list and match email client-side; an empty result by email is not proof the account is absent.

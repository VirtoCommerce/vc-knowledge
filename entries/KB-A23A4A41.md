whiteLabelingSettings searches the organization's and the store's white-labeling settings independently, each filtered to enabled ones, and merges them per field. A store with no enabled setting removes only the store's own contribution: an organization with white labeling enabled still resolves its own logo and theme, and an organization theme overrides the store theme. Menu and footer link lists resolve against the store's content by list name, so empty menus there reflect missing link-list content, not suppression. This was shown with stores that have no enabled setting; a literal toggle of the store switch to off was not exercised.

Signed-in B2B shopper, playwright Chromium, a fresh navigation per load from about:blank with CDP cache disabled, viewport resize only; I summed the layout-shift entries without recent input from a buffered PerformanceObserver ~5 s after load. Build: Ver. 2.59.0-pr-2458 (upstream vc-frontend, which carries the barcode lookup: a Barcode scan button in the header, and the h1 'No products found for barcode <v>'). /search?barcode=<unknown>: at 1920x1080 the CLS is 0.7240 (2/2 loads) and the largest shift is 0.7074 at ~1.0 s: div.category-products goes from 246,297 1414x783 to 251,237 1404x425 (the controls row and skeletons are removed), the footer enters at y=816, and the empty-state svg (552x528) enters. At 375x812 the largest shift is 0.7398: div.category-products goes from 19,280 322x532 to 24,222 312x417 and the svg and footer enter. In 4 of 5 loads the empty view was replaced by the skeleton layout ~14 ms later and then came back ~160 ms later: three 0.7398 shifts and a CLS of 2.2785. The remaining load scored 0.798. On vc-frontend-next 3.0.0-alpha.2685, signed in, the same route scores 0.412 at 1920 and 0.758 at 375, with a single collapse and no flicker. So the barcode shift is inherited from upstream, and the theme does not amplify it.

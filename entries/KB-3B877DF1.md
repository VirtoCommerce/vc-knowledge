Reorder all is offered only on Completed orders. On a Completed order whose two lines were priced 124.99 (qty 2 and qty 1), clicking it opened an 'Adding products to cart result' dialog ('Successfully added 2', SKU + quantity per line, View cart / Print / OK) and the cart got qty 2 and qty 1 at today's catalog prices (189.00 and 349.00, matching the PDP), not the order's 124.99 — no price-change notice is shown. With the same SKU already in the cart at qty 1 (and qty 2 in a second run), the result was one line at 2 (and 3), never a duplicate row; an unrelated pre-existing line kept its quantity. The header cart badge counts total quantity.

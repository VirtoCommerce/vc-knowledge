Stable 16 (platform 3.1076.1). GET /api/platform/security/users/{userId} answers 200 with body null (the segment is treated as a userName). GET /api/platform/security/users/id/{userId} and GET /api/platform/security/users/{userName} return the user; after an Admin lock both show lockoutEnd 9999-12-31 and no isLockedOut field.

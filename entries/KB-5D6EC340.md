On /company/dashboard in Edit layout (theme 2.60.0-alpha.2579), each widget header shows a 'Max rows' spinbutton labelled 'Maximum rows shown by <widget>, between 1 and N': N=20 for My recent orders, N=10 for Top sellers, Tasks & due dates and Document library. On the Document library spinbutton, 1 and 10 are accepted; typing 0 becomes 1 and typing 11 becomes 10 when focus leaves, with no validation message and no console error. Setting 8 and saving made the widget show 8 rows after reload, and Edit layout showed 8 again; the value persists as settings maxRows on the block in salesRepLayout.

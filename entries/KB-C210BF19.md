On B2B-store, POST /api/site-builder-pages/grouped {storeId, name, permalink, cultureName} without a visibility field created the group with visibility:false (the shell's New page blade defaults the Visibility switch to on). After content save and POST grouped/publishing/{id}?publish=true (publish-status published:true), anonymous Query.slugInfo(permalink) kept returning entityInfo null for 90+ s and the storefront rendered '404 Page not found'. PUT /api/site-builder-pages/grouped with visibility:true made slugInfo resolve the published page id within 15 s and the storefront rendered the published content. Anonymous Query.builderPage(groupId) returned the content regardless of visibility.

Published multilanguage page with en-US, de-DE, fr-FR (custom permalink) versions; the fr-FR version's UserGroups set to a group neither the anonymous visitor nor the signed-in buyer belongs to (PUT grouped, userGroups as a string). pageDocument.alternates of en-US then lists de-DE and en-US only and the head hreflang drops fr-FR. slugInfo(fr permalink, fr-FR) anonymously still returns entityInfo with the fr-FR version's objectId, and pageDocument(id) for that id returns null; the storefront /fr/<fr permalink> renders the 404 view. /fr/<en permalink> renders the en-US version (DefaultLanguage fallback). Clearing UserGroups restores the fr-FR version.

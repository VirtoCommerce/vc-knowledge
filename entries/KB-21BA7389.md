A customer token (password grant with storeId) reads its own push inbox with pushMessages(first, after, keyword, sort, unreadOnly, withHidden, cultureName) on /graphql. PushMessageConnection has totalCount, pageInfo, edges, items (no unreadCount field); PushMessageType has id, shortMessage (HTML), createdDate, isRead, isHidden (no createdBy or sender field), so a sender or organization name is observable only inside shortMessage text.

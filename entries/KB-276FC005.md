Sales rep loaded /de/account/lists (full page load) and shared a list with one organization (no typed note). The storefront sent Mutation.sendCustomerCommunication with cultureName de-DE, title 'Eine neue Liste von Ihrem Vertriebsmitarbeiter' and the German default message 'Hallo! Ich habe die Liste „<name>“ gerade für Ihre Organisation freigegeben. Schauen Sie sie sich an:' + blank line + /shared-list/<key>. A recipient of that organization browsing the storefront in English saw the German text in the header Notifications dropdown. The notification language follows the sender's UI locale; whether it should follow the recipient's is an open product question.

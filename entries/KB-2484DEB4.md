Plain rep token, POST /graphql/sales-rep, store B2B-store, 2026-10-20 ~18:10 UTC. Query.salesRepActivities with period from 2026-10-19T00:00:00Z to 2026-10-19T23:59:59.999Z returned GA login buckets with occurredAt 2026-10-20T05:00:00Z and 2026-10-20T00:00:00Z (precision hour) alongside the 2026-10-19 17:00Z/18:00Z buckets; the same query with period from 2026-10-20T00:00:00Z to now returned searches/productViews/logins all 0. Hour labels themselves are UTC (an event sent 17:35 UTC sits in the 17:00Z bucket). So the GA read filters by calendar date in the GA property's timezone (west of UTC by at least 6 h here) and returns every hour of that local day, while occurredAt is converted to UTC: period from/to are not honoured to the instant, and the results for a UTC day can include hours of the next UTC day.

On vcst_qa with Payment 3.1013.0 and platform 3.1101.0, POST /api/payment/search {storeId, skip, take} was paged until an empty page with take 0,1,2,3,4,5,8,100, with and without isActive:true, on 4 stores. Every store has 6 registered methods. B2B-store has all 6 saved. Once paging passes the 6 saved rows, all 6 codes come back as new id-less instances: take=1 gives 12 rows across 13 pages, and totalCount is 6 on the first pages and then 10, 11 or 12. Store Electronics has 1 saved method (DefaultManual) and 5 unsaved. With take=1, page 1 reports totalCount 1 and later pages report 7; DefaultManual returns on page 2 without its id; with take=0 totalCount is 1. With take=2..5 one unsaved code repeats across a page boundary. Stores with no saved methods report totalCount 0 for take=0 but 6 for take>=1, with no repeats. The isActive:true variant showed no deviation, because only saved methods matched it. No run reached the 30-page cap. The fixed behaviour on Payment 3.1014.0-pr-83 is KB-7F0B12BF.

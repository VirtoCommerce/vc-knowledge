Signed-in B2B buyer, theme vc-frontend-next 3.3.0, store B2B-store, product 'UGREEN Revodok Pro 7-in-1 USB-C Hub' (SKU UGR-75774). Query.products / Query.product return availabilityData {isActive:true, isAvailable:true, isBuyable:true, isInStock:true, availableQuantity:0}. The category card and PDP show an 'In stock' badge without a number (other products show the count), and '+' on the card adds it (header 'Cart: 1', 'in Cart 1'). On /cart the same line shows the 'In stock' chip AND a red line message 'The product is out of stock', and the Order summary shows 'Something went wrong. Please try again later.'. ChangeFullCartItemsQuantity to 3 answers validationErrors PRODUCT_FFC_QTY 'Changed quantity is unavailable. Available quantity is 0.' while the UI keeps qty 3 and totals 3x. On /bulk-order the line 'UGR-75774,2' is rejected in the 'Add to cart SKU errors' dialog with 'The product is out of stock' and nothing is added. A product with a positive availableQuantity (Anker 364, 4469) behaves consistently in all four places.

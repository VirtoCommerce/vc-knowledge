Product in cart at 49.99 (list 49.99, no sale). In Admin, a SALE price of 0 was saved on the price list item (list price 49.99 unchanged). After 40s+, /cart and Query.cart still showed the stale line (listPrice/salePrice/placedPrice/extendedPrice 49.99, validationErrors []), while the product page and recentlyBrowsed showed price.actual 0.00 with list 49.99. Clicking Place order sent addOrUpdateCartPayment whose cart response repriced the line: salePrice 0.00, placedPrice 0.00, extendedPrice 0.00, listPrice 49.99, discountTotal 49.99, and a CART_PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE validation error on the LineItem and on the cart ("The product is no longer available for purchase"). Cart totals then read subTotal 49.99 (list-based), discountTotal 49.99, total = tax + shipping. createOrderFromCart returned HTTP 200 with data null and error "The cart has validation errors"; the storefront showed an "Error when creating an order" toast, the line with a red "no longer available" banner, and Place order disabled. So a zero effective price blocks checkout instead of producing a free line.

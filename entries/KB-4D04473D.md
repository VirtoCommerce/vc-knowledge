2026-11-09 on vcptcore_regression (platform 3.1076.8, theme 2.59.0, VirtoCommerce.Return 3.1004.0): GraphQL introspection lists returnableItems, returnPolicy, return, returnReasons, returns, returnStatuses and the mutations createReturn/submitReturn/updateReturn/cancelReturn, but no organizationReturns. Signed in on the storefront as an organization member whose membership role is the seeded organization-returns viewer role, /account/returns?scope=organization rendered the plain own Returns list (search, Filters, empty state) with no 'My returns' / organization tabs and no error. The organization tab and query arrived with Return 3.1005.0-pr-28 and theme 2.60.0-pr-2523 (observed on vcst_qa in other entries), so on older bundles every organization-returns case is an environment block, not a product failure.

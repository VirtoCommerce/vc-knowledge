On the storefront search card and PDP, requests for catalog/<folder>/<code>_md.jpg and _sm.jpg (assets host /cms-content/assets/...) returned 200 image/jpeg with cache-control: max-age=14400 and cf-cache-status: MISS (first hit after regeneration; Last-Modified matched the new blob). Any client or edge that cached the old thumbnail may keep it for up to 4 hours after a Regenerate run. Treat "new thumbnail visible" checks with a cache-busted URL or check Last-Modified/size, not the picture.

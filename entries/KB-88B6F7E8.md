Stable 16 (Platform 3.1076.1). Customer user of B2B-store with emailConfirmed=false. With Stores.EmailVerificationEnabled=true, Admin Security > Users > user > 'Resend link' fired POST /api/platform/security/users/{id}/sendVerificationEmail (200) and a ConfirmationEmailNotification to the user's email appeared in Notifications > Notification activity feed, status Sent (API) / 'Success' (UI), within seconds. After switching Email verification off in store Settings (OK + Save, REST false), the same Resend answered 200 but no new journal entry appeared after 40 s (journal total unchanged). emailConfirmed stayed false in both cases.

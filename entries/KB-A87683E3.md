On a RabbitMQ background-job engine (BackgroundJobs 3.1055.0, no Hangfire dashboard), the recurring job CleanupCompareListsJob (cron 0 2 * * *, setting-driven) is triggered on demand by POST /api/background-jobs/enqueue {"name":"CleanupCompareListsJobHandler"}; GET /api/platform/jobs/{id} then reports {state:null, completed:true} after ~2 s whether or not anything changed. ProductComparison.AnonymousListLifetimeHours refuses 0 (POST /api/platform/settings answers HTTP 500 "Setting … is invalid"); 1 is accepted. With lifetime 1, a guest list created ~62 min earlier was deleted, a guest list created seconds before and a signed-in buyer's list were kept. The same job deletes the list a user keeps for an organization the contact was removed from, and the list of a deleted user; removing the membership or deleting the user alone deletes nothing until the job runs. The job runs across all stores.

Storybook story components-organisms-vcstatwidget--row (flex flex-wrap gap-4, cards grow basis-44). At 1280px all six cards sit on one line and are all 174px high (stretch to the tallest). At 375px each card wraps onto its own line, so align-items: stretch has nothing to equalise against and each card keeps its content height: 156, 156, 136, 136, 106 (the loading card) and 92 (the error card). So the loading/error height shrink of a standalone card reappears in a row as soon as the row wraps to one card per line; only cards sharing a flex line are masked.

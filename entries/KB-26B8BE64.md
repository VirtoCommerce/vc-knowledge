For a user who has a progress row (an order landed while the window was open), a mission whose EndDate passed about 90 s earlier is still returned by loyaltyMissionProgress with statuses [InProgress, Completed], status InProgress and daysRemaining 0. Once the expiry job runs the progress row's status becomes Expired and the mission drops out of that filter: a mission that ended 19 days earlier had every progress row at status Expired. A user with no progress row on a closed mission does not get it at all. Missions still in their window that have no progress row for the user are returned with isStarted false and 0%. Observed on Loyalty 3.1009.0, vcst, 2026-10-01.

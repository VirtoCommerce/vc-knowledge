For a user who has a progress row (an order landed while the window was open), a mission whose EndDate passed about 90 s earlier is still returned by loyaltyMissionProgress with statuses [InProgress, Completed], status InProgress and daysRemaining 0. Once the expiry job runs the progress row's status becomes Expired and the mission drops out of that filter: a mission that ended 19 days earlier had every progress row at status Expired. A user with no progress row on a closed mission does not get it at all. Missions still in their window that have no progress row for the user are returned with isStarted false and 0%. Observed on Loyalty 3.1009.0, vcst, 2026-10-01. A separate observation 21 minutes after a mission's endDate also had loyaltyMissionProgress return status InProgress with daysRemaining 0; the storefront /account/missions card read '0 days left' with a danger dot and an enabled Open mission button, and the order modal showed the same '0 days left' with no ended state. A mission whose endDate falls later the same day reads '1 day left'.

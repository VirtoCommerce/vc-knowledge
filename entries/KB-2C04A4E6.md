On B2B-store a promotion auto-adds a gift line (isGift true, price 0, qty 1) to a cart. After a rep addItem of qty 1, xAPI cart.items showed one line (qty 1) while REST GET /api/carts/{id} showed that line plus the gift line; salesRepCustomerCartStatistics period.selectedItemQuantity read 2, and stayed line quantity + 1 at every later quantity (qty 5 -> 6, qty 15 -> 16). The money figures (total) exclude gifts by design, but the item-quantity figures do not filter isGift. Likewise salesRepTopSellers counted the gifted product's units: an order of 14 units plus a 1-unit gift showed 15 units. Whether gifts should count toward these quantities is not stated by the field descriptions ('Summed quantity of the line items selected for checkout').

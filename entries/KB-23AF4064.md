On a local build of the SalesRep plugin (VCST-6158 working tree), signed in as a sales rep, the customer profile /company/my-customers/{orgId} renders an "Available coupons" widget that sends Query.salesRepCoupons with storeId, cultureName and first = the block's saved maxRows (5 when the layout was never saved) and no organization argument. The widget's rows on two different served customers, read seconds apart, were the identical codes, and its "All promotion coupons" link opens /company/my-customers/{orgId}/coupons, whose first rows are the widget's rows in the same order. Items are ordered by endDate ascending, items with no endDate last, ties by id. The page requests first 10 with after as a string offset ("0", "10", "20"); three pages captured back to back returned 23 distinct items equal to totalCount, and changing page scrolls the window to the top. Because paging is by offset, coupons created between two page loads shift items forward, so the same coupon can appear on two pages if totalCount changed in between. Coupon codes render uppercased (host CouponItem card).

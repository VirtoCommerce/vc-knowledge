On a PDP with 10 related products (markup-tracked list related_products, related_type product), Edge: cold load at 390 pushed one view_item_list with 4 items (the short grid renders 4 cards); cold load at 1920 pushed one with all 10 (the carousel renders every slide in the DOM, not just the 5 visible). On one page view, 390->1920->390 pushed three view_item_list events (4, 10, 4) with the same list id and related_id. A click on a card's image Next slide arrow pushed nothing; a click on the card title pushed one select_item with the list's item_list_id/name/related_id/related_type. Pre-markup source sent the whole relatedProducts array once per array change.

Build range (judged 2026-10-29): everything above holds only for the vc-frontend PR 2543 build 2.60.0-pr-2543-0127-01276486, which was never released. From the PR's final commit fdabd62d ("send whole product lists and dedupe id-less markup items") and in every released theme since 2.60.0, the related-products widget binds the whole relatedProducts array (v-track-item on the outer VcWidget; dom-analytics engine.ts sends a bound array as the whole list, hidden cards included), so each page view pushes ONE view_item_list related_products with all items at every width, and crossing lg sends nothing more (the tracked element stays the same; scan() re-sends only when the item ids change). Observed on theme 2.61.0-alpha.2605 on vcst_qa: 10 items at 390, 800 and 1920 (4, 4 and 10 cards rendered), no re-send on 1920->800->1920->390->1920.

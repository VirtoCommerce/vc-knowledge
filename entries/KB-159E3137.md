Build-scoped (vc-frontend PR #2543, VCST-6017, dom-analytics markup tracking).

From commit fdabd62d of that PR (merged as 0bb1cab6, first released in vc-frontend 2.60.0): client-app/shared/static-content/components/related-products.vue binds relatedProducts to the list container with v-track-item, and the dom-analytics engine's collect source (client-app/core/plugins/dom-analytics/engine.ts) returns that bound array instead of the rendered product-card descendants. On a PDP with 10 related products (list related_products, related_type product) a cold load at 390, 800 and 1920 each pushes one view_item_list with all 10 items, in the association order, although the short grid below lg renders only 4 cards; resizing 1920->800->1920->390->1920 on one page view pushes no further related_products view_item_list. Re-observed 2026-10-27 on vcst_qa, theme 2.61.0-pr-2577-4c17-4c171a96, Chrome, guest.

On the pre-fix PR build theme 2.60.0-pr-2543-0127-01276486 (never released) the list was collected from the rendered cards: cold load at 390 pushed one view_item_list with 4 items, at 1920 one with all 10 (the carousel renders every slide in the DOM), and 390->1920->390 on one page view pushed three events (4, 10, 4) with the same list id and related_id.

On every build checked: a click on a card's image Next slide arrow pushes nothing; a click on the card title pushes one select_item with the list's item_list_id/item_list_name/related_id/related_type. Pre-markup source sent the whole relatedProducts array once per array change.

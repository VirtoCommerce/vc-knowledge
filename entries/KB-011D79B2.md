On /account/missions the PerSku mission modal sends the GraphQL mutation UpdateShortCartItemQuantity (updateCartQuantity) with the absolute stepper quantity per product, not an increment. On reopen the stepper is pre-filled with the quantity already in the cart, an 'in Cart N' badge is shown, and Add to cart stays disabled until the quantity changes. After a successful add the modal closes and the header cart badge updates.

On a fresh-DB local stack, the 8GB (RAM section) and 256GB SSD (Storage section) option products of a configurable laptop were created active, buyable, stocked and storefront-visible, but deliberately given NO row in any price list (PUT /api/products/prices refuses list 0, KB-52C3F83E). Anonymous Query.productConfiguration (storeId B2B-store, en-US, USD) returned both required sections with all three options each; the two unpriced options came back with listPrice.amount 0.0 and extendedPrice.amount 0.0, next to their priced siblings (100/250 and 75/150). So omitting the price row is a working way to seed a zero-surcharge option: it is not filtered out the way an unresolvable option product is (KB-EB991080).

On theme 2.61.0-pr-2458-3f0b with GoogleEcommerceAnalytics 4.1005.0-pr-61-5267 (B2B-store), a sales rep signed in via /sign-in (landing page _s=1 hit: own organization id, is_sales_rep=true, session_kind=self, then one en=login success=true). Selecting a served customer organization in the header account menu reloads the page; that page's _s=1 page_view carried up.organization_id = the selected customer org, up.contact_id = the rep's contact, up.is_sales_rep=true, up.session_kind=self. Later hits on the page (search, view_item) carry no up.* on the wire and inherit the page-level set. session_kind stays 'self' (it does not mark the rep as acting for a customer); is_sales_rep is the only discriminator.

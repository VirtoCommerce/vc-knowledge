Build 2.59.0-pr-2458, guest on /sign-in (anonymous catalog redirects there), Firefox 375x812. The menu is a full-screen opaque nav.mobile-menu with no role, no aria-modal, no aria-label; main/footer are not inert or aria-hidden. Opening it leaves focus on the Main menu button underneath; the first four Tabs walk hidden header controls (logo link, phone, search, cart) before reaching Theme toggle, language selector, an unnamed close button, the links and Settings. Tab past Settings goes to the sign-in form (Email, Password, Show/hide password) invisible behind the menu. Escape does not close the menu, even with focus on Settings inside it. Clicking the close button closes it and focus falls to BODY rather than returning to Main menu. Upstream source mobile-menu.vue has no focus or keyboard handling and the close button has no aria-label.

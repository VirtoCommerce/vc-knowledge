Edge 155, 1440x900, light mode, default preset served, signed in as a Sales Representative. In the Schedule call popup opened from the customer page's Quick actions tile, the Notes textarea (.vc-textarea__input, 516x106) has border 1px #d4d4d4 on a #ffffff fill inside a #ffffff dialog = 1.48:1, while the Title, Customer, Due date, Time and Priority wrappers are 1px #a3a3a3 = 2.52:1. Clicking into Notes keeps the 1.48:1 border and adds a 2px #1b789b outline offset 2px (4.99:1). VcTextarea sets a bare Tailwind border, and tailwind.config.ts sets the default border colour to neutral-300, which is #d4d4d4 in default, coffee, red and mercury. The derived dark values are 1.92:1 (default.dark), 2.77:1 (coffee.dark) and 1.88:1 (red.dark) against each preset's additional-50.

A cart line created by Mutation.updateCartQuantity (the storefront PDP quantity-stepper path for a simple product) is returned AND stored (GET /api/carts/{id}) with listPrice 0 and salePrice 0. Mutation.setCartItemSubscription then fails with SUBSCRIPTION_ERROR "This product has no price of its own. Choose one of its variations to subscribe." because its EnsureHasOwnPrice check reads the stored line price (meant for a variation parent; configured lines are exempt). The same product added with Mutation.addItem is stored with its price ($190) and setCartItemSubscription succeeds. A few minutes later, after the cart is recalculated/saved, the same line can be subscribed. Reproduced as a buyer on two simple products (one $35, one $190) in a Subscribe & Save plan.

The mission card type chip icon comes from a type map in vc-frontend mission-card.vue (2.59.0 and dev): PerSkuAll and PerSkuAny barcode, OrderValue cash, OrderCount shopping-bag, all solid. A device-scale crop of the 'Order count' chip shows a bag with a handle arc whose small render can look like a padlock, but the icon is shopping-bag (the ui-kit lock-closed glyph is a different, rectangular shape). A private mission card (Order value) renders with the same chips and no lock marker.

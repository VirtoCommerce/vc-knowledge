On the product page as a guest, clicking Share opens a 'Share product' panel rendered as a body-level sibling of #app (the teleport host), not inside the sidebar. Escape closes it; clicking another header control (language) closes it; following a breadcrumb or browser Back leaves no panel behind. With the panel open, Tab from the Share button lands on the next sidebar control (Send link by email), not on the panel's share links: the panel is not in the trigger's tab sequence and does not take focus (the popover is not in dialog mode). Same at 375px. Selects inside the New address modal and the catalog Sort-by render inline, not in the host; the address modal's Country list closes on Escape while the modal stays.

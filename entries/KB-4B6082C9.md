As a sales rep on /company/tasks (Firefox), picking customers whose organization names are 121 and 101 characters long in the Customers filter: at 1024 px the two customer chips measured 757 and 819 px inside a 740 px chips container; the chip text and its remove button spill out and the page scrolls sideways (document scrollWidth 1071 against 1024). The same happens at 1100 px (1109 against 1100) and 640 px (769 against 640); at 1200 and 1279 px both chips fit. Chips neither wrap their text nor truncate it. Names of roughly 95 characters or more are needed to trigger it at 1024 px.

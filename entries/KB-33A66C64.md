On a store with Loyalty.LoyaltyBalanceCalculationMode=Organization, a freshly created contact that is a member of one organization placed an xAPI order (createOrderFromCart; the order carried that organizationId). The resulting operation-log rows carry both userId and organizationId, and their running balance is the organization's pooled balance (tens of thousands, shared with the other members), not a per-user one. GET /api/loyalty-program-operation-log/balance/user/{userId} returned {"balance":0} for that user after the earn, while GET .../balance/organization/{organizationId} returned the pooled total, and an operation-log search filtered by userId returned 0 rows. A fixture that funds a per-user balance by an earn order therefore cannot converge in Organization mode for an organization member.

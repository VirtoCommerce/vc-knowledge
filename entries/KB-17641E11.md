Read from the GetOrderStatistics variables the storefront sends (VCST-6078 PR build), with browser time zone and clock emulated in Chrome. weekFrom = Monday 00:00 local of the current week, all To bounds = today 23:59:59.999 local; prevWeek = the same weekdays of the previous week (Wed Oct 21 -> Oct 12-14); prevFrom/prevTo = day 1 to the same day of the previous month (Nov 9 -> Oct 1-9; Mar 31 2027 -> Feb 1-28; Feb 29 2028 -> Jan 1-29); lastYear = Jan 1 to the same day last year (clamped to Feb 28). LA DST-end Sunday Nov 1 is handled as a 25-hour day; in UTC-10 on Monday 09:30Z the week is the previous Mon-Sun and list dates show the local day. Rendered: a lower period shows a red '-N% vs last week/month/year', higher shows '+1,050% vs last month' (thousand separator), an empty baseline shows nothing, never NaN. Layout drives the request: a hidden WEEK card sends withWeek:false, hidden YTD with AOV kept sends withYearOverYear:false (AOV still rendered), max rows N sends GetOrders first:N; with every block hidden neither GetOrderStatistics nor GetOrders is sent and 'All blocks are hidden' + 'Restore default layout' renders; that button saves the default layout immediately (persisted after reload) and announces 'Default layout restored.'

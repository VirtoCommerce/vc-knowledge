Sales rep on /de/company/my-customers/{id}, theme 2.61.0-pr-2458. The widget title span (.vc-widget__title, uppercase, 18px, overflow-wrap:break-word) shares the header row with the 'Gesamte Aktivität →' link; at 1920 px the right column is 350 px and the title gets 165 px, narrower than the single word 'KUNDENAKTIVITÄT', so it renders 'KUNDENAKTIVITÄ' + 'T' on two lines; at 320 px it renders 'KUNDENAKTI' + 'VITÄT' (122 px). At 375 px it fits on one line. At 320 px the Summary value for 'Letzte Web-Anmeldung' wraps as '20.10' / '.2026' because the longer German label squeezes the value column. In English and French the title is not broken at these widths in this session's checks ('Activité du client' wraps between words).

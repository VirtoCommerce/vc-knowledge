Theme 2.63.0-pr-2602, B2B-store, signed-in buyer. PDP 'Subscribe & Save' + Increase quantity sends Mutation.setCartItemSubscription (planId, intervalUnit, intervalCount). /cart then shows the line price discounted (PERCENT 10: $35.00 -> $31.50) with a 'Subscribe & Save' checkbox and frequency combobox per line, and a 'Your subscription' block listing each subscribed product + frequency, the store terms text (same text and version 2026-10 as Query.subscriptionTerms) and an 'I agree...' checkbox; with address, delivery and payment set, Place order stays disabled ('Please accept the terms to place your order') until it is ticked. With a subscription line the payment dropdown offered only card-capable methods (here 'Pay with Apple Pay' and 'Credit card (test)'). After Place order the app goes to /checkout/payment, where the save-card checkbox is checked and disabled ('Your subscription is paid with this card, so it will be saved securely'); saved cards are offered as radios (default preselected) plus 'Use a new card'. The subscription exists right after Place order with status PendingActivation and turns Active once the payment is Authorized; nextOrderDate = order createdDate + interval (1 month, 2 weeks, 10 days, 3 months all matched). One-time lines in the same order get no subscription. Order status stays New with the payment Authorized.

Seeded user with no roles, authorization-code + PKCE token for client claude-admin-qa (aud {BACK_URL}/admin/mcp, 30-min access token, no "permission" claim in the JWT). PUT /api/platform/security/users adding role AGENT-TEST-MCP-Approver: tools/list stayed at 5 tools (no approve_pending_change) at t0 and t+20s and showed 6 only after a refresh_token grant; removing the role left approve_pending_change listed until the next refresh. With role IT Platform Operator granted and the token refreshed, get_platform_health ran; after the role was removed the SAME token still ran get_platform_health at t0 and t+30s, and only after a refresh was it refused "Access forbidden: This tool requires authorization." Platform REST behaves the same: with a fresh token, granting mcp:audit:read left POST /api/mcp/audit/search at 403 (as KB-040FD11E describes). Only GET /api/platform/security/currentuser shows the new permission list at once, so it does not show what the token authorizes. Locking the account (POST /api/platform/security/users/{id}/lock) or Disconnect ends the session at once instead (401 "The OAuth session has ended", refresh invalid_grant).

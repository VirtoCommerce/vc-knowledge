Guest on the storefront. A configurable product with two Product-type sections both labelled 'Finish' (one required, one optional) was configured on its product page with a different option in each (required = an 'Exterior' option, optional = an 'Interior' option; configured price reflected both) and added to compare from the product page. On /compare the column renders Price, Availability, SKU, Min. order qty and exactly ONE 'Finish' row carrying the required section's option; the optional section's selected option appears nowhere in the table. The configuration rows are keyed by section label, so same-label sections collapse.

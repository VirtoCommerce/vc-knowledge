Signed in as a sales rep on /company/dashboard (theme 2.62.0-alpha.2647). The SalesRepCustomerOrderStatistics request asks for weekVsPrevWeek, mtdVsPrevMonth and ytdVsLastYear comparison(countChangePercent); all three came back null because the previous periods held no orders. The six KPI cards (Orders in New status, Active carts, Orders placed WEEK/MTD/YTD, My customers) show only value and sub-lines; each card's status slot is empty — no percent, no arrow, no NaN/Infinity/'null' text. So a non-null delta rendering cannot be observed on a stand whose previous periods are empty.

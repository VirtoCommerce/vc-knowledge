Started a Product Build (POST /api/search/indexes/index) and, once it had totalCount > 0, a second Build of Product, ContentFile, Category or a single product (documentIds or the product Index widget). The loser was always sealed at once with errors ["Indexation is already in progress."]. In 8 of the contended runs the loser's notification (POST /api/platform/pushnotifications) then switched to the winner's documentType, description and counts, e.g. a Category request ended "Product: indexation finished", 413/413, errorCount 1. Meanwhile the winner's own notification stayed at 50 or 100 of 413 and then jumped to "Indexation completed successfully". Other contended runs stayed clean, so it is intermittent. Source: IndexingJobs is a singleton holding one IndexProgressHandler, and RunIndexJobAsync calls Start() (which reassigns the shared notification) before trying the lock. So do not read the loser's description or counts as its own result. The errors[] entry is reliable.

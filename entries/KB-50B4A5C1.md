On PageBuilderModule 3.1034.0-pr-167 as administrator, the Designer loads content with GET .../grouped/{groupId}/content?includeVersion=true and saves with POST .../grouped/{groupId}/content?includeVersion=true, sending the version it read as an If-Match header; a successful save returns 200 with body {"eTag": "..."} and shows the toast 'Template <page name> saved successfully'. Two tabs loaded the same version; tab 1 saved (200), then tab 2 saved with the now-stale If-Match and got 412. Tab 2 showed a red error toast in the top-right corner reading exactly 'This page changed while you were editing. Your changes have not been saved. Copy your changes before reloading the page.' The tab-2 edit stayed in the field and in the preview, and the Save button stayed enabled. The toast disappears by itself after a few seconds (gone within about 8 s), after which nothing on screen indicates the conflict; clicking Save again repeats the 412 and the toast. Leaving the page did not show any unsaved-changes prompt. After the page was opened again it showed tab 1's content, and re-applying the edit and saving returned 200.

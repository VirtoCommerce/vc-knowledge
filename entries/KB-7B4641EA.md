Theme 2.63.0-pr-2602-8ac5, B2B-store, signed-in buyer. On the PDP with 'Subscribe & Save' chosen, + is debounced ~1.3 s, then Mutation UpdateShortCartItemQuantity adds the line, and only after its response does the PDP send Mutation.setCartItemSubscription (lineItemId, planId, intervalUnit, intervalCount). Clicking the header Cart after the add request but before its response (6 of 6 tries, en and ru, 1440 and 768 px, with or without picking a frequency) means setCartItemSubscription is never sent: the server cart has a one-time line at full price, /cart offers 'Subscribe', and a reload keeps it one-time. Leaving inside the debounce shows a confirm 'Some operations are still in progress...'; accepting it flushes the add but again sends no subscription. Once setCartItemSubscription was sent (4 of 4) the line arrived subscribed. /cart renders lines from Query GetFullCart and the 'Subscribed' row/'Your subscription' block from a second Query GetCartSubscriptionItems (cartSubscriptionItems) sent after it, hence the row appears 0.4-0.6 s after the lines.

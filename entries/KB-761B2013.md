Signed in as a sales rep on /account/dashboard (timeOrigin A), clicking Logout in the account-menu popup landed on /sign-in?returnUrl=/account/dashboard with a new timeOrigin B; signing in with the password form landed on /account/dashboard with another new timeOrigin C. Choosing Deutsch in the header Language selector moved /account/dashboard to /de/account/dashboard with a new timeOrigin as well (same for español and English). So a user switch or a language switch through the UI is always a full reload; a test premise of 'the tab is never reloaded' across these actions cannot be met, and plugin contributions are re-initialised from scratch on each of them.

After saving the account profile, every control in the storefront top header re-rendered without an accessible name: the language, currency, ship-to and theme buttons, the phone link and the account-menu button all appeared in the accessibility tree as unnamed, and the language flag image lost its alt text. The state persisted across repeated reads, so it is not a single-frame artifact, and a full page reload restored every name. The same class of loss was seen after switching organization, where the account-menu button rendered with no text at all and did not self-correct, and after changing the cart's shipping address, where the Pickup/Shipping delivery-option buttons lost their names. The affected controls keep working; only their names are gone, so the impact is on screen-reader users and on any automation selecting by accessible name after an in-page state change.

Created 1230 orders via POST /api/order/customerOrders with customerId = a plain sales rep's ApplicationUser id in two organizations the rep serves, 22 of them with isCancelled=true. Then, as the rep on POST /graphql/sales-rep (store B2B-store): salesRepActivities categoryCounts orders rose by 1230 (27 -> 1257), while salesRepCustomerOrderStatistics period YTD count rose by 1208 (27 -> 1235), exactly the non-cancelled orders. Per-organization salesRepCustomerOrderStatistics and the salesRepCustomers inline orderStatistics also counted only non-cancelled orders (1034 of 1055, 174 of 175) and agreed with each other to the cent. So the activity Orders count and the dashboard order KPI legitimately differ by the number of cancelled orders.

Reading the store, changing one settings[].value and PUTting the whole store body to /api/stores made Return.NotifyOrganizationEmail=false and Return.SendNotifications=false apply to the next submitReturn about 5-10 s later, and the public store setting Return.ReturnEnabled changed within seconds. A null value in that PUT does not clear a stored setting; it reads back as before, so restoring an unset setting means writing its default.

Signed-in B2B buyer, Chrome 375x812, full navigation (page.goto), CLS read with a buffered layout-shift PerformanceObserver ~3.5 s after load. The page shell paints with the footer (.app-footer, logo only, and .app-footer__bottom) inside the viewport; when the page content and the footer link columns load, the footer moves and grows and .app-footer__bottom leaves the viewport. Measured CLS: /account/security 0.183, /account/profile 0.215, empty /cart 0.472, /compare with 2 items 0.191, empty /compare 0.049. The same pages at 1920x1080 stay at 0.010-0.063. Above the 0.1 initial-render budget on every short page measured at 375 px, so it is a shell or footer behaviour, not specific to one page.

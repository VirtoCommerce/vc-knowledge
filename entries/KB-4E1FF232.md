returnableItems(orderId) counts a line as delivered only through shipment items of a shipment that carries a DeliveryDate. A Completed order whose shipment has no items or no DeliveryDate reports every line isReturnable=false, ineligibilityReason NOT_DELIVERED, deliveredQuantity 0, deliveryDate null, so the storefront offers no Request return. Linking shipment items (with the inline lineItem) and stamping DeliveryDate within Return.WindowDays makes the line returnable (isReturnable=true, returnableQuantity = delivered). Order status must also be in Return.AllowedOrderStatuses (default Completed); a Shipped order reports ORDER_STATUS_NOT_ALLOWED.

Signed-in B2B buyer, theme 2.59 storefront, ElasticSearch8 product index. Two products: Query.product(storeId,id,userId,currencyCode USD) returned prices[] with exactly one entry equal to price.actual.amount (100 and 8.3), and admin REST GET /api/products/{id}/prices also listed only that one price list. The product index document for each, however, carries a second per-list field (price_usd_<otherList> = 240 and 9.99) and the generic price_usd = [240] / [9.99] (the max). The storefront price facet [100 TO 101] / [8 TO 9] therefore omits the product the shopper sees at 100 / 8.30, and [9 TO 109] includes the one shown at 'From $8.30'. Consequence for test design: 'every prices[].actual.amount equals price.actual.amount' is NOT a reliable single-price-list discriminator; compare the displayed price with the indexed generic price_<cur> (admin GET /api/search/indexes/index/Product/{id}) instead.

On theme 2.60.0-pr-2523-466f (Firefox 151), both 'My returns' and the organization tab of /account/returns render button cards (.returns-list__mobile-item, labels RMA, Date, Buyer name on the organization tab only, Status, Qty) up to 1023 px and the table from 1024 px, switching on resize without a reload. Table columns: Date 112 px, Buyer name 128 px (160 px from 1280), Status 144 px, RMA and Qty share the rest; header cells stay on one line. Across 768, 960, 1023, 1024, 1040, 1100, 1150, 1200, 1250, 1279, 1280 and 1920 px no text token is broken across lines in the list and the page has no horizontal scroll. Only the cards at 375 px or narrower (still broken at 360 and 320, whole from 390) wrap the bold RMA inside its half-width column: Firefox splits it after the fourth digit of the sequence (RET261009-0002 / 2), Chromium splits it at the hyphen. Card labels are neutral-600 (7.81:1).

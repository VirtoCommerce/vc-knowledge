On the pr-2523 builds of theme 2.60.0 (open PR VirtoCommerce/vc-frontend#2523; observed on 466f in Firefox 151, on 4263 in Chromium, and on b9a7 in both on 2026-10-12 over the vcst backend), both 'My returns' and the organization tab of /account/returns render button cards (.returns-list__mobile-item; labels RMA, Date, Buyer name on the organization tab only, Status, Qty) up to 1023 px and the table from 1024 px (returns.vue mobile-breakpoint="lg"), switching on resize without a reload. Table columns: Date 112 px, Buyer name 128 px (160 px from 1280), Status 144 px, RMA and Qty share the rest; header cells stay on one line; the page has no horizontal scroll. RMA, Date and Status values are never broken across lines in the table, but vc-table sets word-break: break-word, so a long unbroken buyer-name token wraps mid-word inside its fixed cell: 'HolderMember/ship' (16 chars) at 1024-1279 px in Chromium and Firefox, whole at 160 px; a 32-char unbroken name wrapped over 3 lines even at 160 px (Chromium, 4263). In the narrow cards the bold RMA wraps inside its half-width column below a width that depends on the RMA string and the browser: Chromium splits it at the hyphen (b9a7: at 383 px and below for RET261012-*), Firefox mid-sequence (b9a7: at 368 px and below for RET261012-*; on 466f with RET261009-* still split at 385 and whole from 386); long buyer names wrap mid-word there too. Card labels are neutral-600 (7.81:1).

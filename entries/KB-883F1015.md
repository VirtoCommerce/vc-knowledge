On a vc-frontend 2.60 PR build (scope-slot loader), with GetSlugInfo/GetCategory delayed 6 s after a breadcrumb click, typing a keyword and pressing Enter while the disabled "Loading search context" button shows navigates to /search?q=<kw>; SearchProducts and GetSearchResults carry filter category.subtree:<store catalogId> only (global, 19 results vs 11 when scoped). The previous category's id is never sent. When the scope settles normally, a submit stays on the category route with ?q= and returns category-subtree results.

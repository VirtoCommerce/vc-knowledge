A JSON Patch sent to PATCH /api/catalog/products (bulk, with a replace /id op) that only adds a seoInfos entry leaves every other field of the product unchanged except one: a product name with leading or trailing whitespace comes back trimmed. The save path trims Name on every product save, so the same happens with POST /api/catalog/products and PATCH /api/catalog/products/{id}. Products imported with a trailing space in the name are silently renamed by any unrelated edit.

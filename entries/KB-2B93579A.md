GET /api/platform/modules, DELETE /api/catalog/products?ids=x, DELETE /api/order/customerOrders?ids=x, DELETE /api/members?ids=x and POST /api/order/customerOrders/search sent with no Authorization header and no cookie all returned 401 with content-length 0 and no WWW-Authenticate response header. Access control holds; tests expecting an error body or WWW-Authenticate on a 401 will not see them.

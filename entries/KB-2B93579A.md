Platform 3.1076.1, 2026-10-12. GET /api/platform/modules, DELETE /api/catalog/products?ids=x, DELETE /api/order/customerOrders?ids=x, DELETE /api/members?ids=x and POST /api/order/customerOrders/search sent with no Authorization header and no cookie all returned 401 with content-length 0 and no WWW-Authenticate response header. GET /api/catalog/catalogs and GET /api/order/customerOrders answered 405 before any auth check (those routes have no GET). Access control holds; tests expecting an error body or WWW-Authenticate on a 401 will not see them.

I signed in to the scoped /graphql/sales-rep endpoint as a sales rep who serves 7 test organizations (B2B-store) and introspected the endpoint. SalesRepCustomer now has the fields phone (String) and primaryContact (SalesRepContact: id, firstName, lastName, middleName, fullName, name, about, photoUrl, emails, phones). I then queried salesRepCustomers{items{phone address{phone} primaryContact{fullName phones}}}. Organization with a primary contact: phone equals that contact's first phone. Organization without a primary contact but with its own phone: primaryContact is null and phone is the organization's phone, which in the observed case also equalled address.phone. The API returns both values as-is, without deduplication. Organization whose default address has a different phone: address.phone carries that other number. Organization that has only sales reps as contact members: primaryContact is null, and phone is null when the organization has no phone of its own. Organization with no data: every field is null. On the older build (pr-28) these list fields did not exist; the PR adds them.

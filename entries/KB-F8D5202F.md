Start is used as sent; neither value carries a timezone and both come back with Z. The Orders module source does `end = end.Value.AddDays(2)` with the comment "Hack: to compensate for incorrect Local dates to UTC", and caches by the date pair. Clients that want a period ending on day D therefore send D-2, and the response endDate is the window really aggregated.

At 375x812 the Filters drawer shows a preset combobox (accessible name is its value, 'Custom date') above a combined 'Date range' field (Start/End textboxes 19px tall plus one 'Open calendar' button). Shift+Tab onto the preset combobox expanded its listbox (Custom date, Last day, Last week, Last month, Last year); pressing Tab moved focus to Start date and then End date while the listbox stayed expanded on top of the date field, and pointer clicks on the date inputs were intercepted by the listbox until an option was chosen. Also at this width the mobile Filters trigger is an icon-only button with no accessible name, and the 'MM/DD/YYYY' placeholder is clipped to 'MM/DD/YYY' in both segments.

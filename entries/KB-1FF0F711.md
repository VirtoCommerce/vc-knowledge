Personal user on /cart with one line, Shipping selected. The Delivery method listbox offers 'Fixed Rate (Ground)' and 'Fixed Rate (Air)', each with a FedEx logo and no price and no estimate text. The cart's availableShippingMethods (in the addOrUpdateCartShipment response) carry price Ground 150.00 / Air 250.00 and optionDescription null for every method. After choosing a method the price shows only in Order summary 'Shipping cost' (+$150.00 / +$250.00) and the Total updates (59.99 + tax + shipping). So a per-method price/estimate in the list is not rendered on this build; the placed order's detail page shows 'Fixed Rate (Ground) ($150.00)'.

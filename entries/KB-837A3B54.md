Signed in as a seeded missions account on B2B-store (Customer balance mode), /account/missions in USD listed 3 pages of missions including the USD OrderValueGoal missions. After switching the session currency to EUR (and locale to de), the page re-queried loyaltyMissionProgress with currencyCode=EUR, the USD-goal order-value missions were no longer listed and the list dropped to 2 pages. Switching back restored them. Whether this is intended (a money goal shown only in its own currency) was not established.

Guest, Edge 1920x1080. The method was to set the focus start point right after a carousel and press Shift+Tab, or to Tab into it and press ArrowRight.

- Focus never lands on either arrow, whether the arrow is enabled or disabled. Shift+Tab skips straight from the content after the carousel to the last slide's 'Increase quantity'.
- The accessibility tree exposes the arrows only as unnamed generic/img nodes.
- ArrowLeft/ArrowRight inside the carousel do nothing.

This was observed on three surfaces:
- the related-products carousel on a product page;
- a page-builder products-carousel block;
- the home page hero image slider.

On the product carousels, Tab still reaches cards in off-view slides, because the browser natively scrolls the slider wrapper. Swiper's own state does not follow that scroll: the prev arrow stays disabled while the content has shifted.

On the hero slider, the slides hold no focusable content, so slides 2+ cannot be reached by keyboard at all.

Source on vc-frontend dev matches the served bundle:
- ui-kit vc-carousel.vue renders div arrows (vc-carousel__btn--prev / --next) with Swiper modules Pagination + Navigation and no A11y module;
- static-content slider.vue renders div arrows (vc-slider__btn) with Navigation only.

By contrast, product-card image carousels on the same pages expose real buttons named 'Previous slide' and 'Next slide'.

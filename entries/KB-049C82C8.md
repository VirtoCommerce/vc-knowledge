On B2B-store after a 7-type Delete and build that rejected one Group A compare fixture (text property Price beside the numeric price field, KB-52EAADEE), an anonymous browser navigated to that product's long SEO URL. Query.slugInfo (in the pageContext request) resolved it: objectType CatalogProduct, isActive true, with its outline. The page then sent GetProduct (Query.product with storeId, id, previousOutline), and the response was {"data":{"product":null}} with no errors[]. The storefront rendered its client-side '404 Page not found' view. A retry gave the same result. Search for the other fixtures in the same category returned them. So the product page needs the product in the Product search index; the SEO URL is not a way around an index rejection, and a test that opens such a product by URL is blocked exactly like one that searches for it.

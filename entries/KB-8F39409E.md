On vcst_qa (ES8), POST /api/search/indexes/index with [{documentType:"Product", deleteExistingIndex:false}] (POST /api/search/indexes answers 405) ran over all 4865 documents. A product the build rejected on a field-type conflict was still returned by GET /api/search/indexes/index/Product/{id} with indexationdate 2026-10-14 — the version written before it acquired the offending property — and stayed findable through xAPI search, while every successfully re-indexed product carried the new build's indexationdate. The `size` field also kept its integer mapping although no indexed product carried an integer Size any more. So a non-delete build writes into the active index: a rejected document silently keeps its previous version, and only a delete-and-build re-derives field mappings — which also drops any such stale document from search for good.

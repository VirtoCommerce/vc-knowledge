On vcst_qa 2026-11-27 (platform 3.1101.0, WhiteLabeling 3.1008.0) Stores > B2B-store > White labeling > Logo widget showed the hint "Select a PNG, GIF, or SVG image" and toolbar Save, Reset, Clear; the Favicon widget showed "Select a PNG, JPG, or WEBP image" with Reset and Clear. The deployed scripts white-labeling-logo.js and white-labeling-favicon.js filter by extension regex png|gif|svg (logo and secondary logo) and png|jpg|jpeg|webp (favicon) with no file size check; Reset reverts unsaved edits, Clear sets logoUrl and secondaryLogoUrl (or faviconUrl) to null. So GIF is accepted for a logo and JPG is rejected for a logo. Nothing was uploaded or saved.

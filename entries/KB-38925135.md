Read in the source of vc-module-sales-rep PR #33 at head 8d4a30bd (sales-rep-tasks-page.vue, sales-rep-task-calendar.vue, constants.ts); not observed live. The day (default today, the 'Due today' chip) and the filters Status, Customers, Priorities and the search term are separate filters that intersect: Status Overdue under the Due today chip lists nothing, and removing the day chip lists every date. The link /company/tasks?filter=overdue is read once and dropped from the URL; it sets Status to Overdue and opens on every date; an unknown status name falls back to no Status filter, also on every date. Reset filters clears the day and every filter but keeps the search term, and refetches at once; picks refetch after a 300 ms debounce. The search term is committed on Enter or the search button, not per keystroke, maxlength 64, with its own clear and a 'Reset search' button on a zero-match list. The calendar footer Clear, or a second click on the picked day, unpicks the day; the calendar dots follow the filters but not the day. The no-match text is 'No tasks match the filters' when a filter or search is applied, else 'Nothing due on this day' (a day picked) or 'No tasks yet'. The release-layout rule that a status tab spans every date no longer holds on this build.

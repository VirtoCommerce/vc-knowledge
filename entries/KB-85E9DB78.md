With the Stages result blade already open at 1920x1080, a double-click on Debug sent one explain request; the first click closed and reopened the result blade, the blade strip scrolled back, and the second click landed on the Stores list row now under the pointer (store-acme), which opened that store and closed the B2B-store blade with its Debug blades. Reproduced twice; a single click works. A page reload also loses the Debug blades: the hash stays on the stores workspace, so the blade has no deep link.

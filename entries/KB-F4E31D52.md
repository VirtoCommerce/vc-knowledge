Customer created three pickup subscriptions in one storefront order (P-PCT monthly, P-FIX weekly, P-NONE yearly, card 4242), changed the third one's card to another saved card with changeProductSubscription, and set all three nextOrderDate to the same instant. One POST scheduler/run created two renewals: one with both 4242 subscriptions (preview 42.50 = 31.50 + 11.00, reminder sent, Notified) and one for the other-card subscription alone (10.00). At cutoff the grouped renewal placed ONE order with both lines and one authorization; both subscriptions advanced (+1 month, +1 week, ordersCount 2). Source: RenewalProcessor.GetGroupKey = store, customer, nextOrderDate day, stored card, payment method, shipping method + option, currency, address. Changing the card of a subscription does not detach it from an open renewal; changing date or quantity or frequency does (the renewal is cancelled when it loses its last item).

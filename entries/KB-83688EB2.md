Signed in as a sales rep serving one organization (store B2B-store, Firefox). On /company/my-customers/<org GUID> the Sales statistics block (period tabs 6 months / 12 months / Current year / Q1-Q4) does not write the period to the URL. Choosing Q3 on the profile then clicking Open in Reporting opened /company/customer-sales?customer=<org GUID>&period=q3 with Q3 pressed and the customer preselected; choosing Q1 there (URL replaced to period=q1) and going Back showed the profile still on Q3, so the Reporting period does not overwrite the profile's. Opening the profile with ?period=q1 still showed the stored Q3 (the parameter is ignored; the link kept period=q3). With two tabs on the same profile, choosing Q2 in one switched the other tab to Q2 without reload, including its Open in Reporting href. Last year switches are shared both ways: Monthly sales Last year turned on on Reporting was on in the profile block, AOV Last year turned on in the profile was on on Reporting, Monthly sales turned off on Reporting was off in the profile. Choosing All my customers on Reporting and then opening the profile sent a fresh SalesRepMonthlyOrderStatistics with organizationId = the customer (no cache reuse across scopes). The Sales Rep Dashboard /company/dashboard has no Last year switch.

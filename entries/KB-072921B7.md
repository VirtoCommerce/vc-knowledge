Signed-in B2B-store org user, theme vc-frontend-next 3.3.0, Chromium 1920. On /cart with a shipping address, delivery Fixed Rate (Ground) and payment 'Bank card (CyberSource)', a 'Payment card' section renders exactly four field labels (div.vc-label, not <label>): 'Card number*' above the CyberSource microform card-number iframe, 'Cardholder name*' (textbox), 'Expiration date*' (textbox, placeholder MM / YYYY) and 'Security code*' above the microform security-code iframe. No label reads 'CVV'; the iframe inputs carry their own accessible names 'Card number' and 'Card security code'. Clicking the 'Card number*' and 'Security code*' labels raised no console error. Payment options listed: Bank card (Authorize.Net), Bank card (CyberSource), Manual, Account Balance Payment, Bank card (Skyflow), Invoice / Purchase order (net 30). Observed in two runs the same day.

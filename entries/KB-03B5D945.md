Signed in as a plain Sales Representative at 1920 px. Account menu and rail section "Sales Rep hub": Dashboard, My customers with badge 5, Tasks (no Calendar entry, no Document library). /company/my-customers listed 5 organization rows (badge = row count). /company/tasks renders heading Tasks, scope chips Today/All and status tabs Upcoming/Overdue/Completed with counts, a day table, and a Due dates month calendar; clicking a marked day (2 tasks) filtered the table to that day with a removable date chip. A full-load deep link to /company/tasks rendered the same page with the URL unchanged and no console error or [MF] line. Every hub page chunk (dashboard, my-customers, customer-profile, sales-rep-tasks-page) was served from /modules/$(VirtoCommerce.SalesRep)/plugins/vc-frontend/assets/, none from the host /assets. The 390 px mobile menu shows the same three entries under a Sales Rep hub sub-menu.

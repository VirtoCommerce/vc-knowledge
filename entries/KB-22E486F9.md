Return 3.1005.0-pr-28-fb4f, 2026-10-09. Two returns submitted and then cancelled on the previous build (their notification journal holds ReturnRegisteredEmailNotification and ReturnCancelledEmailNotification to the buyer AND to the organization's email, proof they had been submitted) now have submittedDate null after the migration (which backfilled CreatedDate for every status except Draft and Cancelled/Canceled). A holder's return(id) answers null and organizationReturns omits them, while a return submitted and cancelled AFTER the deploy keeps its date and stays listed and readable. Same history, different visibility depending on the deploy date; the history needed to tell them apart (Registered notification, or the submit) was available at migration time. Described as by design by the developer.

Platform 3.1076.1, 2026-10-12. With a password-grant token for the built-in admin account, GET /api/platform/security/currentuser returned 200 with id, userName and isAdministrator=true, but roles=[] and permissions=[]. Admin rights come from the isAdministrator flag, not from an assigned Administrator role, so an assertion that the roles array is non-empty does not hold for this account.

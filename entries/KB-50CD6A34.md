The storefront promotionCoupons query searches with only-active and public-only criteria, so a coupon is listed only if its promotion is active, public and not past its endDate; a coupon missing from /account/coupons is usually one of those three. The id on a promotionCoupons item is the promotion's id, not the coupon's; coupon entity ids come from the admin coupons search. A 'private coupon shown publicly' is typically a separate public promotion carrying the same code, not a filter leak.

Stable 16 (Customer 3.1029.0). Created two AGENT-TEST members (a contact and an organization). DELETE /api/members?ids=<a>,<b> answered 204, but GET /api/members?ids=<a>&ids=<b> still returned both. DELETE /api/members?ids=<a>&ids=<b> answered 204 and removed both. A success status therefore does not prove the delete; cleanup must repeat ids= (same binding applies to DELETE /api/platform/dynamic/properties?propertyIds=... which worked with repeated propertyIds).

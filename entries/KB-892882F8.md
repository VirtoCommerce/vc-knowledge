Return 3.1005.0-pr-28-fb4f, Platform 3.1078.0, 2026-10-12. Three buyer drafts created with xAPI createReturn and cancelled with cancelReturn without submit (stored submittedDate null, invisible to organization holders through Query.organizationReturns and Query.return). Signed in as administrator, /workspace/Return keyword search by their RMA prefix listed all three with Return status Cancelled, next to a submitted-then-cancelled return; clicking a row opened the return blade (GET /api/return/{id} 200, GET /api/return/{id}/available-statuses 200 answering [Cancelled]) showing Status Cancelled. POST /api/return/search by objectIds returned them with submittedDate null. The SubmittedDate gate applies only to the xAPI organization reads; the back office still sees every return.

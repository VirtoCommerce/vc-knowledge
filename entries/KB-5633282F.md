On /bulk-order the "Bulk order pad" offers two modes: Copy&Paste (lines of SKU,Quantity) and Manually (rows with SKU and Quantity inputs, "Add 5 more rows", Reset, "Add to cart"). Entering a SKU does not load product name, price or availability on the row; typing a partial SKU shows no autocomplete dropdown; there is no file-upload control. The submit button is labelled "Add to cart" (not "Add All to Cart"); submitting 3 rows added all 3 lines with the entered quantities and redirected to /cart without a toast.

Seven attempts on one build (fresh article and re-opened article; Title edited via fill and via keystrokes; with and without Title (in list) set via 'copy from title'; with null and non-empty Content; after focusing and typing into the Content editor): after clicking Reset the Title returned to the last-saved value, the 'Has unsaved changes' banner disappeared, Save and Reset went disabled, and the Network log showed no PUT /api/news between the edit and the next explicit Save. Closing the blade right after Reset closed it with no 'Close anyway' confirmation. An earlier regression run reported the banner persisting after Reset; that was not reproduced.

On Return 3.1005.0-pr-28-62f9 a holder of xapi:my_organization:return:view gets a colleague's non-draft return from return(id) with customerName and organizationName, and availableActions edit/submit/cancel all isAvailable=false with unavailableReason RETURN_NOT_FOUND. cancelReturn, updateReturn and submitReturn on the colleague's returns answer RETURN_NOT_FOUND and change nothing; createReturn on the colleague's order answers ORDER_NOT_FOUND. A colleague's draft stays null. The holder's own returns keep their actions.

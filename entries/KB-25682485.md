A colleague's draft stays null. The holder's own returns keep their actions. The read rule holds up to Return 3.1005.0-pr-28-62f9. From commit d9233ee1 (VCST-6226; unreleased PR VirtoCommerce/vc-module-return#28) ReturnAccessService.CanViewReturnAsync requires SubmittedDate != null: on pr-28-fb4f (vcst, 2026-10-12, own fixtures, two organizations, membership-role and organization-role holders) return(id) of a colleague's submitted (Requested) or submitted-then-cancelled return comes back read-only with edit/submit/cancel isAvailable=false RETURN_NOT_FOUND, while a colleague's draft cancelled without submit and a live draft are null. cancelReturn on the colleague's Requested return and on a draft answers RETURN_NOT_FOUND with nothing changed; createReturn on the colleague's order answers ORDER_NOT_FOUND.

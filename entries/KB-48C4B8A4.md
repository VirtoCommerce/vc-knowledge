On vcst_qa 2026-10-28 (platform 3.1090.0, storefront theme 2.61.0-alpha.2605), an org maintainer holding platform:security:loginOnBehalf, signed in with password, opened /account/impersonate/{userId} for a seeded Locked customer. POST /connect/token (grant_type=impersonate) answered HTTP 200 (not 400), the storefront redirected to /blocked ("Your account is blocked") and the header then showed 'Sign in' i.e. the operator's own session was gone too; the operator had to sign in again. No impersonation banner appeared. The same operator impersonating a seeded Invited (email unconfirmed) customer succeeded silently (200, banner shows the invited user). This matches the older 2026-05-14 contract recorded in suite 082 IMP-049 and contradicts the case's 2026-07-15 re-lock (server-side 400, graceful error, operator preserved).

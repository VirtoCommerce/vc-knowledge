Signed-in personal customer on B2B-store whose only USD shopping cart was 'default' (plus a SavedForLater cart and a EUR cart). On /cart, one simple product line (unit 59.99) was stepped +, -, +, -, +, - with the line stepper about 10-20 s apart. Each click sent ChangeFullCartItemsQuantity (no cartId; storeId, currencyCode, userId only), answered 200 with errors[] empty, server time 0.23-1.02 s, returning the same cart with the correct totals. DOM reads about 2 s after each click already showed matching line total, line-group Subtotal, summary Subtotal and Total (59.99/71.99 or 119.98/143.98) and the values held on every later read up to 10 s; no revert to the old amount. So multi-second stale totals are not expected on a one-cart account; when seen, check the account for competing carts of the same store and currency first (KB-2EDC661F). Not tested here: typed quantity input or firefox (KB-FC70723D).

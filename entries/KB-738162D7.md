On Loyalty 3.1009.0, POST /api/loyalty-missions with periodicity "Monthly" returned 200 and the body kept periodicity "Monthly"; "None" also returned 200. GET /api/loyalty-missions/new defaults periodicity to None. The 3.1009.0 validator has no periodicity rule. Whether a non-None value is honoured by accrual was not checked here.

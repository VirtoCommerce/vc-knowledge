As a signed-in buyer on B2B-store with an empty cart, the PDP of a product with a PERCENT 10 plan showed Price $35.00, One-time $35.00 and Subscribe & Save $31.50. I added 1 unit and ticked Subscribe & Save (setCartItemSubscription, monthly), then reloaded the PDP. GetProduct returned price.actual 31.50 (list 35.00, discountAmount 3.50), and GetSubscriptionOffers returned price 31.50, discountAmount 3.15, subscriptionPrice 28.35. The page then showed Price $35.00 crossed out to $31.50, One-time $31.50 and Subscribe & Save $28.35 crossed out from $31.50. The /cart line itself was priced right ($31.50, one discount of $3.50). Source: xCart's LoadCartToEvalContextMiddleware runs the cart-map promotion pipeline for every PromotionEvaluationContext, including catalog price evaluation. The product-subscription SubscriptionPromoEntriesMiddleware then tags the catalog promo entry by product id, so the catalog price gets the subscription reward. The offers handler then subtracts the discount from that ActualPrice again.

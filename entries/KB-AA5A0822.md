Created two products through POST /api/catalog/products, one with measureUnit "box" and one with none, then added each with xAPI addItem as a B2B buyer in USD and in EUR carts. The lineItem measureUnit came back "box" for the first and null for the second, in both currencies and in two stores. xAPI product(id, storeId) also returns measureUnit "box" / null. No default unit such as EA is filled in at the cart layer.

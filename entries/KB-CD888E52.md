Fresh non-admin Manager accounts (no contact; one with storeId null, one with storeId=B2B-store) each got 200 from POST /connect/token grant_type=password both with the form field storeId=B2B-store and without any storeId. A Customer B2B buyer on B2B-store got 200 only with storeId and 400 invalid_grant user_cannot_login_in_store without it. So the store rule binds Customer accounts, not Managers.

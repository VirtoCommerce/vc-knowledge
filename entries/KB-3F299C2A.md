On a fresh personal contact, Mutation.updateMemberAddresses(command:{memberId, addresses:[a1,a2,a3]}) with three addresses that differ in line1 and postalCode (no key, no name) returned and stored ONE address, the last (currentCustomerAddresses totalCount 1). A batch of 2 gave totalCount 1 as well, also with differing addressType or name. The same three addresses with a distinct `key` each gave totalCount 3. Single-address calls append (1, 2, 3, ...), and an identical copy of an existing address is skipped. So a seeder or client that sends several keyless addresses in one call silently loses all but the last; the storefront itself sends one address per call.

On vcst B2B-store, currency XPT (DecimalDigits 0, Rounding001, AwayFromZero), a price list assigned to the store with list 10.50 and sale 7.20 (no promotion) returns via Query.product: list 11, sale 7, discountAmount 3, actual 8, saleWithTax 9, actualWithTax 9. So actual (without tax) is a whole unit above sale. Cause: DiscountAmount is built from (ListPrice - SalePrice).Amount, which is rounded by the currency's rounding policy (DecimalDigits, RoundingType, MidpointRounding); ActualPrice = ListPrice - DiscountAmount keeps the unrounded remainder (10.50 - 3 = 7.50, which rounds to 8). Therefore the defect is NOT limited to list prices with more than 2 decimals: any currency whose rounding step is coarser than the price precision (DecimalDigits 0, or cash rounding Rounding005/01/05/1) shows it, with or without tax, and the error is up to the rounding step. A port of DefaultMoneyRoundingPolicy into a simulation gives the same result for every RoundingType.

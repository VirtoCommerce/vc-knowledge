Established through the cache itself, with a TTL-only statistics key (SalesRep 3.1013.0-pr-18, CartInvalidateOnChange=false, TTL 60): a key warmed with ONE read and then made stale by a cart change read back 11 stale / 9 fresh over 20 cookie-less reads, so at least two replicas each hold their own in-memory copy. A staircase census (a quantity change between 13 single reads, so each replica freezes the value it first computed) then returned exactly 2 distinct frozen values over 30 cookie-less reads (20/10 split), consistent with two replicas. Every cookie-less response sets INGRESSCOOKIE (value '<epoch>.<ms>.<pid>.<random>|<hash>', the suffix after '|' constant across /graphql, /graphql/sales-rep and REST /api responses = one backend). Re-sending a response's INGRESSCOOKIE pinned the request to the replica that produced it: 10/10 re-sends of the warming cookie returned that replica's stale value, and 6/6 re-sends per pod for each of the two pods returned that pod's frozen value; the pinning held for mutations on /graphql and admin REST too. Cookie-less requests are spread roughly evenly across the two pods.

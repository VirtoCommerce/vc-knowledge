Signed in on vcst as a contact in exactly 2 organizations and opened the account menu on /account/dashboard: the Organizations listbox listed both orgs (active first) with a static label and no search textbox. In vc-frontend source (useUserOrganizations.ts) isShowSearch = totalOrganizations > SEARCH_THRESHOLD with SEARCH_THRESHOLD = 10, so the search box is by design only for users with 11+ organizations. Clicking the other option switched the active organization (header showed the new org). A test asserting the search box needs a persona with more than 10 organizations.

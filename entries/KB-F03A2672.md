On Loyalty 3.1009.0, a full GET-merge-PUT of a Published mission with only the name changed returned 500 {"message":"Mission '<id>' is 'Published' and cannot be modified. Only the transition Published -> Archived is allowed.","stackTrace":null}. A PUT of the unchanged GET body also returned 500. A read-back showed nothing persisted. Controls: the same PUT on a Draft returned 200, and PUT with status Archived on the Published mission returned 200. Source 3.1009.0: LoyaltyMissionService.EnsureMutableAsync throws InvalidOperationException, which the controller does not map to a 4xx.

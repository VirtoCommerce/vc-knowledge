Theme 3.1.0-pr-11-d67b-d67b0e0a, Draft quote edit page, a line quantity typed as 0 then blurred: the spinbutton 'Product quantity' (id input-<n>) is exposed as invalid and matches the selector [aria-invalid="true"][aria-describedby="input-<n>-details"]; the element #input-<n>-details is the one rendering 'Value must be a positive number' under the stepper, and the input is resolvable by role with that accessible description. At a valid quantity (e.g. 6, above stock 5) the input no longer carries aria-invalid="true" and the -details element is not rendered. At 1920px the message text is visually ellipsized ('Value must be a positive...') in the narrow quantity column, while the full text remains the accessible description.

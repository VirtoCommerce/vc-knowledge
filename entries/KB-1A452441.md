Signed in as a B2B buyer with two passkeys on /account/security (Edge, accessibility snapshot), each key row shows the key name and details and two icon-only buttons; both rows' buttons are named exactly "Edit" and "Delete". Renaming one key to a distinct name does not change them. A locator by role+name cannot single out a row's button (needs nth). The page template sets a fixed generic aria-label per button (theme 2.61.0-pr-2601). Rename/Delete dialogs themselves are labelled.

On /products-with-options/configurable-caps-shirts/vintage-california-beach-pullover-hoodie the variant groups (Color, Size, Size chart, Fabric) render as role=radiogroup 'Variant options' containing plain <button> elements (data-test-id variant-picker--Color--Black). After hover and click, a locator for [aria-checked=true], [aria-selected=true] or [aria-pressed=true] on the option matches nothing and the accessibility snapshot lists only 'button Black'. This product offers a single value per group, so an unselected sibling could not be compared.

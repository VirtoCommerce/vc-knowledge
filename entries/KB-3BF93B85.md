Anonymous, B2B-store, Firefox, desktop, on /printers, where the header search bar shows the chip 'Remove: Printers' before the 'Search within Printers' input. Keyboard path: focus the input, press Shift+Tab (focus lands on the chip button), press Enter. The chip unmounts and document.activeElement === document.body. The next Tab lands on the search input, because Firefox keeps the sequential-focus starting point where the removed chip was. Mouse path: click the chip. activeElement === body again, and the next Tab lands on the first 'Skip to main content' skip link, so focus restarts from the top of the document. Both paths behaved the same on a theme 3.0.0-alpha.2685 build and on a 2.60.0 PR build. Source: search-bar.vue onScopeItemClick only calls useSearchScore.removeScopeItemById, which filters the reactive scope array, and nothing moves focus afterwards. WCAG 2.4.3 focus-order gap. Chromium was not checked.
